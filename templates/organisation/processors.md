# Data processor agreements

The agreements the organisation has with those who process personal data on its behalf. Write one section per agreement, with the name of the processor as its heading. Say in prose which system or service the agreement covers, what personal data the processor handles, when the agreement was signed, where it is kept and who in the organisation is responsible for it.

## Example: Example Payroll AS

This section is an example, not a fact about the organisation. Replace it with a real section or delete it.

The organisation has a data processor agreement with Example Payroll AS for the payroll system. Example Payroll AS handles the names, national identity numbers, bank account numbers and salaries of all employees. The agreement was signed on 1 March 2024 and is kept in the contract archive. The head of finance is responsible for it.
