# Roles

The roles in the organisation and the people who hold them. Write one section per role, with the name of the role as its heading. Say in prose who holds the role, what the role is responsible for and who steps in when that person is away.

## Example: Data protection officer

This section is an example, not a fact about the organisation. Replace it with a real section or delete it.

Kari Nordmann is the data protection officer. She advises the organisation on personal data and is the contact for the data protection authority. Ola Hansen steps in when she is away.
