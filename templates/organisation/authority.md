# Authority

Who has the authority to decide what in the organisation. Write one section per kind of decision, with the decision as its heading. Say in prose which role decides, which roles must be asked or told, and who decides when that role is away.

## Example: Notify the data protection authority of a breach

This section is an example, not a fact about the organisation. Replace it with a real section or delete it.

The managing director decides whether a personal data breach is notified to the data protection authority, after advice from the data protection officer. The chair of the board is told. The head of finance decides when the managing director is away.
