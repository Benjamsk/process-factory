# Policies

The policies and routines the organisation already has. Write one section per policy, with the name of the policy as its heading. Say in prose what the policy covers, who owns it, when it was last approved and where it is kept.

## Example: Information security policy

This section is an example, not a fact about the organisation. Replace it with a real section or delete it.

The information security policy says how employees handle passwords, devices and personal data. The head of IT owns it. The managing director approved it on 15 January 2025, and it is kept in the staff handbook.
