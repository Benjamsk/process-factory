# Case 9: The employee boards a flight right after reporting the loss

Kind: Conflicting rules

## Situation

Tuesday 24 November 2026, 11:00. Support adviser Maja Eriksen telephones head of IT Marius Tangen from the gate at Oslo airport to say that her company laptop is gone from her hand luggage and that she last had it at the security check about forty minutes earlier. The report reaches the company at 11:00. She says she is boarding at once and cannot be reached for about three hours. The laptop does not answer in device management and cannot be located.

## Who acts

Head of IT, Marius Tangen.

## What the person knows

He knows the laptop cannot be located, which on its own is a ground for a wipe, and that it held personal data in the office suite and the support desk, so Kari Nordmann must be asked before the wipe; she works on Tuesdays. He knows the employee must be told before the wipe, where she can be reached, and asked what exists only on the device, and that the wipe does not wait more than an hour because she cannot be reached. He does not know what existed only on the laptop, and only she can tell him; she cannot be reached until about 14:00.

## What should happen

Two rules pull against each other here and the specification settles this one: the employee is told before the wipe and asked what exists only on the device, but the wipe is not held back more than an hour because she cannot be reached, so after an hour it goes ahead without her answer. What was lost is then not known from her, and the record says so.

Every time counts from 11:00.

- By 12:00 the device is located and locked as far as device management allows, and that it could not be located goes into the record.
- By 13:00 the sessions of Maja Eriksen are ended, her password changed, her access tokens revoked in the office suite, Nordsky Cloud and the support desk, and the device blocked, and Nora Lind is told.
- Kari Nordmann is told about the lost device when the facts are started.
- By 15:00 the six facts are established and written into the record. What only Maja Eriksen can give beyond what she said at 11:00 is written as `not established` with the reason that she could not be reached.
- By 15:00 Marius Tangen decides whether the device is wiped, having asked Kari Nordmann first. The device cannot be located, so it is wiped. The attempt to reach Maja Eriksen, what she was asked and that she could not be reached go into the record, and the wipe is held back for that at most 1 hour.
- Work that existed only on the device is lost, and the record says what it was, or that it is not known because she could not be reached.
- By 15:00 Kari Nordmann decides whether the loss is a personal data breach, and her decision, her name, her reason and the time go into the record whether the answer is yes or no.
- Where the facts show the laptop was taken rather than mislaid, she reports the theft to the police with Marius Tangen's help within 24 hours of 11:00 and the case number goes into the record. Where the laptop was worth more than 10,000 kroner, Tomas Berg tells the insurer within 3 working days. Within 2 working days of the loss she has a replacement laptop and her access back, and Nora Lind is told.

## Pass

The record shows that Maja Eriksen was asked before the wipe where she could be reached and what existed only on the device, with the time of the attempt, and that the wipe was held back for no more than one hour on her account and then done, with Kari Nordmann asked beforehand. It shows the lock attempt by 12:00, the account measures and the block by 13:00 with Nora Lind told, the six facts by 15:00 with `not established` and the reason for what she could not give, a wipe decision by 15:00 naming who decided, a line saying what was lost or that it is not known because she could not be reached, and Kari Nordmann's decision whether the loss was a personal data breach with her name, her reason and the time by 15:00.
