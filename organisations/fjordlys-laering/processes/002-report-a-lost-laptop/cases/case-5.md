# Case 5: The head of IT is on holiday with his telephone off

Kind: Person away

## Situation

Tuesday 24 November 2026, 14:00. Support adviser Jostein Vold is working in a cafe when his work phone is taken from the table. Head of IT Marius Tangen is on a week of holiday abroad with his telephone off. Jostein Vold telephones him at 14:05 and gets no answer, and at 14:10 he reaches lead developer Amina Farah, who steps in for the head of IT. The report reaches the company at 14:10. The equipment register shows the work phone was bought for 8,900 kroner.

## Who acts

Lead developer, Amina Farah.

## What the person knows

She knows she steps in for the head of IT on everything he holds in this process, including the decision to lock or wipe the device and to block Jostein Vold's accounts and access, and that she administers device management while he is away. She knows the phone was taken and not mislaid, and where and when. Device management gives her the model and serial number, the encryption, the screen lock and the last contact. She does not know whether anyone else could have seen the screen or the password, or what was stored on the phone that exists nowhere else, until Jostein Vold tells her. She knows the trigger tells an employee to telephone Marius Tangen in working hours, and that the specification does not say whom the employee telephones in working hours when he cannot be reached; it says only that she steps in on everything the head of IT holds.

## What should happen

Every time counts from 14:10, when the report reached the company.

- By 15:10 Amina Farah locates and locks the phone in device management.
- By 16:10 she ends Jostein Vold's sessions, changes his password and revokes his access tokens in the office suite, Nordsky Cloud and the support desk, blocks the device in device management, and tells Nora Lind.
- She tells Kari Nordmann about the lost device when she starts establishing the facts. Kari Nordmann works on Tuesdays.
- By 18:10 the six facts are established and written into the record, with `not established` and the reason where one cannot be established.
- By 18:10 she decides whether the device is wiped. The phone was taken rather than mislaid, which is one of the grounds, so it is wiped, and she asks Kari Nordmann first because the phone held personal data. Before the wipe Jostein Vold is told where he can be reached and asked what exists only on the device, and that goes into the record.
- By 18:10 Kari Nordmann decides whether the loss is a personal data breach, and her decision, her name, her reason and the time go into the record whether the answer is yes or no.
- Within 24 hours of 14:10 Jostein Vold reports the theft to the police with the help of the head of IT, which Amina Farah gives while Marius Tangen is away, and the police case number goes into the record.
- The phone was worth 8,900 kroner, which is not more than 10,000 kroner, so the insurer is not told.
- Within 2 working days of the loss Jostein Vold has a replacement device and his access back, and Nora Lind is told. The replacement goes into the equipment register and the lost phone is marked there as lost, with the date.

## Pass

The record shows every measure and decision taken by Amina Farah, named as the one who took it, with the lock by 15:10, the sessions, the password, the tokens and the block by 16:10 with Nora Lind told, the six facts and the wipe decision by 18:10 with Kari Nordmann asked before the wipe, Kari Nordmann's breach decision with her name, her reason and the time by 18:10, a police case number within 24 hours, and the replacement device and the access within 2 working days with Nora Lind told. No step waits for Marius Tangen to come back, and the record holds no entry saying the insurer was told. The record also says plainly that the specification does not say whom an employee telephones in working hours when the head of IT cannot be reached.
