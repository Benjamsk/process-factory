# Case 2: The laptop is found in a school staff room

Kind: Ordinary path

## Situation

Wednesday 25 November 2026. Support lead Petter Dahl leaves a meeting at a school at 13:00 and cannot find his company laptop. He telephones head of IT Marius Tangen at 13:20, and the report reaches the company at 13:20. At 15:20 the school telephones to say the laptop is in the staff room and was in a locked cupboard from 13:00.

## Who acts

Head of IT, Marius Tangen.

## What the person knows

He knows the report reached the company at 13:20 and what Petter Dahl told him: that the laptop was mislaid at the school. Device management gives him the model and serial number, the encryption, the screen lock and the last contact, and the device answers. From 15:20 he knows the device was found, when and where, and what the school says about who could have reached it in the meantime. He does not know by himself whether the device is safe to use again; that is his to decide and to say.

## What should happen

Every time counts from 13:20, and the device being found again does not end the process by itself.

- By 14:20 the device is located and locked in device management.
- By 15:20 the sessions are ended, the password changed, the tokens revoked in the office suite, Nordsky Cloud and the support desk, and the device blocked, and Nora Lind is told.
- Kari Nordmann is told about the lost device when he starts establishing the facts. She works on Wednesdays.
- By 17:20 the six facts are established and written into the record, with `not established` and the reason where one cannot be established.
- By 17:20 he decides whether the device is wiped, and asks Kari Nordmann first because the laptop held personal data in the office suite and the support desk.
- By 17:20 Kari Nordmann decides whether the loss is a personal data breach, and her decision, her name, her reason and the time go into the record whether the answer is yes or no.
- The case is closed with a line in the record saying that the device was found, when, where and whether anyone else could have used it in the meantime. Nothing already done is undone until Marius Tangen says the device is safe to use again.
- Within 2 working days of the loss Petter Dahl has a laptop and his access back, and Nora Lind is told.
- The equipment register is kept up to date for the device.
- The process ends when he is working again, the record is complete and Kari Nordmann has said whether the loss was a personal data breach.

## Pass

The record shows the lock by 14:20, the sessions ended, the password changed, the tokens revoked and the device blocked by 15:20 with Nora Lind told, the six facts by 17:20, a wipe decision by 17:20 with Kari Nordmann asked before any wipe, and her breach decision with her name, her reason and the time. It holds a closing line saying the device was found at 15:20, where, and whether anyone else could have used it. The block and the revocations are still in place at 15:20 and are undone only after Marius Tangen has said in the record that the device is safe to use again, and Petter Dahl has a laptop and his access back within 2 working days with Nora Lind told.
