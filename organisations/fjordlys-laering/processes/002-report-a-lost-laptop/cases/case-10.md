# Case 10: A memory stick with support case exports is missing

Kind: Missing knowledge

## Situation

Wednesday 25 November 2026, 09:30. Support adviser Sondre Lie tells head of IT Marius Tangen by telephone that the memory stick he kept in his desk drawer is not there. It holds exported lists of support cases. He cannot say when he last saw it; it could have been any time in the past week, and he does not know what the last export on it held. The report reaches the company at 09:30. The memory stick is not in device management and not in the equipment register.

## Who acts

Head of IT, Marius Tangen.

## What the person knows

He knows the scope covers any memory stick or external disk with company data on it, so the process runs. He knows the memory stick cannot be located, locked, blocked or wiped, because device management does not hold it and nothing can reach it. He does not know its model or serial number, whether anything on it was encrypted, where and when it went missing, whether it was taken or mislaid, who else could have seen what was on it, or what it held that exists nowhere else, and Sondre Lie cannot tell him. He knows the sign-in logs of the office suite and the Nordsky Cloud access logs will not answer for it either, because a memory stick signs in to no system.

## What should happen

The specification says the six facts are established and all six written into the record, with `not established` and the reason where something cannot be established rather than a blank, and that is what carries this case. It says the measures on the device are done in device management, and it does not say what takes their place for a device that device management does not hold; that gap is not filled here.

Every time counts from 09:30.

- By 11:30 his sessions are ended, his password changed and his access tokens revoked in the office suite, Nordsky Cloud and the support desk, and Nora Lind is told.
- Kari Nordmann is told about the lost device when the facts are started. She works on Wednesdays.
- By 13:30 the six facts stand in the record, each either established or marked `not established` with its reason: the four from device management because the memory stick is not a managed device, the ones from the sign-in logs because it signs in to no system, and what Sondre Lie cannot say with the reason that he cannot say it.
- By 13:30 Kari Nordmann decides from the established facts whether the loss is a personal data breach, and her decision, her name, her reason and the time go into the record whether the answer is yes or no.
- The record says who reported the loss and when he noticed it, the time it reached the company, that the exported lists of support cases were on the memory stick as far as he can say, and what he says existed only on it.
- As far as anyone knows the memory stick was mislaid rather than taken, so there is no report to the police; where the facts later show it was taken, the theft is reported with Marius Tangen's help within 24 hours of 09:30 and the case number goes into the record.
- Within 2 working days of the loss Sondre Lie has his access back, and Nora Lind is told.

## Pass

The record shows all six facts, with every fact that could not be had written as `not established` together with its reason and no blanks, the sessions ended, the password changed and the tokens revoked by 11:30 with Nora Lind told, and the decision of Kari Nordmann with her name, her reason and the time by 13:30 whether the answer was yes or no. The record states plainly that the memory stick could not be located, locked, blocked or wiped because device management does not hold it and that the specification does not say what replaces those steps. Sondre Lie has his access back within 2 working days.
