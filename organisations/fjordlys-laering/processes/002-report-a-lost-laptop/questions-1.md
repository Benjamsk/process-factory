# Questions 1

## Questions

1. What exactly starts the process: a laptop the employee is sure was lost or stolen, or also one that is only missing for now, for example left behind on a train and perhaps recoverable?
2. Does the process cover only company laptops, or also work phones, tablets, USB drives and a private device the employee uses for work?
3. What must be done at once when a loss is reported, and in which order: locking or wiping the laptop remotely, changing the employee's password, ending their sessions and revoking their access tokens, blocking the device in the systems, something else?
4. What must be established about the lost laptop before those measures are decided, and how: what was stored on it locally, whether it was encrypted, whether it was logged in to the reading app or Nordsky Cloud, and where and when it went missing?
5. How quickly must each step happen after the loss is reported, and are those times counted in working hours or around the clock, including evenings and weekends?
6. How is a loss reported and acted on when the employee notices it outside working hours, in the evening or at the weekend, given that the duty is to report a security incident the same day?
7. On what basis is a laptop wiped or locked remotely, and is the employee asked first, given that wiping destroys work that exists only on the device?
8. At which point in this process is it decided whether the loss is a personal data breach, and does the breach process then take over, run alongside this one, or is this process only its first step?
9. Does the process also cover getting the employee working again, with a replacement laptop and their access restored, or does it end once the security measures are done?
10. What is recorded about each lost laptop, including the losses that turn out to be harmless, where is that record kept and for how long?

## Missing from the organisation context

These are not questions to answer here. Give them in `/process org`.

- Whether the company has a device management system that can locate, lock or wipe a company laptop remotely, whether company laptops are encrypted, and who administers it: belongs in `organisation/systems.md`.
- Whether there is a register of which laptop each of the 48 employees has, with serial numbers, and who keeps it: belongs in `organisation/systems.md`.
- Who decides to wipe or lock a lost laptop remotely and to block the employee's accounts and access tokens, and who decides when that role is away: belongs in `organisation/authority.md`.
- Whether the information security policy says anything about lost or stolen devices beyond the duty to report a security incident to the head of IT the same day, for example that laptops must be encrypted and screen-locked: belongs in `organisation/policies.md`.
- Whether the company has a routine for reporting lost equipment to the police and to its insurer, and who owns it: belongs in `organisation/policies.md`.
