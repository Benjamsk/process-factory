# gdpr review of draft-12.md

Verdict: CHANGES

- [medium] Steps 16 and 17, and the tellings of Nora Lind in steps 7 and 38: these steps handle personal data of the person who had the device for which the `Legal basis` section of `spec.md` gives no basis under Article 6 - the private email address or postal address the company holds in the employment file, when the person left, what they handed back and what they said about it then, and, to Nora Lind, that this named person's accounts and access were blocked and for how long they could not work; the Article 6(1)(f) basis there is given only for "the device's own entries in device management and the entries of that one account in the sign-in logs of the office suite and the access logs of Nordsky Cloud" in the window from when the device went missing until its access was blocked, and the Article 6(1)(c) basis only from the moment the loss is decided to be a personal data breach. The basis for these steps is missing from `spec.md` as well, so the draft writer cannot state it and must report the finding.
- [medium] `When to hand over`, the branch where the report ends with Ingrid Solheim or Tomas Berg: the one who takes the report writes the reporter's name, how they can be reached and what they said into the record, but nobody is named to give that reporter the telling of step 3 within 1 hour of the report, although step 3 names Amina Farah and on-call developer Erik Moen for the case where they answered the report; Articles 13 and 14 require that the person is told what is recorded about them and why when the data is obtained. The draft must say that whoever takes the report in that branch gives the reporter that telling. `spec.md` does not assign it in that branch either.
- [low] `What to record`: the list does not hold a question a reporter or the person who had the device puts to data protection officer Kari Nordmann about their own data, as steps 3 and 13 invite them to do, with the answer given and its date; Article 5(2) asks that such a request is recorded, and the nearest item in the list, "every question this document did not cover and the answer that was given", is about situations the document does not cover rather than about a person's request about their own data. `spec.md` does not list it either.
