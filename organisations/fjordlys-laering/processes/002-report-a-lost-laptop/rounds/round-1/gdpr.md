# gdpr review of draft-1.md

Verdict: CHANGES

- [medium] Steps 2, 3, 4, 6 and 7 (locate and lock, end the sessions and revoke the tokens, block the device, the six facts, the wipe): these steps process personal data about the employee who lost the device, among it when and from where the device last contacted the company and, from the sign-in logs of the office suite and the Nordsky Cloud access logs, which systems their account was signed in to and whether anything was reached after the loss, but no basis of Article 6 covers them: the section `Legal basis` of `spec.md` names only the routine for lost or stolen equipment, the information security policy, the company's own rules on who may decide and the duties of `Handle a personal data breach`, and none of these is one of the six bases of Article 6 (consent, contract, legal obligation, vital interests, public interest, legitimate interests); the basis is missing from `spec.md` as well, so the draft writer cannot state it and must tell the user.
- [medium] Steps 2 and 6: the draft never says that the employee whose device is lost is told that their location data in device management and the log entries of their account in the office suite and in Nordsky Cloud are read about them, and why, which Article 13 requires where personal data is collected about a person; step 8 tells the employee only before the wipe, and about what existed only on the device, which is something else. The draft must say that the employee is told what is collected about them and why; this is not in `spec.md` either, so the draft writer must tell the user.
- [medium] Steps 2, 4, 6 and 7, the system device management: Article 28 — `organisation/systems.md` says device management holds personal data about the 48 employees (the employee each device is given to, when and from where the device last contacted the company, whether its disk is encrypted and its screen locked) and that Microsoft supplies it as part of Microsoft 365, but the only section for Microsoft in `organisation/processors.md` is the agreement for the office suite, and it names only the email and documents of the 48 employees, not device management and not that device data; the agreement that covers device management is missing from the organisation context, not from the draft.
- [medium] `What to record`, the paragraph on the equipment register: Article 5 — the entry the process writes there holds personal data (which employee had which lost device and which replacement, and the date it was marked lost), but the draft gives a retention time only for the case record, 3 years and 5 years, and none for the equipment register. The draft must say how long that entry is kept; `spec.md` gives no time for it either, so the draft writer must tell the user.
