- [medium] Steps 15 and 16, and the fifth bullet of step 13: the draft has head of people Nora Lind read the employment file of the person who had the device, both their private email address and postal address and what the file says about them (when they left, what they handed back and what they said about it then), and send the telling to that private address, but no lawful basis of Article 6 covers this processing. The basis for reading and using the employment file is missing from `spec.md` as well, so the draft writer cannot state it from the specification and must tell the user.

Step 13 tells them about the reading of device management and the logs, about what Nora Lind gives from the employment file and about the entry in the equipment register, but not that the record holds who reported the loss and what they said about them, or why. Step 13 of `spec.md` lists the same tellings and does not name this one either, so the draft writer cannot take it from the specification and must tell the user.
