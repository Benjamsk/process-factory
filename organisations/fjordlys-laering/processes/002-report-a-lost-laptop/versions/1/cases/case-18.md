# Case 18: Pupil data is downloaded from the laptop after it went missing

Kind: Edge of scope

## Situation

Wednesday 25 November 2026. The company laptop of lead developer Amina Farah is taken from her bicycle bag outside the office at about 13:30. She telephones head of IT Marius Tangen at 14:00, and the report reaches the company at 14:00. Device management shows that the screen was not locked when the laptop last contacted the company, and the access logs of Nordsky Cloud show that a pupil data export was downloaded with her account at 13:50, after the laptop went missing. Marius Tangen tells Kari Nordmann about the lost laptop when he starts establishing the facts, and the six facts stand in the record by 18:00. Kari Nordmann works Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.

## Who acts

Data protection officer, Kari Nordmann.

## What the person knows

She knows the six established facts, among them that the screen was not locked, that the laptop was signed in to Nordsky Cloud and the office suite, and that a pupil data export was downloaded with Amina Farah's account at 13:50, after the laptop went missing. She knows the data of about 90,000 pupils lies in Nordsky Cloud. She knows the loss reached the company at 14:00 and that this is the moment the company became aware. She knows the processing is limited to the window from when the laptop went missing until its access was blocked, and that where more than that window is needed she decides what is looked at and why and writes it into the record. She does not know what else was reached before the block beyond what the logs of that window show.

## What should happen

Every time counts from 14:00.

- By 18:00 Kari Nordmann decides from the six established facts whether the loss is a personal data breach and writes the decision into the record with her name, the reason and the time. Here, with the screen unlocked and a pupil data export downloaded after the laptop went missing, it is a personal data breach.
- In the same hour she decides it, she starts `Handle a personal data breach` by telling Marius Tangen and Ingrid Solheim that the loss is a personal data breach and that the breach process now runs.
- Marius Tangen then holds both processes and keeps one record and one timeline for the two. The moment the loss reached the company, 14:00 on Wednesday 25 November 2026, is the moment the company became aware, so the 72 hours of that process count from 14:00 and not from her decision, and the record says both times.
- This process keeps running and neither process waits for the other. This process keeps the device, the accounts, the report to the police, the insurer and the replacement; the other takes the fact page, Datatilsynet, the school owners and the people affected. The duties to tell the school owners, the parents and Datatilsynet are not duties of this process.
- Because something was reached from the laptop after it went missing and more than the window from 13:30 to the block is needed to see what was taken, Kari Nordmann decides what is looked at and why and writes that into the record. What is looked at is not used to see how Amina Farah works or what she worked on, and no conclusion about her performance or conduct is drawn from it.
- From her decision the processing rests also on Article 6(1)(c) of the GDPR with Articles 33 and 34, besides the legitimate interest it rested on from the start.
- Marius Tangen asks her before the wipe, because the laptop held personal data, and tells her in every case what was done to the device. The record of the case is kept 5 years from the day the case is closed, because the loss was also a personal data breach, and so is the entry in the equipment register.
- This process ends when Amina Farah is working again, the record is complete and her decision stands in the record; her decision that it is a breach does not end it, and the breach process running alongside does not end it either.

## Pass

The record holds Kari Nordmann's decision that the loss is a personal data breach, with her name, the reason and the time, written no later than 18:00. It shows that in the same hour she told Marius Tangen and Ingrid Solheim that it is a personal data breach and that `Handle a personal data breach` now runs. It holds both times: 14:00 as the moment the loss reached the company and the company became aware, from which the 72 hours of that process count, and the time of her decision. There is one record and one timeline for the two processes, kept by Marius Tangen. The steps of this process after her decision, the wipe, the police, the insurer and the replacement, are timed from 14:00 and none of them waits for the breach process. Where more than the window from 13:30 to the block was looked at, the record says that she decided it and why, and it holds no conclusion about how Amina Farah works. The record and the equipment register entry are marked to be kept 5 years from the day the case is closed.
