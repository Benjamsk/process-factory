# Answers 3

## 1. Where the person who had the lost device has left the company, or cannot be reached within 4 hours, how are they told what the company reads about them while it handles the loss and why, and within what time?

Nora Lind sends it to them, in writing, at the private email address or postal address the company holds for them in the employment file she keeps, within 24 hours of the report. It says the same things the person reached in the first 4 hours is told: what is read, which is the device's own entries in device management and the entries of their account in the sign-in logs of the office suite and the access logs of Nordsky Cloud for the window from when the device went missing until its access was blocked; that it is read only to find out what could have been reached; that it is not used to judge how they worked; how long the record is kept; and that Kari Nordmann is the data protection officer they can ask. Where the company holds no address that reaches them, Nora Lind says so in the record, with what was tried, and the telling is not dropped: it is sent at the first address the company later has.

For a person still at the company who simply could not be reached within the 4 hours, Marius Tangen sends the same thing to their work email within those 4 hours, so that it is waiting for them, and says it again when he first speaks to them.

## 2. Where neither Kari Nordmann nor Henrik Aas can be reached within 4 hours of the report, who decides whether the loss is a personal data breach, and by when must that decision be made?

Marius Tangen decides it, within the same 4 hours of the report, and Amina Farah where he is away. He decides it one way: where he cannot establish that the loss is not a personal data breach, he decides that it is one, and `Handle a personal data breach` starts at once. The 72 hours of that process already run from the moment the loss reached the company, so a decision that waits for the data protection officer spends the company's own deadline, and the cost of running the breach process for a loss that turns out to be harmless is far lower than the cost of starting it two days late.

He writes in the record that he decided it himself, whom he tried to reach and when, and what he based it on. Kari Nordmann, or Henrik Aas, looks at it again at the first hour one of them is reachable, and may decide that it is not a personal data breach after all; she then writes that line in the record with her name, her reason and the time, and the breach process stops with that line in its own record. She does not undo what was already done.

## 3. Where neither the on-call developer nor Marius Tangen can be reached and the report ends with Ingrid Solheim, or with Tomas Berg in her place, what does the one who took the report do with it: which steps do they carry out themselves, and what do they hand to Marius Tangen when he can be reached?

They do four things themselves and nothing in the systems, because they have no access to device management:

1. Open the record of the case and write in it the time the report reached the company, who reported it, what they said, and that they are holding it.
2. Telephone, in this order and not by email, the on-call developer on the on-call number, then Marius Tangen on his mobile, then Amina Farah, and keep trying every 30 minutes until one of them answers. Write each attempt and its time in the record.
3. Tell the person who reported it that the case is held and that someone will come back to them.
4. Ask the person who had the device for the facts only insofar as they are on the telephone anyway, and write what they say in the record.

They hold the case until Marius Tangen or Amina Farah writes in the record that they have taken it. The deadlines keep running while nobody with access can be reached, and the record says which deadline was missed and why, so that the gap is visible rather than quietly absorbed. Where four hours pass with nobody reachable, Ingrid Solheim, or Tomas Berg in her place, telephones Kari Nordmann, or Henrik Aas, so that the breach question is at least in the hands of someone, and writes that in the record.

When Marius Tangen can be reached, he gets the record as it stands, with the time of the report, the attempts and their times, whatever the person who had the device said, and what was not done. He then carries on from the step the process has reached, and does not start again from the beginning.
