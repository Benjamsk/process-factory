# Answers 5

## 1. Must the fact page say what the likely consequence of the breach is for every kind of person affected, so for teachers, employees and job applicants as well as for the pupils?

Yes. The fact page must say, for every kind of person the breach affects, what the likely consequence is for them: pupils, teachers, school contacts, employees and job applicants. Where a kind of person is affected and the consequence cannot be established, the fact page says that it is not established and why, and that counts as a high risk until it is established.

## 2. Who telephones Silje Haugland and Petter Dahl at home when a breach is confirmed outside their working hours?

The on-call developer, the one named on the rota for that week. The on-call developer is the only person besides Marius Tangen and Ingrid Solheim who can be reached around the clock, and already closes support on the subject of the breach outside working hours. If neither Silje Haugland nor Petter Dahl answers, the on-call developer telephones Ingrid Solheim and tells her that support has nobody.

## 3. Who puts the sentences support may use into the support desk, and who replaces the standing holding message with that wording once it is approved?

Petter Dahl, and Silje Haugland when he is away. The same person does both: he puts the standing holding message into the support desk when support is closed on the subject in working hours, and he replaces it with the approved wording the moment Ingrid Solheim has decided it and Kari Nordmann has checked it. Outside working hours the on-call developer puts the standing holding message in, and Petter Dahl replaces it with the approved wording when he is next reachable.

## 4. Who tells Nora Lind that a case is a breach of employee or applicant data, so that she starts her part of the process?

Marius Tangen, in the same step and at the same time as he tells Kari Nordmann and Ingrid Solheim, whenever the breach touches the payroll system, the hiring system or employee or applicant data in the office suite. He does not wait for anyone to think of her.

## 5. What do the 30 days for a measure that could let the same breach happen again and the 90 days for any other measure count from: the report of the breach, the closing of the case, or something else?

From the day the case is closed. The dates on the measures are written down within 2 weeks of the case being closed, a measure that could let the same breach happen again is in place within 30 days of the case being closed, and any other measure within 90 days of the case being closed.

## 6. Which hours of which days count as working hours in this process, so that a reader can tell whether a step falls to the on-call developer or to Silje Haugland and Petter Dahl?

Working hours are Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 16:00, the hours in which Silje Haugland and Petter Dahl are reachable. Every other hour, including every evening, every weekend and every public holiday, is outside working hours. A step that falls partly on each side belongs to whoever holds it at the moment the step has to be done.

## 7. On which legal ground does the company handle the health data that the payroll system holds, the absence and sick leave of the 48 employees, when such data has to go into the fact page, into a notification to Datatilsynet and into the message to the people affected?

On Article 9(2)(b): the handling is necessary for the company to carry out its obligations in the field of employment law, together with Article 6(1)(c), the legal obligation of Articles 33 and 34 to establish what happened, to notify Datatilsynet and to tell the people affected. On that ground the absence and sick leave are named in the fact page, in the notification and in the message only as the categories "absence" and "sick leave", with the number of employees affected and nothing about any individual's health, diagnosis or reason for absence. Where a notification or a message would need more than the category, Kari Nordmann decides what is written.

## 8. Is a breach of data the company controls notified to Datatilsynet in every case unless the fact page shows that the breach is unlikely to be a risk for the people affected, and is that the only ground on which it is not notified?

Yes. Datatilsynet is notified in every case, and the only ground for not notifying is that the fact page shows the breach is unlikely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of the people affected. Where the fact page cannot establish the risk, the breach is notified. Ingrid Solheim's decision is about that one question and nothing else.

## 9. When the fact page shows that a breach is likely to be a high risk for the people affected, are they always told, so that only the wording and the way they are reached remain to be decided?

Yes. Where the fact page shows a high risk for any kind of person affected, those people are told, and the decision is only about the wording and how they are reached. For the pupils of the reading app the company is the processor, so telling them means telling their school owner, which already happens within 24 hours, and the school owner decides about the parents. For teachers, school contacts, employees and job applicants the company is the controller and tells them itself, without undue delay and at the latest 72 hours after the fact page shows the high risk.

## 10. Does each affected school owner get its own separate message, all of them sent at the same time, so that no school contact sees the addresses of the others?

Yes. Silje Haugland sends one message per affected school owner, addressed to that school owner alone, and sends them all at the same time. No school contact sees the address of another, and no school owner is told before another.

## 11. How long are the secured copies of the Nordsky Cloud access logs kept, counted from the closing of the case?

12 months from the day the case is closed, and then Marius Tangen deletes them. They are kept longer only while a case about the breach with Datatilsynet, with a school owner or before a court is still open, and then until that case ends, and the record says why they were kept longer. Where the logs are kept, and that they hold personal data, is written in the record.

## 12. Is the decision whether the people affected are told written in the record with its reason and the name of the person who took it, also when the decision is not to tell them, as the decision on Datatilsynet is?

Yes. The decision is written in the record whether the answer is yes or no, with the reason, the name of the person who took it and the time, exactly as the decision on Datatilsynet is. The same holds for the decision that a case is not a breach at all.
