# Case 11: The 24 hours to the school owners run out at 03:00 on a Sunday

Kind: Outside working hours

## Situation

A breach of pupil data in the reading app is reported on a Saturday at 03:00 and is confirmed the same morning. Forty-one school owners are affected. The fact page is ready by Saturday evening and Ingrid Solheim has decided what the message to the school owners says. Silje Haugland and Petter Dahl are reachable in working hours only, Monday to Friday 08:00 to 16:00, and the 24 hours to the school owners run out at 03:00 on the Sunday.

## Who acts

Head of customer success, Silje Haugland.

## What the person knows

She knows that the breach is confirmed and that she was telephoned at home when support was closed to the subject. She knows how the school owners are reached, which is her part of this, and she has the message Ingrid Solheim decided and the part about the data that Kari Nordmann wrote. She does not know anything more about the systems than the fact page says.

## What should happen

All the times run around the clock, including evenings and weekends, and the 24 hours to the school owners run from the company becoming aware at 03:00 on the Saturday, not from the next working day. Every affected school owner is told within that 24 hours and before Datatilsynet is notified, and the message goes to all 41 at the same time, not to the big ones first. Silje Haugland sends it, Ingrid Solheim decides what it says and Kari Nordmann writes the part about the data, and the message says what happened, which data and how many pupils are affected, what the company has done and what the school owner should do. Each school owner's own 72 hours to Datatilsynet start when it is told, so a late message shortens the school owner's time. Petter Dahl steps in when Silje Haugland is away, and if neither of them answers at home it is escalated to Ingrid Solheim. The specification does not say who sends the message when the deadline falls outside the hours in which Silje Haugland and Petter Dahl are reachable; it gives the sending to Silje Haugland and requires the message within the 24 hours. The gap is not filled here.

## Pass

All 41 affected school owners received the same message by 03:00 on the Sunday, at the same time as each other and before any notification to Datatilsynet. The message says what happened, which data and how many pupils are affected, what the company has done and what the school owner should do, with the part about the data written by Kari Nordmann and the wording decided by Ingrid Solheim. The record holds the message that went to the school owners and the time it was sent. If Silje Haugland could not be reached, the record shows that Petter Dahl sent it, or that it was escalated to Ingrid Solheim.
