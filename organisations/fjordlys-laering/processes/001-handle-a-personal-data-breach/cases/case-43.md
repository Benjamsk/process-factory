# Case 43: Only one of the support team is reachable

Kind: Person away

## Situation

A breach of pupil data in the reading app at nine school owners is reported on a Wednesday at 08:30, and Marius Tangen confirms a real breach at 09:00 and telephones Petter Dahl to say that it is a real breach and that support is closed on the subject. Of the five people in the support team, Hanne Riise, Jostein Vold and Maja Eriksen are off sick and Sondre Lie is on a course all week with his telephone off, so Thea Nygård is the only one of the five reachable. By 11:00 the AI support assistant has handed over ninety cases and the queue is still growing. Silje Haugland is at work.

## Who acts

Support lead, Petter Dahl.

## What the person knows

He knows that the breach is confirmed, that support is closed on the subject and that pupils and school owners are affected. He has the standing configuration and the standing holding message ready, approved in advance by Ingrid Solheim and Kari Nordmann, and he knows that the sentences support may use are not decided yet. He knows which of the support team are reachable, and that Wednesday is one of Kari Nordmann's three working days. He does not know how many pupils or which school owners are affected, because that is the facts work that fills the fact page.

## What should happen

Where support is closed on the subject, Petter Dahl switches on the standing configuration, which puts the AI support assistant into hand-over for breach cases, and puts the standing holding message into the support desk, and writes down when he did it. Where fewer than two of the support team are reachable in working hours, Petter Dahl answers the queue himself, and Silje Haugland steps in for him when he is away; he is not away, so the queue is his, and Thea Nygård takes cases beside him. While the breach is running, whoever answers answers only with the sentences that stand in the support desk and brings every question they cannot answer to Petter Dahl, and he collects every question support cannot answer and brings it to Kari Nordmann once a day, so that the answers are added rather than each person inventing something. Ingrid Solheim decides the sentences support may use within 3 hours of the report, so by 11:30, after advice from Kari Nordmann, whose check is done inside that time, and Petter Dahl puts them into the support desk in place of the standing holding message within 4 hours of the report, so by 12:30; where Ingrid Solheim is not reachable in that time Tomas Berg decides them, and where neither is reachable the standing holding message stays and the record says why. The sentences say that the company knows about it, that it is looking into it, who is handling it and when the person will hear more, and nothing else. The AI support assistant is not switched off, because that would bury the support team on the worst day of the year: it keeps answering the ordinary cases that carry no sign, within the AI use policy, and hands over every case that carries a sign of the breach and answers nothing in those cases. Petter Dahl may add a word or a sign to the list while the case runs and writes down what he added and when, and he may take nothing off the list while the case runs, however long the queue is. The specification gives the queue in this situation to Petter Dahl himself and says nothing about bringing in anyone from outside the support team to answer it, nor about how long a case may wait; that gap is not filled here. What it does require is that nothing is answered outside the sentences that stand in the support desk, and the rest of the process runs on at its own times: the logs secured and the breach stopped within 4 hours of 08:30, Kari Nordmann and Ingrid Solheim and Silje Haugland told within 4 hours, the fact page finished within 24 hours, and every affected school owner told within 24 hours of the company becoming aware and before Datatilsynet is notified.

## Pass

The record says when the standing configuration was switched on and the standing holding message put into the support desk, and names Petter Dahl as the one who did it. Petter Dahl answered the queue himself beside Thea Nygård, and nobody outside the support team answered a case about the breach. Every answer that went to a teacher or a school contact stood within the sentences that stood in the support desk at that moment and said nothing about how many pupils are affected, whether data was accessed or whose fault it is. The sentences Ingrid Solheim decided, with Kari Nordmann's check, stood in the support desk by 12:30 on the Wednesday in place of the standing holding message, or the record says why the standing holding message stayed. Nothing was taken off the list of hand-over signs while the case ran, and the record names anything Petter Dahl added and when. The questions support could not answer were brought to Kari Nordmann that day. The nine affected school owners were told by 08:30 on the Thursday and before any notification to Datatilsynet.
