# Case 7: Documents about pupils in the office suite

Kind: Edge of scope

## Situation

On a Thursday at 13:00 Microsoft notifies the tenant administrator that the account of one employee was taken over for three days and that the mailbox and the files of that account were downloaded. The account belongs to an employee whose documents include spreadsheets about pupils at several school owners, exported from the reading app, and the employee's email with teachers. No access was made to the reading app or to Nordsky Cloud.

## Who acts

Data protection officer, Kari Nordmann.

## What the person knows

She has the notification from Microsoft through Marius Tangen, the tenant administrator, and she knows that the office suite holds the email and documents of all 48 employees, which include documents about pupils, teachers and job applicants. She keeps the record of processing activities, so she knows which processing activities those documents belong to. She does not know which documents were in the account or how many pupils and which school owners they name, because that is work in the systems that fills the fact page.

## What should happen

The process covers a personal data breach of all personal data in all the company's systems, and the office suite is one of them, so this is in scope and is handled as one case with one record and one timeline. The fact page says, for each kind of data, whether the company is the controller or the processor. Teacher data is data the company is the controller for, so Ingrid Solheim decides whether Datatilsynet is notified after advice from Kari Nordmann, at the latest 48 hours in, and the notification goes within 72 hours of becoming aware. For the pupil data in the documents the specification names only one situation, pupil data in the reading app, where the company is the processor and the school owner is the controller; it does not say which the company is for exported spreadsheets about pupils that sit in the office suite. The gap is not filled here. What the specification does require is that Kari Nordmann keeps one list of duties for the case with both sides on the same list, that the deadline that binds the case is the earliest one on it, and that the fact page states for each kind of data whether the company is the controller or the processor, writing what cannot be established as not established.

## Pass

One record and one timeline exist for the case, not one per kind of data. Marius Tangen had the case within 1 hour of the notification arriving at 13:00, and Kari Nordmann and Ingrid Solheim had it by 17:00. The fact page states, for each kind of data in the account, whether the company is the controller or the processor, and where that could not be established it says so. Kari Nordmann's single list of duties for the case exists, holds the pupil side and the controller side together and names the earliest deadline as the one that binds. Ingrid Solheim's decision on Datatilsynet for the teacher data is in the record with its reason and her name by 13:00 on the Saturday.
