# Case 49: The facts in the support desk are due on a Saturday morning

Kind: Outside working hours

## Situation

On Friday 27 November 2026 at 19:00 a school contact telephones the on-call number: when she opens her own cases in the support desk she can see the cases of two other school owners, with the names and work email addresses of their teachers and the text of what they wrote. Erik Moen, who is on call that week, takes the report and wakes Marius Tangen, who confirms a real breach at 20:00. Erik Moen telephones Silje Haugland and Petter Dahl at home at once and both answer. The system touched is the support desk, and the next working hour is 08:00 on the Monday.

## Who acts

Head of IT, Marius Tangen.

## What the person knows

He was woken by Erik Moen and has the report. He knows the support desk holds the name, work email address, telephone number and school of about 6,000 teachers and 310 school contacts, the text of every case and the notes the support team writes, and that Silje Haugland is the person responsible for it, with Petter Dahl stepping in when she is away. He knows that both of them are reachable in working hours only, Monday to Friday 08:00 to 16:00, and that both answered when Erik Moen telephoned them at home. He knows Friday evening is not one of Kari Nordmann's three working days. He does not know how many cases and how many teachers and school contacts are exposed, or whether anyone outside those school owners read them; that is the facts work in the support desk.

## What should happen

All the times run around the clock, including evenings, weekends and public holidays, and the clock starts at 19:00 on the Friday, when the report reached the company. Marius Tangen asks the person responsible for every system his record names as possibly touched within 2 hours of the report, so by 21:00, and outside working hours the on-call developer asks for him; whom he asked and when goes into the record. The person responsible for the support desk establishes in it the categories of data touched, whose data and how many people, the time window and what the logs of that system show, and gives that to Marius Tangen within 12 hours of being asked, so by 09:00 on the Saturday, so that the fact page can be finished within 24 hours of the report, by 19:00 on the Saturday; where that person is away, the person who steps in for them in the section Roles does it, which for Silje Haugland is Petter Dahl. The specification does not say who establishes the facts in the support desk when the person responsible and her stand-in are both outside the hours in which they are reachable and neither is away in the sense of the section Roles; it names the person responsible, names the stand-in, and sets the 12 hours and the 24 hours. The gap is not filled here. What the specification does require runs on: the Nordsky Cloud access logs secured and the breach stopped within 4 hours of 19:00, with the on-call developer doing at once every one of closing the access, revoking the token and locking the account that the role can do and telling Marius Tangen what was done and when; Erik Moen starting the fact page with what is known in the first hours, with Marius Tangen or Amina Farah taking it over, and Marius Tangen owning it and signing it off; Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold on the firm's emergency number, because Friday evening is not one of Kari Nordmann's working days, and Ingrid Solheim having the case within 4 hours, and Silje Haugland told at the same time because teacher and school contact data is touched; support closed on the subject because teachers and school contacts are affected, so Erik Moen switches on the standing configuration and puts the standing holding message into the support desk and writes down when it was done; the sentences support may use decided by Ingrid Solheim within 3 hours with Henrik Aas's check, with Petter Dahl replacing the standing holding message with them when he is next reachable; and the fact page saying what cannot be established and why rather than leaving it blank, with the case handled as a high risk until it is established. The company is the controller for support desk data, teacher data and school contact data, so Ingrid Solheim decides at the latest 48 hours after the report whether Datatilsynet is notified and the notification goes at the latest 72 hours after the company became aware.

## Pass

The record shows that the person responsible for the support desk was asked by 21:00 on the Friday, names who was asked, by whom and at what time, and shows what was given back and when. A fact page exists by 19:00 on the Saturday, started by Erik Moen and owned and signed off by Marius Tangen, and whatever could not be established about the support desk stands on it as not established and why, with no blank field, and was handled as a high risk while it was not established. The logs were secured and the breach stopped by 23:00 on the Friday, and the record says which of closing the access, revoking the token and locking the account were done, which were not and why, and that Erik Moen told Marius Tangen what he had done and when. Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold, Ingrid Solheim and Silje Haugland had the case by 23:00 on the Friday. The record says when the standing configuration was switched on and the standing holding message put into the support desk and who did it, and the sentences Ingrid Solheim decided with Henrik Aas's check replaced the standing holding message when Petter Dahl was next reachable. Ingrid Solheim's decision on Datatilsynet stands in the record with its reason, her name and the time by 19:00 on the Sunday.
