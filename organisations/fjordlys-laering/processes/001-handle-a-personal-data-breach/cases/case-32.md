# Case 32: Neither the managing director nor the head of finance can be reached for the sentences

Kind: Person away

## Situation

On a Tuesday at 08:40 Nordsky AS reports that an export of the reading app with the names, classes and reading levels of pupils at nine school owners lay open on the internet for a day. Marius Tangen takes the case and confirms a real breach at 09:30, and because pupils and school owners are affected Petter Dahl switches the AI support assistant into hand-over for breach cases and puts the standing holding message into the support desk at 09:40. Ingrid Solheim is on an aircraft all morning and Tomas Berg is in a meeting abroad with his telephone off, and neither can be reached before the afternoon. The 3 hours for the sentences support may use run out at 11:40 and the 4 hours for those sentences to stand in the support desk at 12:40.

## Who acts

Support lead, Petter Dahl.

## What the person knows

He knows that the breach is confirmed, that he switched the hand-over on at 09:40 and put in the standing holding message that Ingrid Solheim and Kari Nordmann approved in advance, and that the sentences support may use are decided by Ingrid Solheim and checked by Kari Nordmann, who works that Tuesday. He can see the cases coming into the support desk. He does not know when Ingrid Solheim or Tomas Berg will be reachable, and he does not know what the fact page will say about how many pupils are affected.

## What should happen

Ingrid Solheim decides the sentences support may use with Kari Nordmann's check done inside 3 hours of the report, and where Ingrid Solheim is not reachable in time Tomas Berg decides them in her place. Where neither of them is reachable to decide them within the 3 hours, the standing holding message stays and the record says why, so nothing is written in their place and no support person makes up sentences of their own. Support may say that the company knows about it, that it is looking into it, who is handling it and when the person will hear more, and nothing else, and may not say how many pupils are affected, whether data was accessed, whose fault it is or that it is probably nothing. The AI support assistant keeps answering ordinary cases, hands over every case that carries a sign of the breach and says nothing about the breach itself, not even that it is happening. Petter Dahl collects every question support cannot answer and brings it to Kari Nordmann once a day. As soon as Ingrid Solheim or Tomas Berg has decided the sentences and Kari Nordmann has checked them, Petter Dahl puts them into the support desk in place of the standing holding message. The rest of the case runs on: the fact page is finished within 24 hours of the report, and every affected school owner is told within 24 hours of 08:40 and before any notification to Datatilsynet.

## Pass

At 11:40 and at 12:40 the standing holding message approved in advance by Ingrid Solheim and Kari Nordmann still stood in the support desk, and no other wording stood there. No support person and no manager wrote sentences of their own in the meantime. The record says why the standing holding message stayed, naming that neither Ingrid Solheim nor Tomas Berg could be reached to decide the sentences within the 3 hours. When the sentences were decided, the record names who decided them and when, shows that Kari Nordmann checked them, and shows that Petter Dahl put them into the support desk in place of the standing holding message and when. The record says when the AI support assistant was switched into hand-over and by whom, and no answer it sent says anything about the breach. The questions support could not answer were brought to Kari Nordmann that day. All nine affected school owners received their own message by 08:40 on the Wednesday and before any notification to Datatilsynet.
