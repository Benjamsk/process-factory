# Case 10: A parent writes to the managing director

Kind: Late or wrong notice

## Situation

On a Thursday at 16:20 a parent sends an email to Ingrid Solheim's own address. He writes that he found a page on the internet that plays his daughter reading aloud, that the page is not behind any login and that he found it by searching for his daughter's name. He has sent the same message to nobody else. Ingrid Solheim reads it at 16:25 between two meetings.

## Who acts

Managing director, Ingrid Solheim.

## What the person knows

She knows what the parent wrote and the address he gives. She knows that read-aloud recordings of pupils are data of the reading app and that the parent is not a customer of the company but a parent at a school owner. She does not know whether the page is the reading app's own storage, how many recordings can be reached that way or which school owners the pupils belong to, and she cannot find that out herself.

## What should happen

Every report, from inside or outside the company, ends up with Marius Tangen, and the clock starts when the report reaches the company at 16:20, not when someone gets round to reading it. When an outsider, a researcher or a parent, writes to the general address or to the managing director, whoever receives it forwards it to Marius Tangen and telephones him, so Ingrid Solheim forwards the email and telephones him rather than only forwarding it. Marius Tangen has the case within 1 hour of 16:20. He secures the Nordsky Cloud access logs before anything is deleted or fixed in production, stops the breach, and both are done within 4 hours of 16:20, which is past the end of the working day because all the times run around the clock. He writes down the time of the report and who reported it. Kari Nordmann and Ingrid Solheim have the case within 4 hours. The data is pupil data of the reading app, so every affected school owner is told within 24 hours of 16:20, at the same time and before Datatilsynet is notified, and the school owner then notifies Datatilsynet and decides whether the parents are told. The specification leaves the notification of the parents to the school owner, so the company does not answer the parent with that decision.

## Pass

The record names 16:20 on the Thursday as the time of the report and the parent as the one who reported it. Ingrid Solheim both forwarded the email to Marius Tangen and telephoned him, and he had the case by 17:20. The Nordsky Cloud access logs were secured and the page closed by 20:20 the same evening, before any fix went to production. Kari Nordmann, or Henrik Aas if she was away, and Ingrid Solheim had the case by 20:20. Every affected school owner was told by 16:20 on the Friday and before any notification to Datatilsynet, and the decision whether the parents are told was left to the school owner.
