# Case 30: The biggest school owner wants something in writing now

Kind: Conflicting rules

## Situation

On a Tuesday at 10:00 a developer reports that a page of the reading app returned the reading history of pupils at other schools, and Marius Tangen confirms a real breach at 11:00. At 11:30 the school contact of the largest affected school owner, which runs 40 schools, telephones Silje Haugland: a teacher has told it something is wrong, it wants a written account of what happened before the end of the day, and it says it will telephone Ingrid Solheim if it does not get one. Kari Nordmann has not yet had the case, and Ingrid Solheim has not yet decided what the message to the school owners says. Ten other school owners are affected.

## Who acts

Head of customer success, Silje Haugland.

## What the person knows

She knows that the breach is confirmed, that pupil data of the reading app is affected, so the company is the processor and the school owners are the controllers, and she knows how each school owner is reached, which is her part of this. She has the sentences support may use once they are decided. She does not know how many pupils are affected, which school owners they belong to or what the logs show, and the fact page is not written yet.

## What should happen

Two rules of the specification pull in different directions. Every affected school owner is told without undue delay and at the latest 24 hours after the report, which is 10:00 on the Wednesday, and Silje Haugland is the one who sends the messages. At the same time nobody emails the school owners before the data protection officer has looked at the case, Ingrid Solheim decides what the message says after advice from Kari Nordmann, who writes the part about the data, Silje Haugland is asked how the school owners are reached before that decision is taken, and the messages go one per affected school owner, addressed to that school owner alone, all at the same time, with no school owner told before another and no telephone call to the big ones first. The specification settles it on the second set: Silje Haugland does not write that school owner an account of her own at 11:30, and the size of the school owner buys it nothing. Kari Nordmann and Ingrid Solheim have the case within 4 hours of 10:00, so by 14:00, and the eleven messages go together within the 24 hours and before any notification to Datatilsynet, because a school owner that heard about the breach from Datatilsynet or from a newspaper would mean the company had broken the contract. Until then, what the school contact is told is what support may say: that the company knows about it, that it is looking into it, who is handling it and when it will hear more, and nothing about how many pupils are affected, whether data was accessed or whose fault it is.

## Pass

Nothing went to that school owner at 11:30 beyond the sentences support may use, and the record shows that the first written account it received was the message all eleven school owners received. Kari Nordmann had looked at the case before any message went to a school owner, and the record shows she and Ingrid Solheim had the case by 14:00 on the Tuesday. Ingrid Solheim decided what the message says after advice from Kari Nordmann, who wrote the part about the data, and the record shows Silje Haugland was asked how the school owners are reached before that decision. All eleven affected school owners received their own message at the same time, by 10:00 on the Wednesday and before any notification to Datatilsynet, and no school contact saw the address of another. The record holds the message that went to the school owners and the time it was sent.
