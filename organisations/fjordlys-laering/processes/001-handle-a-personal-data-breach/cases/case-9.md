# Case 9: Not a breach, on a Monday

Kind: Person away

## Situation

On a Monday at 08:30 an employee reports that she clicked a link in a phishing email and typed her password into the page before she realised. Marius Tangen resets her password and reads the Nordsky Cloud and office suite sign-in logs: there is no sign-in from anywhere but her own machine, and the account was locked within twelve minutes. By 11:00 he is satisfied that no personal data was exposed and wants the case closed the same day. Monday is not one of Kari Nordmann's three working days.

## Who acts

Head of IT, Marius Tangen.

## What the person knows

He knows the report, what he did and what the sign-in logs of Nordsky Cloud and the office suite show. He knows that Kari Nordmann works Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday and that Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold can be reached on the firm's emergency number on the other days. He does not know what Datatilsynet would make of the case, which is the advice he does not give himself.

## What should happen

A suspicion that personal data may have been exposed starts the process, and a phishing click by an employee is named as such a start, so the case is a reported case and a document is written for it. Marius Tangen may judge that it is not a breach, but he may not close the case even when it looks obvious to him, and he may not close his own case. The decision that the case is not a breach is the data protection officer's, and she must agree before it is closed. Because Monday is not one of Kari Nordmann's working days, the case goes to Henrik Aas on the firm's emergency number and does not wait for Tuesday. The line in the record is written by the one who decides, with the name and the reason, so that there is a name on it if Datatilsynet ever asks. The logs Marius Tangen secured are stored where Kari Nordmann can find them again. The case is notified to nobody outside the company.

## Pass

The case has a record of its own, written on the Monday. Henrik Aas, not Marius Tangen, decided that the case is not a breach, and he was reached on the firm's emergency number on the Monday rather than the case waiting for Tuesday. The record holds the line that closes the case with the name of the person who decided and the reason. The record also holds when the case was reported and by whom, what was done and what the sign-in logs showed. No school owner, no affected person and not Datatilsynet was notified. The secured logs are stored where Kari Nordmann can find them again.
