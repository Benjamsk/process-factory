# gdpr review of draft-1.md

Verdict: CHANGES

- [high] Step 17, and the third bullet under `Who must be told` / outside the company: telling the affected people is left to Ingrid Solheim's free choice with no deadline for that choice, so the 24 hours run from a moment that may never come; Article 34 requires that the affected people are told without undue delay whenever the breach is likely to be a high risk for them, and the draft must say that the people are told when the risk for them is high and give the decision a deadline the reader can act on, at the latest the same 48 hours as the decision on Datatilsynet. `spec.md` gives no deadline for this decision either.
- [medium] Step 12, the fact page: the only judgement of the risk is "the likely consequence for the children", so a breach of teacher, support desk, employee or applicant data gets no risk judgement at all, although Articles 33 and 34 and the decisions in steps 15 and 17 rest on it; the page must hold the likely consequence for every group of people whose data is affected. `spec.md` names only the children here.
- [medium] `What to record`, last paragraph: the Nordsky Cloud access logs secured in step 2 hold personal data about everyone who logged in, and the draft gives them neither a place ("where Kari Nordmann can find them again" is not a place) nor a time they are kept, while the breach record itself is kept for five years; Article 5 requires that the draft says where the secured logs are kept and how long. `spec.md` gives neither.
- [medium] `What to record`, the list of what the record of a breach holds: only the decision on Datatilsynet is recorded with its reason and the name of the person who took it; Ingrid Solheim's decision in step 17 on whether the affected people are told must be recorded the same way, with its reason and her name, whether the answer is yes or no, because Articles 33 and 34 require a decision not to tell the people to be documented with its reason. `spec.md` lists it only for Datatilsynet.
- [medium] `When this applies`, the paragraph on scope, and step 12: the payroll system is in scope and `organisation/systems.md` says it holds the absence and sick leave of the 48 employees, which is health data, so a payroll breach makes the draft record, notify and tell about special category data; Article 9 needs a ground of its own for that, and the section `Legal basis` of `spec.md` gives none, so the ground is missing from `spec.md` as well as from the draft.
- [low] Step 14, and the first bullet under `Who must be told` / outside the company: "which data and how many pupils are affected" sent to all affected school owners at the same time does not say that each school owner is told about its own pupils only, and Article 5 lets a school owner have no more than the data it needs for its own pupils; the draft should say that the message tells each school owner which of its own pupils and how many are affected.
