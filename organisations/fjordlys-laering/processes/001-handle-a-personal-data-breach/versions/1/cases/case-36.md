# Case 36: The ground for not notifying falls away a week later

Kind: Missing knowledge

## Situation

On a Tuesday at 09:00 Hjelpedesk AS reports that a link to an export of the support desk contact list, with the names, work email addresses, telephone numbers and schools of about 6,000 teachers and 310 school contacts, was reachable without logging in for eight minutes the day before. The fact page is finished on the Wednesday and says, on what the logs of Hjelpedesk AS show, that nobody outside the company fetched the export, and Kari Nordmann judges that the breach is unlikely to result in a risk for the teachers and the school contacts. On the Wednesday at 15:00 Ingrid Solheim decides that Datatilsynet is not notified, and the decision goes into the record with its reason, her name and the time. On the Tuesday of the following week at 10:00 Hjelpedesk AS writes again: its first reading of its logs was wrong, and the export was fetched twice from outside the company. The case is still open, because Kari Nordmann has not yet said that the record is complete.

## Who acts

Managing director, Ingrid Solheim.

## What the person knows

She has the record, the fact page, the advice Kari Nordmann gave her before she decided, and what Hjelpedesk AS has now written. She knows that she decided on the Wednesday that Datatilsynet is not notified and why, and that the data is support desk data, which the company is the controller for. She does not know whether the two fetches were used for anything, and the logs of Hjelpedesk AS cannot tell her that.

## What should happen

Datatilsynet is notified for a breach of data the company controls unless the fact page shows that the breach is unlikely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of the people affected, and that is the only ground for not notifying; where the fact page cannot establish the risk, the breach is notified. The ground the decision of the Wednesday rested on has fallen away, so Ingrid Solheim decides that one question again, after advice from Kari Nordmann and after asking Marius Tangen what happened in the systems, and Tomas Berg decides when she is away. The Tuesday is one of Kari Nordmann's three working days. The fact page carries what Hjelpedesk AS has now said, Kari Nordmann judges the risk for the teachers and for the school contacts again, each kind of person separately, and writes that judgement on the page as a sentence with the reason, and what still cannot be established is written as not established and counts as a high risk until it is established. Kari Nordmann writes the notification from the fact page and sends it through Datatilsynet's own reporting form, Marius Tangen reads it first for whether the technical description is actually true, and Ingrid Solheim approves it before it is sent. Because it goes later than 72 hours after the company became aware, which ran out at 09:00 on the Friday of the week before, the notification says why it is late. The sent notification, the time it was sent and the receipt from Datatilsynet go into the record, next to both decisions on Datatilsynet, each with its reason, the name of the person who took it and the time. Where the fact page now shows a high risk for the teachers or the school contacts, they are told, and the decision is only about the wording and the way they are reached: Ingrid Solheim decides after advice from Kari Nordmann, Silje Haugland tells them, Kari Nordmann writes the part that describes the data and what the person can do and checks that everything required is in the message, and it goes out within 24 hours of Ingrid Solheim's decision and at the latest 72 hours after the fact page shows the high risk. The case is not closed until Kari Nordmann says the record is complete and Ingrid Solheim closes it.

## Pass

The record holds both decisions on Datatilsynet: the decision of 15:00 on the Wednesday not to notify, with its reason, Ingrid Solheim's name and the time, and the decision taken after 10:00 on the following Tuesday to notify, with its reason, her name and the time. The fact page now says that the export was fetched twice from outside the company and carries Kari Nordmann's risk sentence for the teachers and for the school contacts, each with its reason, and what still could not be established is written as not established, with no blank field. Kari Nordmann wrote the notification from the fact page, Marius Tangen read it for whether the technical description is true, Ingrid Solheim approved it, and it was sent through Datatilsynet's own reporting form, and it says why it was sent later than 72 hours after the company became aware. The record holds the sent notification, the time it was sent and the receipt from Datatilsynet. Where the teachers and the school contacts were told, Silje Haugland sent each of them a message within 24 hours of Ingrid Solheim's decision, with the part about the data and what the person can do written by Kari Nordmann, and the record holds that decision with its reason, her name and the time. The case was not closed before Kari Nordmann said the record was complete.
