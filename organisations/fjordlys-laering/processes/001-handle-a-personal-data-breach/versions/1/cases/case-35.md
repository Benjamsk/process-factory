# Case 35: A second system turns out to be touched on the second day

Kind: Missing knowledge

## Situation

On a Monday at 08:00 Microsoft notifies Marius Tangen, the tenant administrator, that the office suite account of an employee in hiring was taken over for two days. By 09:30 his record of the case names the office suite and the hiring system as the systems that may be touched, because that is what the account was used for, and he asks Nora Lind for the facts in the hiring system. On the Tuesday at 14:00, 30 hours after the report, the work on the fact page shows that the taken-over mailbox also held an export from the support desk with the names, work email addresses, telephone numbers and schools of teachers and school contacts. Nobody has asked Silje Haugland anything.

## Who acts

Head of IT, Marius Tangen.

## What the person knows

He has the notification from Microsoft, what the office suite shows about the two days and what Nora Lind has given him about the hiring system. He knows that Silje Haugland is responsible for the support desk and that Hjelpedesk AS supplies it. He does not know how many teachers and school contacts are in the export, or whether the person who took the account over opened it; that is work in the systems that has still to be done.

## What should happen

Marius Tangen asks the person responsible for every system his record names as possibly touched within 2 hours of the report, and asks a further one as soon as a new system turns out to be touched, so he asks Silje Haugland at 14:00 on the Tuesday and does not wait for the fact page to be finished first. Whom he asked and when goes into the record, for her as for Nora Lind. Each person responsible does the work in their own system and gives what they find to Marius Tangen within 12 hours of being asked, so Silje Haugland gives it by 02:00 on the Wednesday and Petter Dahl steps in where she is away, and Marius Tangen still owns the fact page and signs it off. The fact page then says, for the support desk data as for the rest, which data was affected and how sensitive, which people and how many, whether the company is the controller or the processor for it, the window, whether anyone actually accessed it and the likely consequence for each kind of person affected, and what cannot be established is written as not established and why rather than left blank, and counts as a high risk until it is established. The fact page is not finished until Kari Nordmann says she has what she needs, and she may ask for anything more her advice requires. Teachers and school contacts turn out to be affected, so support is closed on the subject of the breach from that moment: the AI support assistant is switched into hand-over for breach cases and the standing holding message is put into the support desk, in working hours by Petter Dahl, and whoever does it writes down when. Kari Nordmann keeps one list of the duties of the case, with the new system on the same list, and the deadline that binds the case is the earliest one on it. Ingrid Solheim decides whether Datatilsynet is notified at the latest 48 hours after the report, which is 08:00 on the Wednesday, after advice from Kari Nordmann and after asking Marius Tangen what happened in the systems.

## Pass

The record names the office suite and the hiring system as the systems first named as possibly touched, and shows that Nora Lind was asked by 10:00 on the Monday. The record shows that Silje Haugland was asked at 14:00 on the Tuesday, as soon as the support desk turned out to be touched, and names her and the time. She gave Marius Tangen what she found by 02:00 on the Wednesday. One record, one timeline and one list of duties exist for the case, with the support desk on the same list, and the list names the earliest deadline as the one that binds. The fact page is owned and signed off by Marius Tangen, covers the support desk data as well, and writes what could not be established as not established and why, with no blank field. Kari Nordmann said she had what she needed before the fact page was treated as final. The record says when the AI support assistant was switched into hand-over and the standing holding message put into the support desk, and by whom. Ingrid Solheim's decision on Datatilsynet stands in the record with its reason, her name and the time by 08:00 on the Wednesday.
