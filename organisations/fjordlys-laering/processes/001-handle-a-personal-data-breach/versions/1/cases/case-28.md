# Case 28: The six months run out while the support lead is on leave

Kind: Person away

## Situation

The standing configuration of the AI support assistant and the standing holding message for the support desk were last reviewed on 20 May 2026. Petter Dahl has been on leave since 1 October 2026 and comes back in February 2027. The six months since the last review run out on 20 November 2026. No breach is running, and nothing has reminded anyone: the standing configuration and the standing holding message have simply been sitting ready since May.

## Who acts

Head of customer success, Silje Haugland.

## What the person knows

She knows that the standing configuration hands breach cases over to a human so that nobody has to write it at midnight, and that the standing holding message is approved in advance by Ingrid Solheim and Kari Nordmann. She is responsible for the support desk and the support team of five, and she steps in for Petter Dahl while he is away. She does not know whether the list of signs still matches the words teachers are using, or whether the holding message still reads right; that is what the review is for.

## What should happen

The standing configuration and the standing holding message are reviewed every six months, so the review falls due on 20 November 2026 whether or not a breach is running, and it is the support lead's step. He is away, and Silje Haugland steps in for him. The specification names, for the case where the support lead is away, that the head of customer success switches the hand-over on, puts the holding message and the approved sentences into the support desk and decides about an answer the assistant has already sent; it does not name the six-monthly review in that list. The gap is not filled here. What the specification does require is that the standing configuration that hands breach cases over to a human is ready before any breach, that the standing holding message is ready and approved in advance by Ingrid Solheim and Kari Nordmann, and that both are reviewed every six months. Where the review changes the holding message, the changed message is the one that has been approved in advance by Ingrid Solheim and Kari Nordmann, because that is what makes it usable at midnight. The signs the configuration hands a case over on are the words of the specification in Norwegian and in English, together with a case from a parent rather than a teacher or a school contact and a case that names the system the breach is in.

## Pass

The standing configuration and the standing holding message were reviewed by 20 November 2026, six months after 20 May 2026, and not left until Petter Dahl came back in February 2027. Both still exist ready to switch on, so that nothing has to be written while a breach runs. The standing configuration still hands over every case that carries a sign of the breach to a human. Where the review changed the standing holding message, the message in place carries the approval of Ingrid Solheim and Kari Nordmann, given before it was put in.
