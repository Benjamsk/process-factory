# Case 22: An answer has already gone out and the support lead is on holiday

Kind: AI failure

## Situation

Petter Dahl is on holiday for a fortnight. A breach of pupil data in the reading app is reported on a Wednesday at 08:00 and Marius Tangen confirms it at 09:00. Silje Haugland switches the AI support assistant into the standing hand-over configuration and puts the standing holding message into the support desk at 09:20. At 10:30 she goes back through the morning queue and finds that at 08:40, before the hand-over was switched on, a teacher wrote "the recordings of my class have disappeared" and the assistant answered her itself: it wrote that the recordings of her class had been available outside the app and were being looked at, and it named the pupil whose recording the teacher had asked about. The word recordings is not on the list of signs the assistant hands a case over on.

## Who acts

Head of customer success, Silje Haugland.

## What the person knows

She knows that the breach is confirmed, that she switched the hand-over on at 09:20 and put the standing holding message in, and that she steps in for Petter Dahl while he is away. She can read the answer the assistant sent and the teacher's case, and she knows that Wednesday is one of Kari Nordmann's three working days. She does not know what the fact page will say about how many pupils are affected, and she may say nothing about the breach beyond the sentences support is given.

## What should happen

Anything about an answer the AI support assistant has already sent is decided by the support lead, and by the head of customer success when he is away, so the decision about the answer of 08:40 is Silje Haugland's. The answer touches personal data, because it names a pupil, so she asks Kari Nordmann, who works that Wednesday. The assistant may say nothing about the breach, not even that there is one, and that answer did, so it is the kind of answer the hand-over is there to prevent. From 09:20 the assistant hands every case that carries a sign of the breach to a human and answers nothing in those cases, while it keeps answering the ordinary cases that carry no sign. The teacher's case goes to a human support person. The sentences support may use stand in the support desk as actual sentences within 4 hours of the report, which is by 12:00; Ingrid Solheim decides them and Kari Nordmann checks them, and Silje Haugland puts them in and replaces the standing holding message with them while Petter Dahl is away. Every question support cannot answer is collected and brought to Kari Nordmann once a day. The specification does not say who may add a word or a sign to the list of signs in working hours while the support lead is away: it gives that to the support lead, and outside working hours to the on-call developer, who tells him. The gap is not filled here, and what is still required is that nothing is taken off the list while the case runs and that every word or sign added stands in the record with who added it and when.

## Pass

The record says that Silje Haugland switched the AI support assistant into hand-over at 09:20 on the Wednesday and put the standing holding message in, and names her as the one who did it. Silje Haugland, not Petter Dahl and not Marius Tangen, decided what happens about the answer of 08:40, and the record shows that she asked Kari Nordmann because the answer named a pupil. The teacher's case is in the hands of a human support person. From 09:20 no answer the assistant sent says anything about the breach, and it is still answering ordinary cases. The sentences support may use stood in the support desk by 12:00, decided by Ingrid Solheim and checked by Kari Nordmann, and they replaced the standing holding message. Nothing was taken off the list of signs while the case ran, and the record names every word or sign added, by whom and when.
