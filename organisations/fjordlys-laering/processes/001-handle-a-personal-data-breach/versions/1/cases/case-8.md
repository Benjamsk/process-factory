# Case 8: The AI support assistant answers a teacher who asks about the leak

Kind: AI failure

## Situation

A breach of pupil data in the reading app was confirmed on a Tuesday at 09:00 and support was closed to the subject at 09:30. At 11:00 a teacher writes to support: "A parent tells me our pupils' reading recordings have been leaked. Is that true?" The AI support assistant answers the message itself from a help article about recordings and does not hand the case to a human. Three more messages that use the words leak and privacy are answered the same way in the next hour.

## Who acts

Support lead, Petter Dahl.

## What the person knows

He knows that the breach is confirmed and that support was closed to the subject at 09:30, he knows the standing configuration he prepared and the approved help articles the assistant answers from, and he can see the four cases the assistant answered. He does not know what the fact page will say about how many pupils are affected, and he may not say anything about the breach beyond the sentences support is given.

## What should happen

The AI support assistant is not switched off while the case runs, because it keeps answering ordinary support cases from the approved help articles. Petter Dahl must configure it to hand over to a human every case that touches the breach, and it must hand over on the ordinary signs a teacher would use, words such as leak, hacked, data, privacy or Datatilsynet, and a complaining parent, rather than on someone guessing the right keyword. The thing that must happen is that Petter Dahl actually changes the configuration and writes down when he did it. The assistant may say nothing about the breach itself, not even that there is one. The four cases go to a human, and the sentences support may use must exist as actual sentences in the support desk within 4 hours of the report, with Ingrid Solheim deciding the wording and Kari Nordmann checking it; in working hours the real wording replaces the standing holding message. Petter Dahl collects every question support cannot answer and brings it to Kari Nordmann once a day. The specification does not say what is done about an answer the assistant has already sent. The gap is not filled here.

## Pass

The assistant hands over every case that uses words such as leak, hacked, data, privacy or Datatilsynet, and every complaining parent, and the record says when Petter Dahl changed the configuration and that he did it. None of the answers the assistant sent says anything about the breach. The four cases that were answered by the assistant are in the hands of a human support person. Sentences support may use are in the support desk by 13:00 on the Tuesday, decided by Ingrid Solheim and checked by Kari Nordmann, and they replace the standing holding message. The questions support could not answer were brought to Kari Nordmann that day.
