# Case 50: Two school owners cannot be reached at the contact the support desk holds

Kind: Missing knowledge

## Situation

A breach of pupil data in the reading app is reported on a Tuesday at 08:00 and Marius Tangen confirms a real breach at 09:00. Twenty-three school owners are affected. Ingrid Solheim decides what the message to them says at 18:00 on the Tuesday, after advice from Kari Nordmann and after asking Silje Haugland how the school owners are reached. When Silje Haugland prepares the messages, the support desk holds a school contact for each of the twenty-three, but for two of them the email bounces back, because the person has left and nobody told the company who took over, and the telephone number in the support desk rings out. The 24 hours run out at 08:00 on the Wednesday.

## Who acts

Head of customer success, Silje Haugland.

## What the person knows

She knows how the school owners are reached, which is her part of this, and that the support desk holds the name, work email address, telephone number and school of the school contact of each of the 310 school owners. She has the message Ingrid Solheim decided and the part about the data that Kari Nordmann wrote. She knows that a school owner is the municipality or the private body that subscribes to the reading app and is the controller for its own pupils, that it is an organisation and not a person, and that a teacher is not a school contact unless they are also named as one. She does not know who at those two school owners has taken over from the person who left, and the support desk cannot tell her.

## What should happen

Silje Haugland sends one message per affected school owner, addressed to that school owner alone, to its school contact, and sends them all at the same time, without undue delay and at the latest 24 hours after the company became aware, so by 08:00 on the Wednesday. No school contact sees the address of another, and no school owner is told before another. The message says what happened, which data and how many pupils are affected, what the company has done and what the school owner should do, with the part about the data written by Kari Nordmann and the wording decided by Ingrid Solheim. The affected school owners are told before the company notifies Datatilsynet, and that is not a choice, because each school owner is the controller for its own pupils and its own 72 hours start when the company tells it, so a message that does not arrive is time taken off that school owner. The specification does not say what is done where the school contact the support desk holds cannot be reached and the company knows no other contact for that school owner: it names the school contact as the person the company writes to and requires the message to the school owner within the 24 hours. The gap is not filled here. A question this process does not answer goes to Kari Nordmann on the three days she works, and Tuesday is one of them, and to Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold on the other days and at weekends; a question nobody above can answer goes to Ingrid Solheim, and to Tomas Berg when she is away; and every such question and the answer given go into the record, so that the next version of this process covers it. What the specification does require runs on: the twenty-three messages go out together inside the 24 hours, the 24 hours on the two that did not arrive keep running and are not stopped by the bounce, and the record holds every message that went out about the breach with the time it was sent and who sent it, and where the same wording went to many people it holds that wording once, how many people it went to, which kinds of person they were and the time it was sent, and not a copy per person.

## Pass

The record shows that one message per affected school owner was sent, each addressed to that school owner alone and to its school contact, all at the same time and by 08:00 on the Wednesday, and that no school contact saw the address of another. The record holds that wording once, with how many school owners it went to and the time it was sent, and names Silje Haugland as the one who sent it. For the two school owners whose school contact could not be reached, the record says that the message did not arrive, what was tried and when, and holds the question Silje Haugland brought to Kari Nordmann on the Tuesday and the answer that was given. No notification went to Datatilsynet before the messages to the school owners went out.
