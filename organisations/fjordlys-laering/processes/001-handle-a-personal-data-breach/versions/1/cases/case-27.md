# Case 27: A school owner asks the company to notify Datatilsynet for it

Kind: Edge of scope

## Situation

A breach of pupil data in the reading app is reported on a Wednesday at 08:00. The eleven affected school owners each receive their own message on the Thursday at 14:00. At 16:30 on the Thursday the school contact of one of them writes back to Silje Haugland: the school owner has nobody who handles personal data, it asks Fjordlys Læring AS to notify Datatilsynet about its pupils on its behalf and to write the letter that goes to the parents, and it asks for an answer tomorrow. Silje Haugland brings the question on the same afternoon.

## Who acts

Data protection officer, Kari Nordmann.

## What the person knows

She has the case, the fact page and the message that went to the school owners, and she keeps the record of the case and the one list of its duties. She knows that the company is the processor for the pupil data of the reading app and that each school owner is the controller for its own pupils, and that Thursday is one of her three working days. She does not know what that school owner will do if the company says no, and the process does not tell her.

## What should happen

The process leaves two things out: the notification to Datatilsynet about the pupils of a school owner, because the school owner is the controller and notifies Datatilsynet itself, and the decision whether the parents of the pupils are told, which the school owner decides and not the company. So the question the school contact asks is outside what this process does, and the process does not say whether the company may do either of them in the school owner's place. The gap is not filled here. A question this process does not answer goes to Kari Nordmann on the three days she works, and Thursday is one of them, and to Henrik Aas on the other days and at weekends; a question none of them can answer goes to Ingrid Solheim, and to Tomas Berg when she is away. The question and the answer that is given go into the record, so that the next version of this process covers it. What the process does require runs on unchanged: every affected school owner was told before any notification to Datatilsynet, the company's own decision on Datatilsynet is only about the data the company is the controller for, and the deadlines on the one list of duties keep running while the question is answered.

## Pass

The record of the case holds the question from the school owner and the answer that was given, with the name of the person who answered it and the time. The answer came from Kari Nordmann, or from Ingrid Solheim where Kari Nordmann could not answer it, and not from Silje Haugland on her own. The company's own notification to Datatilsynet, where it was decided, covers only data the company is the controller for and not that school owner's pupils. Nothing the company sent decided whether the parents of that school owner's pupils are told.
