# Systems

The systems the organisation uses and the personal data each one holds. Write one section per system, with the name of the system as its heading. Say in prose what the system is used for, what personal data it holds and about whom, who is responsible for it and who supplies it.

## Reading app

The reading app is the product Fjordlys Læring AS sells. Pupils in Norwegian primary schools read in it and teachers follow their progress. It holds the first name, last name, class, school, reading level, reading history and recordings of the pupil reading aloud, for about 90,000 pupils between 6 and 13 years old, and the name, work email address and school of about 6,000 teachers. Amina Farah is responsible for it. Fjordlys Læring AS builds it and runs it on Nordsky Cloud.

## Nordsky Cloud

Nordsky Cloud is the cloud platform the reading app runs on. It holds the databases and file storage of the reading app, and the access logs of everyone who logs in to the app or to the platform itself. All pupil and teacher data of the reading app lies here. Marius Tangen is responsible for the platform. Nordsky AS supplies it and runs it in data centres in Oslo and Stavanger.

## AI support assistant

The AI support assistant answers the first message in every support case in the support desk. It reads the case, the help articles and the teacher's school and subscription, and either answers or hands the case to a human. It sees the name, work email address and school of the teacher who wrote in, and the text of the case, which sometimes names a pupil. It never has access to the pupil database. Petter Dahl is responsible for which answers it may send on its own. It runs on Claude from Anthropic, inside the support desk.

## Support desk

The support desk is where support cases from teachers and school owners arrive and are answered. It holds the name, work email address, telephone number and school of about 6,000 teachers and 310 school contacts, the text of every case, and the notes the support team writes. Silje Haugland is responsible for it. Hjelpedesk AS supplies it.

## Payroll system

The payroll system is used to pay salaries. It holds the name, national identity number, bank account number, salary, absence and sick leave of all 48 employees. Tomas Berg is responsible for it. Lønn Norge AS supplies and runs it.

## Hiring system

The hiring system is used to advertise jobs and handle applications. It holds the name, email address, telephone number, CV, references and interview notes of everyone who applies, about 700 applicants a year. Nora Lind is responsible for it. Rekrutt AS supplies it.

## Office suite

The office suite is used for email, documents, spreadsheets and video meetings. It holds the work email of all 48 employees and whatever they write and store in it, which includes documents about pupils, teachers and job applicants. Marius Tangen is responsible for it. Microsoft supplies it as Microsoft 365.

## Contract archive

The contract archive is the folder in the office suite where signed contracts are kept: the subscription contracts with the 310 school owners, the data processor agreements and the employment contracts. It holds the names and work contact details of the people who signed. Tomas Berg is responsible for it. It is part of the office suite.

## Employment files

The employment files are where the company keeps what it holds about each of the 48 employees as an employee: the signed employment contract, the start date and, for someone who has left, the end date, and the person's private email address, private postal address and private telephone number, which is how the company reaches them when they no longer have a work account. They are documents in the office suite, in a folder only Nora Lind, Ingrid Solheim and Tomas Berg can open. Nora Lind keeps them and is responsible for them, and Ingrid Solheim keeps them when she is away. A file is kept for 5 years after the person leaves the company.

## Staff handbook

The staff handbook is where the company's policies and routines are kept, including the on-call rota and the on-call telephone number. It is a document in the office suite that every employee can open, and every employee is given the on-call telephone number on a card when they start, so that it can be reached from a telephone without signing in to anything. Nora Lind is responsible for it.

## Device management

Device management is how the company manages the laptops and work phones of the 48 employees. It runs on Microsoft Intune, which is part of the office suite subscription. It holds the model and serial number of each device, the employee it is given to, when and from where the device last contacted the company, and whether its disk is encrypted and its screen locked. Every company laptop and work phone is encrypted with full-disk encryption and locks its screen after 5 minutes, set from here, and a device can be located, locked and wiped remotely from here. Marius Tangen administers it as the tenant administrator, and Amina Farah when he is away. Microsoft supplies it as part of Microsoft 365.

## Equipment register

The equipment register is the list of which laptop, work phone and other equipment each of the 48 employees has, with the model, the serial number, the date it was given out and the date it was returned. Marius Tangen keeps it in the office suite and is responsible for it. Nora Lind tells him when an employee starts or leaves, and the purchase invoices are kept in the contract archive.
