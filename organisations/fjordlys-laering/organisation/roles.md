# Roles

The roles in the organisation and the people who hold them. Write one section per role, with the name of the role as its heading. Say in prose who holds the role, what the role is responsible for and who steps in when that person is away.

## Managing director

Ingrid Solheim is the managing director of Fjordlys Læring AS. She is responsible for the company as a whole, signs contracts with school owners and is the one who speaks to the press. Head of finance Tomas Berg steps in when she is away.

## Head of product

Jonas Vikane is the head of product. He is responsible for what goes into the reading app, for the release plan and for the two development teams. Lead developer Amina Farah steps in when he is away.

## Head of customer success

Silje Haugland is the head of customer success. She is responsible for the schools that are customers, for onboarding a new school owner and for the support team of five people. Support lead Petter Dahl steps in when she is away.

## Support lead

Petter Dahl is the support lead. He runs the support queue day to day, decides which case is escalated to a developer and owns the answers the AI support assistant is allowed to send on its own. Silje Haugland steps in when he is away.

## Data protection officer

Kari Nordmann is the data protection officer. She advises the company on personal data, keeps the record of processing activities and is the contact for Datatilsynet, the Norwegian data protection authority. She works three days a week. Lawyer Henrik Aas of the external firm Aas & Wold steps in when she is away.

## Head of IT

Marius Tangen is the head of IT. He is responsible for the company's own systems, for access rights, for the logs in the cloud platform and for the security of the reading app in production. Lead developer Amina Farah steps in when he is away.

## Head of finance

Tomas Berg is the head of finance. He is responsible for invoicing the school owners, for payroll and for the contract archive. Ingrid Solheim steps in when he is away.

## Head of people

Nora Lind is the head of people. She is responsible for hiring, for the employment contracts of the 48 employees and for the staff handbook. Ingrid Solheim steps in when she is away.

## On-call developer

The on-call developer is one of the eight developers of the two development teams, on a weekly rota that Amina Farah sets a month ahead and keeps in the staff handbook. The role takes every alert and every report that arrives outside working hours, can restart and roll back the reading app, can revoke an access token or lock an account in Nordsky Cloud, and reaches Marius Tangen or Amina Farah when a decision is needed that the role cannot take. The week beginning 23 November 2026 is held by Erik Moen. The rota always names a second developer as the stand-in for that week; for the week beginning 23 November 2026 that is Lise Berntsen.

## Reachability outside working hours

The two development teams share a weekly on-call rota, and the on-call developer can be reached around the clock on the on-call telephone number in the staff handbook. Marius Tangen can be reached on his mobile around the clock and has agreed to be. Ingrid Solheim can be reached on her mobile around the clock, and Tomas Berg when she cannot. Kari Nordmann works Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday and is not reachable outside those days; Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold can be reached on the firm's emergency number on the other days and at weekends. Silje Haugland and Petter Dahl are reachable in working hours only, Monday to Friday 08:00 to 16:00.

## Lead developer

Amina Farah is the lead developer. She is responsible for the code of the reading app, for the on-call rota of the two development teams and for the pupil data that the app stores. Jonas Vikane steps in when she is away.

## Support team

The support team is the five people who answer teachers and school owners in the support desk: Hanne Riise, Jostein Vold, Maja Eriksen, Sondre Lie and Thea Nygård. They take the cases the AI support assistant hands over and the cases it may not answer, and while a personal data breach is running they answer only with the sentences that stand in the support desk and bring every question they cannot answer to Petter Dahl. Petter Dahl decides day to day who takes which case, and Silje Haugland is responsible for the team. When one of the five is away the other four take the queue; when fewer than two of them are reachable in working hours, Petter Dahl answers the queue himself and Silje Haugland steps in for him when he is away.
