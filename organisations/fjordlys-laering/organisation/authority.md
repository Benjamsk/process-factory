# Authority

Who has the authority to decide what in the organisation. Write one section per kind of decision, with the decision as its heading. Say in prose which role decides, which roles must be asked or told, and who decides when that role is away.

## Notify Datatilsynet of a personal data breach

Ingrid Solheim decides whether a personal data breach is notified to Datatilsynet, after advice from Kari Nordmann. Marius Tangen must be asked what happened in the systems. Tomas Berg decides when Ingrid Solheim is away.

## Tell the school owners and the parents about a breach

Ingrid Solheim decides whether the school owners whose pupils are affected are told, and what the message says, after advice from Kari Nordmann. Silje Haugland must be asked how the school owners are reached and is the one who sends the message. The school owner, not Fjordlys Læring AS, decides whether the parents are told. Tomas Berg decides when Ingrid Solheim is away.

## Take the reading app or a part of it offline

Marius Tangen decides to take the reading app or a part of it offline to stop an ongoing incident. Jonas Vikane and Silje Haugland must be told at once, because teaching stops. Amina Farah decides when Marius Tangen is away.

## Answer a request from a pupil, a parent or a school about personal data

Kari Nordmann decides what the answer to a request about personal data says and whether the request is refused. Amina Farah must be asked to pull the data out of the reading app. Silje Haugland is told so that support knows the case is handled. Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold decides when Kari Nordmann is away.

## Let an AI system send an answer on its own

Petter Dahl decides which answers the AI support assistant may send on its own, within the AI use policy. Kari Nordmann must be asked when the answer touches personal data. Silje Haugland decides when Petter Dahl is away.

## Give a school owner a refund or a price reduction

Tomas Berg decides a refund or a price reduction up to 50,000 kroner, after being asked by Silje Haugland. Above that amount Ingrid Solheim decides. Ingrid Solheim decides when Tomas Berg is away.

## Sign or change a data processor agreement

Ingrid Solheim signs a data processor agreement, after advice from Kari Nordmann on what the agreement must say. The role responsible for the system must be asked. Tomas Berg signs when Ingrid Solheim is away.

## Lock or wipe a lost device remotely

Marius Tangen decides to locate, lock or wipe a company laptop or work phone remotely. Kari Nordmann must be asked when the device held personal data, and is told in every case. The employee who lost the device is told before it is wiped where they can be reached, and the wipe is not held back for longer than an hour because they cannot be. Amina Farah decides when Marius Tangen is away. Outside working hours the on-call developer may locate and lock a device at once without asking anyone, and tells Marius Tangen what was done.

## Block an employee's accounts and access

Marius Tangen decides to end an employee's sessions, change their password and revoke their access tokens in the office suite, Nordsky Cloud and the support desk. Nora Lind must be told, because the employee cannot work until it is undone. Amina Farah decides when he is away. Outside working hours the on-call developer does it in Nordsky Cloud at once and reaches Marius Tangen or Amina Farah for the other systems.
