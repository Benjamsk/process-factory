# gdpr review of draft-8.md

Verdict: CHANGES

- [medium] Step 30: the rule that absence and sick leave are written only as the categories, with the number of employees affected and nothing about any individual, is addressed to the data protection officer alone, but that health data enters the case in step 27, where the head of finance establishes the facts in the payroll system and gives them to the head of IT, and in steps 26 and 28, where the head of IT, the lead developer or the on-call developer writes the fact page; as the draft stands none of them is told to limit what they pass on and write to the two categories and the number, which Articles 5 and 9 require for a special category of data. The draft must put the limit on the head of finance in step 27 and on whoever writes the fact page in steps 26 to 28, not only on the data protection officer in step 30.
- [medium] Steps 38 and 43 turn on what the fact page shows about the risk for the people affected, that the breach is unlikely to result in a risk or that it is a high risk, but step 28, which lists what the fact page holds, has no item for the level of risk, and no step of the draft says who judges how large the risk is and writes that judgement on the page; Articles 33 and 34 make that judgement the thing that decides whether Datatilsynet is notified and whether the people are told. The draft must say, in step 28 or 29, that the fact page states for every kind of person affected whether the breach is unlikely to result in a risk or is a high risk, and who states it on whose advice. This is not in `spec.md` either: its step 28 lists the same items and its steps 38 and 43 read the same way, so the draft writer cannot add it without the specification saying it.
- [low] Steps 39 and 40 say that the data protection officer writes the notification from the fact page but not what the notification must contain, while step 45 does say it for the message to the people affected; Article 33 requires the notification to give the nature of the breach with the categories and the approximate number of people and of records concerned, the name and contact details of the data protection officer, the likely consequences and the measures taken or proposed, and of these the fact page carries everything but the name and contact details of the data protection officer and the number of records. The draft would be better if step 39 listed what the notification must contain, as step 45 does for the message to the people. This is not in `spec.md` either.
