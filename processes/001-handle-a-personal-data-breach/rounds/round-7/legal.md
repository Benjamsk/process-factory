Verdict: CHANGES

- [high] Step 30: the rule that absence and sick leave are named only as the categories absence and sick leave, with the number of employees affected and nothing about any individual's health, diagnosis or reason for absence, is addressed to the data protection officer alone, while the head of finance establishes the payroll facts and gives them to the head of IT in step 27 and the head of IT writes the fact page in step 28 without being bound by it; the draft must put that restriction on whoever establishes the facts about the payroll system and whoever writes the fact page, not on the data protection officer alone. - [high] Step 9: taking the reading app or a part of it offline carries no time, and it is the step that stops the breach where closing the access, revoking the token and locking the account do not fit, so in that case no step says when the breach must be stopped; step 9 must say within 4 hours of the report, as steps 6 and 8 do. Rule: the deadline "The logs secured and the breach stopped: within 4 hours" of the section `Deadlines` of `spec.md`.
- [medium] Steps 39 and 40: the notification to Datatilsynet is written from the fact page, and neither those steps nor the fact page in step 28 requires it to give the name and contact details of the data protection officer, which a notification must contain; the step must say that the notification names the data protection officer with her contact details, and Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold when she is away, as the message to the people affected does in step 45. `spec.md` does not say it either. Rule: GDPR Article 33(3)(b), as the section `Legal basis` of `spec.md` names Article 33.
