# gdpr review of draft-14.md

Verdict: CHANGES

- [high] Steps 48 to 50, and the two situations in `When this applies`: the affected school owners are told only for "a breach of pupil data in the reading app", so a breach of the pupil data the company holds elsewhere, such as the documents about pupils that `organisation/systems.md` says the office suite holds, reaches no school owner at all, although Article 33(2) obliges the company as processor to tell the controller of every breach of the pupil data it processes for it; steps 48 to 50 must cover a breach of pupil data in any system the process covers, and `spec.md` narrows them to the reading app in the same way, so this is missing from `spec.md` too.
- [medium] Step 29: the AI support assistant reads every support case, including "every case that comes from a parent", and so gives Anthropic the name and email address of a parent, while the Anthropic agreement in `organisation/processors.md` names only the text of the support case and the name, work email address and school of the teacher who wrote in (Article 28); the draft must have a case from a parent handed to a human support person without the assistant reading it, and `spec.md` does not cover this either.
- [low] Step 5: the reporter is told what the record holds about them, why, how long it is kept and whom to ask only "where that person is outside the company", so an employee who reports a suspected incident, whose name and account of it go into the record under step 6, is told none of it (Articles 13 and 14); the draft must say that the same four things are said to a reporter inside the company, and `spec.md` says them only for a reporter outside the company.
