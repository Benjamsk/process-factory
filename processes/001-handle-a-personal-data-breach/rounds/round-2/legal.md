# legal review of draft-2.md

Verdict: CHANGES

- [high] Step 17 and the duty to Datatilsynet in `Who must be told`: the draft lets Ingrid Solheim decide whether Datatilsynet is notified and gives no standard for that decision, so a reader may take a free "no"; the step must say that where the company is the controller Datatilsynet is notified unless the breach is unlikely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of the people affected (GDPR article 33), and that only that ground carries a "no". `spec.md` does not say it either. Rule: The GDPR, as the section `Legal basis` of `spec.md` names it.
- [high] Step 19 and the duty to the people affected in `Who must be told`: the draft makes telling the affected people a free decision by Ingrid Solheim; the step must say that where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of those people they are told without undue delay (GDPR article 34), so the decision cannot be a "no" in that case. `spec.md`, whose `Legal basis` says only that the company must consider telling the people affected, does not say it either. Rule: The GDPR, as the section `Legal basis` of `spec.md` names it.
