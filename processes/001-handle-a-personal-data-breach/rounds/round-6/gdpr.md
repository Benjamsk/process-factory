# gdpr review of draft-6.md

Verdict: CHANGES

- [medium] Step 3 (secure the logs): the step tells the head of IT to export and copy the Nordsky Cloud access logs, which hold the names of about 90,000 pupils and about 6,000 teachers, out of the platform, but never says where the copy goes, so an employee who follows it may put it in e-mail, on paper or on a private laptop or USB stick; Article 5 requires the copy to stay in a company system with access limited to those who need it, and the draft must say that the copy is kept in such a system and is not sent by e-mail, printed or put on a private device. spec.md does not name a place for the secured copy either; it only says, in step 24 and in `Records`, that the logs are stored where the data protection officer can find them again and that the record says where they are kept.
- [medium] Step 15 (put all of this on the fact page): the step makes the lead developer the one who writes the whole fact page, including the absence and sick leave of the 48 employees from the payroll system, which is special category health data under Article 9; she is responsible for the reading app and its pupil data and has no part in the payroll system, so giving her that data breaks the data minimisation rule of Article 5 that a step gives personal data only to those who need it for the step. The draft must limit the lead developer's work on the fact page to what spec.md gives her, the Nordsky Cloud access logs, which pupils and which school owners are affected and the time window, and leave the employee part to the head of IT who owns the fact page, with the data protection officer deciding what is written. spec.md does not say who fills in the employee part of the fact page.
- [low] `Who must be told`, Datatilsynet: the entry gives only the outer limit, at the latest 72 hours after the company became aware, while Article 33 requires the notification without undue delay and at the latest 72 hours; the draft uses "without undue delay" for the school owners and for the people affected and should use it here as well.
