# readability review of draft-6.md

Verdict: CHANGES

- [high] Steps 25 and 26: step 25 cannot be done until the list of measures of step 26 exists, because the fifth of its six conditions is that "the list of measures exists with one named person per measure", so the reader who does the steps in their order closes the case before reaching the step that creates the list. Put the step that creates the list of measures with its named persons before the step that closes the case.
- [medium] Step 2: "have the case within 1 hour of the report" is a result and not an action the head of IT can do; the only actions in the step belong to other people (whoever received the report forwards it and telephones him, the on-call developer takes it and wakes him). Name an action for the head of IT in the step, and put the action of whoever receives the report in a step of its own, with the role and the name first.
- [medium] Steps 1, 4 and 11: each asks for more than one action. Step 1: keep the standing configuration ready, keep the standing holding message ready, and review both every six months. Step 4: close the access, revoke the token, lock the account, with no word on whether the reader does all three or only the one that fits the breach. Step 11: switch the AI support assistant into hand-over, put the standing holding message into the support desk, and write down when it was done. Make one step per action, and say for step 4 which of the three the reader does.
- [medium] "When this applies", second bullet of "How a report reaches the head of IT": Nordsky AS, Hjelpedesk AS, Lønn Norge AS, Aas & Wold, Anthropic, Rekrutt AS and Microsoft are named without saying which system each of them supplies, so the reader who receives a report from one of them cannot tell which system and whose data it concerns. Name the system next to each supplier; the organisation files name the system of each.
- [medium] Step 7 against steps 20 and 22 and the first two bullets under "Who must be told" outside the company: the moment the deadlines run from has two names. "When this applies" and step 7 say the clock and the 72 hours start when the report reached the company, while step 20 says "24 hours after the company became aware", step 22 says "72 hours after the company became aware" and the two bullets repeat that, and the draft never says whether these are the same moment. Use one name for that moment throughout, or say plainly how the two differ.
- [medium] Step 10: "support is closed on the subject of the breach" is used before the draft says what closing support means; only step 11 says it means switching the AI support assistant into hand-over and putting the standing holding message in, and step 12 says the assistant keeps answering ordinary cases. The draft also uses "close" for four other things: close the access (step 4), close a case that is not a breach (step 9), close a case about a real breach (step 25) and close the measures (step 26). The reader can take step 10 to mean that support stops answering. Say in step 10 what closing support on the subject means.
- [medium] Step 12: "on the ordinary signs" is used as if the reader already knows what the ordinary signs are, and the list that follows, "words such as leak, hacked, data, privacy or Datatilsynet, and a complaining parent", is left open by "such as", so neither the support lead who sets the hand-over up nor the support person reading the step can tell which cases are handed over. Say which signs make the assistant hand a case over; spec.md does not list them either.
- [medium] Step 27 and "What to record", first paragraph: "the record of processing activities" is used without being explained, next to "the record of the case", which is a different document, so the reader cannot tell the two apart. Say what the record of processing activities is; spec.md calls it the list of the company's processing activities that Kari Nordmann keeps.
- [low] Steps 15, 21 and 23: the references to the GDPR, Article 9(2)(b) together with Article 6(1)(c), Article 33 and Article 34, give the legal reason and no action, and the reader does not need them in order to act.
- [low] Steps 16, 20 and 23: the duty is written as a state and not as an instruction to the person who has to do it: "They stand in the support desk as actual sentences within 4 hours of the report", "Every affected school owner is told", "those people are told". Write them as instructions to the named person who acts.
- [low] Step 8 and the third bullet under "Who must be told" inside the company: "in the same step and at the same time" points at the structure of the document rather than telling the reader anything; "at the same time" alone says what has to happen.
