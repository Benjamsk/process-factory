# Answers 4

## 1. Who decides that a case about a real personal data breach is finished and closed, and what must be in place before it can be closed, given that the dates of the measures are set within two weeks of the case being closed?

I decide, after Kari says the record is complete. Marius cannot close his own case and neither can Kari close it alone - she holds the record, I carry the consequence. Before it can be closed, six things must be in place: the breach is actually stopped and Marius has said so in writing; the fact page is final, including the parts we could not establish, written as not established rather than left blank; the decision on Datatilsynet is in the record with its reason and my name, whether the answer was yes or no, and the notification sent if it was yes; every affected school owner has been told, and every affected teacher, employee or applicant we decided to tell; the list of measures exists with one named person per measure; and the secured logs are stored where Kari can find them again. The dates on the measures come within two weeks after that, so closing the case is not the end of the measures - and to be clear, I close the case, Marius closes the measures, and Kari tells me when a measure is overdue. The case cannot be closed inside 72 hours of the report even if everything is done, because that is roughly how long it takes for the thing we missed to show up.

## 2. Who writes the notification to Datatilsynet and sends it once Ingrid Solheim has decided that it goes, and who approves it before it is sent?

Kari Nordmann writes it, from the fact page, and sends it through Datatilsynet's own reporting form. I approve it before it is sent and nobody else needs to; Marius reads it first for whether the technical description is actually true, because Kari writing "the token was exposed" when it was something else would be worse than being late. When Kari is away Henrik Aas writes and sends it, and I still approve. The sent notification, the time it was sent and the receipt from Datatilsynet go into the record. If we later learn something that changes the picture, Kari sends a supplementary notification without asking me again - we should never be in a position where a correction waits for my calendar.
