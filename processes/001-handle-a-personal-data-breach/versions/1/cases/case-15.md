# Case 15: The logs say something else after the notification was sent

Kind: Missing knowledge

## Situation

An unexplained access in the Nordsky Cloud access logs is reported on a Monday at 08:00. The fact page says that one table with the names, work email addresses and schools of 300 teachers was downloaded in a window of 40 minutes. Ingrid Solheim decided that Datatilsynet is notified, and Kari Nordmann sent the notification on the Wednesday at 18:00. On the Thursday at 09:00 Amina Farah finds in the secured copy of the logs that a second access token was used for three days and that the download covered all 6,000 teachers, not 300. The case is not closed.

## Who acts

Data protection officer, Kari Nordmann.

## What the person knows

She knows what the notification she sent on the Wednesday says, and she now has what Amina Farah found in the secured logs: a three-day window and 6,000 teachers. She keeps the record and the one list of duties of the case, so she knows what has gone out and when. She does not know whether the teacher data was used for anything after the download, and the logs cannot tell her that.

## What should happen

The data is teacher data, which the company is the controller for. When the company later learns something that changes the picture, Kari Nordmann sends a supplementary notification to Datatilsynet without asking Ingrid Solheim again, so no new decision from Ingrid Solheim is waited for. The sent notification, the time it was sent and the receipt from Datatilsynet go into the record, and the fact page carries the window and the number of people affected that the logs now show, with what still cannot be established written as not established and a consequence that is not established counting as a high risk until it is established. Where the fact page now shows that the breach is likely to be a high risk for the teachers, those teachers are told, and the only thing decided is the wording and the way they are reached: Ingrid Solheim decides after advice from Kari Nordmann, Tomas Berg when she is away, Silje Haugland tells the teachers, and Kari Nordmann writes the part of the message that describes the data and what the person can do. That message goes out within 24 hours of Ingrid Solheim's decision and the teachers are told without undue delay and at the latest 72 hours after the fact page shows the high risk, and the decision goes into the record with its reason, the name of the person who took it and the time, whether the answer is yes or no. The fact page is not final, and the case is not closed until Kari Nordmann says the record is complete and Ingrid Solheim closes it, which cannot be inside 72 hours of the report.

## Pass

Kari Nordmann sent a supplementary notification to Datatilsynet after 09:00 on the Thursday, without a new decision from Ingrid Solheim, and the record holds it, the time it was sent and the receipt, next to the first notification and its receipt. The fact page now states the three-day window and 6,000 affected teachers, and what still could not be established is written as not established. The record holds the decision whether the teachers are told, with its reason, the name of the person who took it and the time. If they are told, the message went out within 24 hours of that decision and at the latest 72 hours after the fact page showed the high risk, Silje Haugland sent it, and the part about the data and what the person can do was written by Kari Nordmann. The case was not closed before Kari Nordmann said the record was complete.
