# Case 17: Hjelpedesk AS reports 46 hours after it became aware

Kind: Late or wrong notice

## Situation

On a Thursday at 11:00 Hjelpedesk AS sends an email to Silje Haugland: an export of the support desk contact list lay open on the internet, and it holds the name, work email address, telephone number and school of about 6,000 teachers and 310 school contacts. The email says that Hjelpedesk AS became aware of it on the Tuesday at 13:00, 46 hours earlier, which is inside the 48 hours its agreement allows. The export holds no case text and no pupil data. Silje Haugland is at work.

## Who acts

Head of customer success, Silje Haugland.

## What the person knows

She knows what Hjelpedesk AS wrote, that it is the processor for the support desk and that the agreement obliges it to report a breach within 48 hours, which it has done with two hours to spare. She is responsible for the support desk, so she knows what the contact list holds and how teachers and school contacts are reached. She does not know how long the export lay open, whether anyone downloaded it or which teachers and school contacts are in that export; Hjelpedesk AS and the work on the fact page have to establish that.

## What should happen

Whoever in the company receives a report from a processor forwards it to Marius Tangen and telephones him, so Silje Haugland does both. The clock of the process starts when the report reached the company at 11:00 on the Thursday, and Marius Tangen has the case within 1 hour. He stops the breach within 4 hours, writes in the record the time of the report and who reported it, and tells Kari Nordmann and Ingrid Solheim within 4 hours; Nora Lind is not told in that step, because no employee or applicant data is touched. The data is support desk data, which the company is the controller for, and the fact page says that for each kind of data within 24 hours of the report. Teachers and school contacts are affected, so Marius Tangen closes support on the subject: in working hours Petter Dahl switches the AI support assistant into hand-over for breach cases and puts the standing holding message into the support desk and writes down when, the assistant keeps answering ordinary cases and says nothing about the breach, and the sentences support may use stand in the support desk within 4 hours of the report, decided by Ingrid Solheim and checked by Kari Nordmann, after which Petter Dahl replaces the holding message with them. Ingrid Solheim decides at the latest 48 hours after the report whether Datatilsynet is notified, after advice from Kari Nordmann and after asking Marius Tangen what happened in the systems, and the notification goes at the latest 72 hours after the company became aware. Where the fact page shows that the breach is likely to be a high risk for the teachers and the school contacts, they are told, and only the wording and the way they are reached are decided: Ingrid Solheim decides after advice from Kari Nordmann, Silje Haugland tells the teachers and the school contacts, and Kari Nordmann writes the part that describes the data and what the person can do, within 24 hours of the decision and at the latest 72 hours after the fact page shows the high risk. No school owner is told under the data processing annex, because no pupil data of the reading app is involved.

## Pass

The record names 11:00 on the Thursday as the time of the report and Hjelpedesk AS as the one who reported it, and Silje Haugland both forwarded the email and telephoned Marius Tangen, who had the case by 12:00. Kari Nordmann and Ingrid Solheim had the case by 15:00, and sentences support may use stood in the support desk by 15:00. The record says when the AI support assistant was switched into hand-over and who did it, and no answer the assistant sent says anything about the breach. The fact page exists within 24 hours of 11:00 and says that the company is the controller for the support desk data. Ingrid Solheim's decision on Datatilsynet is in the record with its reason, her name and the time by 11:00 on the Saturday, and if the answer was yes the notification was sent by 11:00 on the Sunday. The record holds the decision whether the teachers and the school contacts are told, and if yes, Silje Haugland sent them the message within 24 hours of that decision, with the part about the data written by Kari Nordmann. No message went to a school owner.
