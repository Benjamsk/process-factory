# Case 1: Nordsky AS reports an open storage bucket

Kind: Ordinary path

## Situation

On a Tuesday at 09:10 Nordsky AS telephones Marius Tangen and sends an email: a storage configuration on Nordsky Cloud let the read-aloud recordings of a group of pupils be downloaded without logging in, over a window of three days that ended the night before. The pupils all belong to two of the 310 school owners. No teacher, employee or applicant data is involved. Everyone who holds a role in the process is at work.

## Who acts

Head of IT, Marius Tangen.

## What the person knows

He knows what Nordsky AS told him: which storage held the recordings, that the window was three days and that it is now closed. He knows the recordings belong to pupils of the reading app, so the company is the processor and the school owners are the controllers. He does not yet know how many pupils, which school owners they belong to or whether anyone outside actually downloaded a recording; the Nordsky Cloud access logs have to be read for that.

## What should happen

Marius Tangen has the case within 1 hour of the report, so by 10:10. He exports and copies the Nordsky Cloud access logs out of the normal retention before anything is deleted or fixed in production, and the logs are secured and the breach stopped within 4 hours. He writes down the time of the report and who reported it. Kari Nordmann and Ingrid Solheim have the case within 4 hours. Marius Tangen decides that this is a real breach, and because pupils and school owners are affected he switches support closed to the subject: the AI support assistant is put into hand-over for breach cases and the standing holding message goes into the support desk, Silje Haugland and Petter Dahl are telephoned at once, and the sentences support may use are actual sentences in the support desk within 4 hours of the report, with Ingrid Solheim deciding the wording and Kari Nordmann checking it. Marius Tangen owns and signs off the facts on one page within 24 hours, with Amina Farah doing the work in the systems, and the page is not finished until Kari Nordmann says she has what she needs. Kari Nordmann keeps one list of duties for the case. Both affected school owners are told within 24 hours of the company becoming aware, at the same time and before Datatilsynet is notified; Ingrid Solheim decides what the message says, Kari Nordmann writes the part about the data and Silje Haugland sends it. The school owners then notify Datatilsynet on their own 72 hours and decide whether the parents are told. Marius Tangen draws up the list of measures with one named person and one date for each. Ingrid Solheim closes the case after Kari Nordmann says the record is complete, and not inside 72 hours of the report.

## Pass

Marius Tangen had the case by 10:10 and the record says so. The secured copy of the Nordsky Cloud access logs exists and is stored where Kari Nordmann can find it, and it was taken before any fix went to production. Kari Nordmann and Ingrid Solheim had the case by 13:10. Sentences support may use were in the support desk by 13:10. The fact page was signed off by Marius Tangen within 24 hours and says, for each kind of data, that the company is the processor. Both school owners received the same message within 24 hours of 09:10 and before any notification to Datatilsynet, and it says what happened, which data and how many pupils are affected, what the company has done and what the school owner should do. The record holds the timeline with real timestamps, and Ingrid Solheim closed the case no earlier than 72 hours after 09:10.
