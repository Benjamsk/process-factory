# Case 21: The one person free to read the secured logs may not open the folder

Kind: Conflicting rules

## Situation

On Saturday 28 November 2026 at 01:20 Nordsky AS telephones the on-call number: an access token of the reading app has been used from outside the company. Erik Moen, who is on call that week, exports the Nordsky Cloud access logs and copies them out of the normal 90-day retention, and starts the fact page with what he knows. Marius Tangen is woken, makes the folder for the case in the office suite on the Saturday morning and sets the rights to it. At 11:00 the fact page needs the secured logs read line by line to say which pupils and which school owners are affected, and the only person free to do that work is Erik Moen, who is not one of the five people who may open the folder. Amina Farah is at a wedding and unreachable until the Sunday.

## Who acts

Head of IT, Marius Tangen.

## What the person knows

He has the report from Nordsky AS, the secured copy Erik Moen took and the fact page Erik Moen started. He knows that the secured copy holds the personal data of pupils and teachers, that he gives the right to open the folder to himself, Amina Farah, Kari Nordmann, Henrik Aas while he stands in for her, and Ingrid Solheim and to nobody else, and that the copy is never sent by email, never printed and never put on a private device, a private account or a memory stick. He knows that Saturday is not one of Kari Nordmann's working days. He does not yet know what the logs show, how many pupils are affected or which school owners they belong to; that is the work the logs have to be read for.

## What should happen

Two rules of the specification pull in different directions. Outside working hours the on-call developer secures the logs and starts the fact page, and the fact page is finished within 24 hours of the report; at the same time only those five people may open the folder, and nobody else is given access without the agreement of the data protection officer. The specification settles the part that matters most: Erik Moen is not simply given the folder, and the copy is not emailed to him, printed for him or put on a memory stick, whatever the hurry. Access for anyone beyond the five needs the agreement of the data protection officer, and the record of the case holds that Marius Tangen set the access rights to the folder and holds the agreement of the data protection officer where anyone else was given access. The specification does not say who gives that agreement on a day Kari Nordmann does not work: it gives Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold her decisions on whether a case is a breach, her advice and the notification to Datatilsynet, and says nothing about this agreement. The gap is not filled here. What is still required is that the fact page is finished within 24 hours of 01:20, that it is not finished until the data protection officer says she has what she needs, that what cannot be established is written as not established rather than left blank, and that the secured copy stays where the data protection officer can find it again.

## Pass

The folder for the case exists in the office suite beside the record, and the record says that Marius Tangen set its rights. Only Marius Tangen, Amina Farah, Kari Nordmann, Henrik Aas and Ingrid Solheim could open it, unless the record holds the agreement of the data protection officer for someone else, naming who was given access, by whom and when. The secured copy was not sent by email, not printed and not put on a private device, a private account or a memory stick, and it is stored where the data protection officer can find it again. A fact page exists within 24 hours of 01:20 on the Saturday, and every fact the logs could not establish is written on it as not established, with no blank field.
