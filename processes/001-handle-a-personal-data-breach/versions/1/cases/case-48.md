# Case 48: Everything is done on the second day and the managing director leaves

Kind: Person away

## Situation

On a Monday at 08:00 Nordsky AS reports that an export holding the names, work email addresses and schools of 300 teachers at twelve schools lay open on the internet for two hours on the Sunday. No pupil, employee or applicant data is involved. Marius Tangen stops it the same morning and says in writing that it is stopped, the fact page is final on the Tuesday at 09:00, Ingrid Solheim decided on the Monday at 16:00 that Datatilsynet is notified and Kari Nordmann sent the notification on the Tuesday at 11:00, the affected teachers were told on the Tuesday at 14:00, and the list of measures with one named person per measure exists on the Tuesday at 15:00. Kari Nordmann says on the Tuesday at 16:00 that the record is complete. Ingrid Solheim leaves on the Wednesday at 06:00 for a week's holiday and is not reachable.

## Who acts

Head of finance, Tomas Berg.

## What the person knows

He knows that Kari Nordmann has said the record is complete and what stands in it, and he steps in for the managing director when she is away. He knows the report reached the company at 08:00 on the Monday. He does not know anything more about the systems than the fact page says.

## What should happen

Ingrid Solheim closes the case, after Kari Nordmann says the record is complete, and Tomas Berg closes it when Ingrid Solheim is away, so the case is his to close while she is on holiday; Marius Tangen cannot close his own case and Kari Nordmann cannot close it alone. The case is not closed inside 72 hours of the report even where everything is done, because that is roughly how long it takes for the thing the company missed to show up, so it is not closed before 08:00 on the Thursday however little is left to do. Before it can be closed, six things are in place: the breach is stopped and Marius Tangen has said so in writing; the fact page is final, including the parts that could not be established, written as not established rather than left blank; the decision on Datatilsynet is in the record with its reason and the name of the person who took it, whether the answer was yes or no, and the notification is sent where it was yes; every affected school owner has been told, and every affected teacher, school contact, employee and job applicant the company decided to tell, and here no pupil data of the reading app was touched, so no school owner is affected and the affected teachers are the people told; the list of measures exists with one named person per measure; and the secured logs are stored where Kari Nordmann can find them again. After the case is closed, Marius Tangen sets the date on each measure within 2 weeks of the case being closed and no date is left open; the named person has their measure in place within 30 days of the case being closed where it could let the same breach happen again and within 90 days otherwise, and Marius Tangen closes the measures; Kari Nordmann checks the list at her six-monthly review of the record of processing activities and tells Ingrid Solheim what is overdue, and an overdue measure goes on the agenda of the Monday leadership meeting until it is done. The record is kept five years from the day the case is closed, and Marius Tangen deletes the secured copies of the Nordsky Cloud access logs 12 months after the day the case is closed, longer only while a case about the breach is still open and then with the reason in the record.

## Pass

The case was not closed before 08:00 on the Thursday, and the record names Tomas Berg as the one who closed it, with the time, and shows that Kari Nordmann had said the record was complete before he did; neither Marius Tangen nor Kari Nordmann closed it. The record holds all six things at the moment of closing: Marius Tangen's written word that the breach is stopped, the final fact page with anything unestablished written as not established, Ingrid Solheim's decision on Datatilsynet with its reason, her name and the time and the sent notification with its receipt, the message that went to the 300 affected teachers with the time it was sent, the list of measures with one named person per measure, and where the secured logs are kept. Every measure had a date written down within 2 weeks of the day the case was closed, with no date left open.
