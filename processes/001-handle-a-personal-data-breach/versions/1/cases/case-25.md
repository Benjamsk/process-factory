# Case 25: The facts about the hiring system while the head of people is off sick

Kind: Person away

## Situation

On a Monday at 09:00 the hiring system shows that a link to an applicant's file worked without logging in and was shared outside the company. Marius Tangen has the case at 09:30, and at 10:00 he asks the person responsible for each affected system for the facts in that system. Nora Lind went off sick on the Friday and is not reachable. No pupil, teacher or support desk data is affected.

## Who acts

Managing director, Ingrid Solheim.

## What the person knows

She knows what Marius Tangen asked her for and that she steps in for Nora Lind while she is away. She knows that the hiring system holds the name, email address, telephone number, CV, references and interview notes of about 700 applicants a year and that Rekrutt AS supplies it. She does not know how many applicants' files could be reached by such a link or whether anyone outside opened one; Rekrutt AS and the work in the system have to establish that.

## What should happen

The head of people establishes the facts about the hiring system and gives them to the head of IT within 12 hours of being asked, which is by 22:00 on the Monday, and where that person is away the person who steps in for her does it, which is Ingrid Solheim. Marius Tangen owns the fact page and signs it off, and it is finished within 24 hours of the report, which is by 09:00 on the Tuesday, and it is not finished until Kari Nordmann says she has what she needs for her advice; what cannot be established is written as not established and why, rather than left blank, and counts as a high risk until it is established. Marius Tangen tells Kari Nordmann and Ingrid Solheim within 4 hours of the report, and at the same time he tells the head of people, because the breach touches the hiring system, so that part also comes to Ingrid Solheim while Nora Lind is away. The head of people's other parts fall to her too: she prepares what managers may say to staff who ask, as sentences and not as improvisation, and she is the one who tells the affected applicants where the fact page shows a high risk for them. The company is the controller for applicant data, so she also decides, after advice from Kari Nordmann and after asking Marius Tangen what happened in the systems, whether Datatilsynet is notified, at the latest 48 hours after the report, and whether the applicants are told, and that second decision is only about the wording and the way they are reached. Support is not closed on the subject, because only job applicants are affected. The message to an applicant holds no personal data of anyone other than the person it is sent to, carries the date it was sent and goes to each person separately.

## Pass

Marius Tangen asked for the facts about the hiring system at 10:00 on the Monday, and they reached him from Ingrid Solheim by 22:00 the same day, and the record shows that she gave them because Nora Lind was away. The fact page was signed off by Marius Tangen by 09:00 on the Tuesday, says that the company is the controller for the applicant data, and writes what could not be established as not established, with no blank field. The record shows that Marius Tangen told Kari Nordmann and the head of people within 4 hours of 09:00 on the Monday. Sentences for managers exist in writing before any manager answers a member of staff. Ingrid Solheim's decision on Datatilsynet stands in the record with its reason, her name and the time by 09:00 on the Wednesday. No holding message went into the support desk and the AI support assistant was not switched into hand-over. If the applicants were told, each one received a message of their own, carrying the date it was sent and holding no personal data of any other applicant, within 24 hours of Ingrid Solheim's decision, with the part about the data and what the person can do written by Kari Nordmann.
