# Case 24: Aas & Wold reports a breach of its own

Kind: Edge of scope

## Situation

On a Tuesday at 09:00 Aas & Wold telephones Marius Tangen and sends an email: an outsider reached the firm's document system for two days last week, and among the files were the record and the fact page of a case Henrik Aas stood in on for Fjordlys Læring AS in September. Those files name pupils of two school owners, with the recordings they had made, and name teachers with their work email addresses. The stand-in on that September case ended when it was closed. Aas & Wold says it became aware of its own breach the day before at 14:00.

## Who acts

Head of IT, Marius Tangen.

## What the person knows

He has what Aas & Wold told him. He knows that Aas & Wold is a processor of the company for the data protection officer stand-in service, that its agreement obliges it to report a breach of its own within 24 hours of becoming aware, and that it reported 19 hours after it became aware. He knows that the files hold pupil data of the reading app, where the company is the processor and the school owners are the controllers, and teacher data, where the company is the controller itself. He knows that Tuesday is one of Kari Nordmann's three working days. He does not know what the outsider read or copied, and he has no logs of his own for the firm's document system.

## What should happen

A processor that reports a breach of the service it supplies starts this process, and Aas & Wold is named as one of them, so the report ends up with Marius Tangen like every other report and the clock starts at 09:00 on the Tuesday, when the report reached the company. He has the case within 1 hour and tells Kari Nordmann and Ingrid Solheim within 4 hours; Tuesday is a day Kari Nordmann works, and it is her own stand-in firm that had the breach. She decides whether the case is a personal data breach, keeps the record and keeps one list of the duties of the case, with the pupil side and the controller side on the one list and the earliest deadline binding the case. The specification names the systems the process covers, the reading app, Nordsky Cloud, the support desk, the AI support assistant, the payroll system, the hiring system, the office suite and the contract archive, and the step that secures the logs is about the Nordsky Cloud access logs; it does not say what is secured when the breach is in a system that a processor runs and the company has no logs of its own. The gap is not filled here. What the specification does require is that the fact page is finished within 24 hours of 09:00 and says for each kind of data whether the company is the controller or the processor, what cannot be established being written as not established rather than left blank; that every affected school owner is told within 24 hours of 09:00, at the same time and before any notification to Datatilsynet, with Ingrid Solheim deciding the message, Kari Nordmann writing the part about the data and Silje Haugland sending it; and that for the teacher data Ingrid Solheim decides within 48 hours whether Datatilsynet is notified, after advice from Kari Nordmann and after asking Marius Tangen what happened in the systems, with the notification at the latest 72 hours after the report. The agreement obliges Aas & Wold to delete or return the case data when the stand-in on that case ends, and the stand-in on the September case had ended.

## Pass

The record names 09:00 on the Tuesday as the time of the report and Aas & Wold as the one who reported it. Marius Tangen had the case by 10:00, and Kari Nordmann and Ingrid Solheim had it by 13:00. One record and one timeline exist for the case, with one list of duties that holds the pupil side and the teacher side together and names the earliest deadline as the one that binds. The fact page exists within 24 hours of 09:00 and says, for each kind of data in the files, whether the company is the controller or the processor, with what could not be established written as not established. Both affected school owners received their own message by 09:00 on the Wednesday, at the same time as each other and before any notification to Datatilsynet. Ingrid Solheim's decision on Datatilsynet for the teacher data stands in the record with its reason, her name and the time by 09:00 on the Thursday. The record says whether the case data of the September case had been deleted or returned when the stand-in on that case ended.
