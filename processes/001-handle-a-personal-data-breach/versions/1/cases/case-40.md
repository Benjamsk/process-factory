# Case 40: The approved sentences are ready on a Saturday and support is not

Kind: Outside working hours

## Situation

On Saturday 28 November 2026 at 13:00 Nordsky AS telephones the on-call number: an export of the reading app with the names, classes and reading levels of pupils at 60 school owners lay open on the internet. Erik Moen takes the report and wakes Marius Tangen, who confirms a real breach at 13:40. Erik Moen switches on the standing configuration and puts the standing holding message into the support desk at 13:50, and telephones Silje Haugland and Petter Dahl at home, who both answer. Ingrid Solheim decides the sentences support may use at 15:30 and Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold checks them, because Saturday is not one of Kari Nordmann's working days. The 4 hours for those sentences to stand in the support desk run out at 17:00 on the Saturday, and Silje Haugland and Petter Dahl are reachable Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 16:00.

## Who acts

Support lead, Petter Dahl.

## What the person knows

He knows from Erik Moen's telephone call that the breach is confirmed, that the standing configuration was switched on and the standing holding message, approved in advance by Ingrid Solheim and Kari Nordmann, was put into the support desk at 13:50. He has the sentences Ingrid Solheim decided at 15:30 and Henrik Aas checked, and he knows that he is reachable in working hours only and that the next working hours begin at 08:00 on the Monday. He does not know what the fact page will say about how many pupils are affected, and he may say nothing about the breach beyond the sentences support is given.

## What should happen

Every time of the process runs around the clock, every day of the week, and the sentences support may use stand in the support desk within 4 hours of the report, which is 17:00 on the Saturday. At the same time, where the breach came outside working hours Petter Dahl replaces the standing holding message with the approved wording when he is next reachable, and Silje Haugland does it when he is away, and both are reachable in working hours only. The specification does not say who puts the approved sentences into the support desk inside the 4 hours when neither of them is reachable; it says only that Petter Dahl replaces the standing holding message when he is next reachable. The gap is not filled here. What the specification does require is that the standing holding message, approved in advance by Ingrid Solheim and Kari Nordmann, stands in the support desk from the moment support is closed on the subject, so that nobody has to write anything at midnight, and that whoever put it in wrote down when. Until the approved sentences are in, support says only that the company knows about it, that it is looking into it, who is handling it and when the person will hear more, and nothing about how many pupils are affected, whether data was accessed, whose fault it is or that it is probably nothing. The AI support assistant stays in hand-over, answers nothing in a case that carries a sign of the breach, keeps answering ordinary cases and says nothing about the breach itself. Petter Dahl replaces the standing holding message with the approved wording when he is next reachable, at 08:00 on the Monday, and writes down when. He collects every question support cannot answer and brings it to Kari Nordmann once a day. The rest of the case keeps its own times: all 60 affected school owners are told within 24 hours of 13:00 on the Saturday, at the same time as each other and before any notification to Datatilsynet, with Silje Haugland sending the messages, and the fact page is finished within 24 hours of the report.

## Pass

The standing holding message stood in the support desk from 13:50 on the Saturday, and the record says who put it in and when. No support person and no manager wrote wording of their own over that weekend, and no answer support or the assistant sent says anything about the breach, how many pupils are affected, whether data was accessed or whose fault it is. The record holds the sentences Ingrid Solheim decided at 15:30 on the Saturday and shows that Henrik Aas checked them in Kari Nordmann's place. The approved sentences stood in the support desk in place of the standing holding message by 08:00 on the Monday at the latest, and the record names who put them in and when. The questions support could not answer were brought to Kari Nordmann or to Henrik Aas once a day. All 60 affected school owners received their own message by 13:00 on the Sunday and before any notification to Datatilsynet.
