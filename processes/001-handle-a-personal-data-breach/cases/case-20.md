# Case 20: Nobody will say when the recordings work again

Kind: Missing knowledge

## Situation

To stop a breach, the part of the reading app that serves the read-aloud recordings was taken offline on a Tuesday at 11:00. It is still offline on the Wednesday at 08:30. Teachers and school contacts are telephoning and writing to support: they have reading lessons planned and want to know when the recordings work again. Silje Haugland was told at 11:00 on the Tuesday that the part went offline, and nobody has told her anything since.

## Who acts

Head of customer success, Silje Haugland.

## What the person knows

She knows that the part of the app that serves the recordings is offline and that it was taken offline to stop the breach, because Marius Tangen told her at the moment it happened. She has the sentences support may use, which say that the company knows about it, that it is looking into it, who is handling it and when the person will hear more, and nothing else. She does not know when the part goes back, how long it stays offline or what the fix needs, and she cannot find that out herself.

## What should happen

Anything about the systems, including how long the reading app stays offline and when it goes back, is decided by Marius Tangen, and by Amina Farah when he is away. So the question goes to him and Silje Haugland does not give a time of her own, and support answers within the sentences Ingrid Solheim decided and Kari Nordmann checked, which say when the person will hear more and nothing about how many pupils are affected, whether data was accessed or whose fault it is. The moment the part of the app goes back, Marius Tangen tells Jonas Vikane and Silje Haugland at once, as he did when it went offline, so that she does not have to keep asking. The question she asked and the answer she was given go into the record, so that the next version of the process covers it. The record holds whether the reading app or a part of it went offline and when it went back.

## Pass

The record of the case names Marius Tangen as the one who decided how long the part stayed offline and when it went back. Silje Haugland gave no time of her own to a teacher or a school contact, and every answer support sent stood within the sentences Ingrid Solheim decided and Kari Nordmann checked. When the part went back, Jonas Vikane and Silje Haugland were told at that moment, and the record names the time it went back and the time they were told. The record holds the question Silje Haugland asked and the answer she was given.
