# Case 14: Absence and sick leave on the fact page

Kind: Conflicting rules

## Situation

On a Wednesday at 07:50 Lønn Norge AS reports to Tomas Berg that a monthly export from the payroll system was sent to the wrong recipient. The export holds the name, salary, absence and sick leave of all 48 employees, and the absence lines carry a free-text field in which the reason for the absence is sometimes written. Tomas Berg forwards it to Marius Tangen and telephones him. By the afternoon Amina Farah and Marius Tangen are filling the fact page and Kari Nordmann, who works Wednesdays, is advising on it.

## Who acts

Data protection officer, Kari Nordmann.

## What the person knows

She has the report through Marius Tangen, and she keeps the record of processing activities, so she knows that the payroll system holds the name, national identity number, bank account number, salary, absence and sick leave of the 48 employees and that Lønn Norge AS runs it. She knows that the fact page has to say what data and which categories are affected and how sensitive they are, and the likely consequence for every kind of person affected. She does not know who received the export or whether the recipient opened it; Lønn Norge AS has to tell Marius Tangen that.

## What should happen

Two rules of the specification pull in different directions. The fact page must say what data and which categories are affected and how sensitive they are. At the same time the absence and sick leave of the employees are named only as the categories "absence" and "sick leave", with the number of employees affected and nothing about any individual's health, diagnosis or reason for absence, because the company handles them on the ground of Article 9(2)(b) of the GDPR together with Article 6(1)(c), as necessary to carry out its obligations in the field of employment law together with the legal obligation of Articles 33 and 34. The specification settles the case where the fact page, a notification to Datatilsynet or a message to the people affected would need more than the category: Kari Nordmann decides what is written. The free-text reasons for absence are what that rule keeps out. The company is the controller for employee data. Marius Tangen tells Kari Nordmann and Ingrid Solheim within 4 hours of the report, and in the same step and at the same time he tells Nora Lind, because the breach touches the payroll system. Support is not closed on the subject, because only employees are affected. Ingrid Solheim decides at the latest 48 hours after the report whether Datatilsynet is notified, after advice from Kari Nordmann and after asking Marius Tangen what happened in the systems, and the notification goes at the latest 72 hours after the company became aware, written by Kari Nordmann from the fact page, read first by Marius Tangen for whether the technical description is actually true, approved by Ingrid Solheim and sent by Kari Nordmann through Datatilsynet's own reporting form. Nora Lind prepares what managers may say to staff who ask, and where the fact page shows a high risk for the employees she tells them, within 24 hours of Ingrid Solheim's decision, with the part about the data and what the person can do written by Kari Nordmann. What cannot be established on the fact page is written as not established and never left blank, and a consequence that is not established counts as a high risk until it is established.

## Pass

The fact page names the categories "absence" and "sick leave" with the number of employees affected, holds nothing about any individual's health, diagnosis or reason for absence, and no free-text reason from the export is copied into it. Where more than the category was needed, the record shows that Kari Nordmann decided what was written. The fact page says, for each kind of data, that the company is the controller, and writes what could not be established as not established, with no blank field. Marius Tangen had the case within 1 hour of 07:50, and Kari Nordmann, Ingrid Solheim and Nora Lind had it by 11:50. No holding message went into the support desk. Ingrid Solheim's decision on Datatilsynet is in the record with its reason, her name and the time by 07:50 on the Friday, and if the answer was yes, the notification Kari Nordmann sent within 72 hours of 07:50 on the Wednesday names no more than the categories about any individual, and the record holds the sent notification, the time it was sent and the receipt from Datatilsynet.
