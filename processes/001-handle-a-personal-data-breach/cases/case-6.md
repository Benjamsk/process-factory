# Case 6: The access logs have already rolled over

Kind: Missing knowledge

## Situation

A bug report shows that for the last four months a web address of the reading app returned a pupil's read-aloud recording to anyone who knew the address, without a login. The report reaches Marius Tangen on a Wednesday at 10:00 and he closes the hole the same morning. When Amina Farah starts on the facts, the Nordsky Cloud access logs only go back 90 days, so the first month of the window cannot be seen at all, and for the rest the logs do not record which recording was fetched.

## Who acts

Head of IT, Marius Tangen.

## What the person knows

He knows the window of four months, the web address and that the hole is now closed, and he knows that the Nordsky Cloud access logs roll over after 90 days and that he has secured a copy of what is left. He does not know whether anyone outside the company fetched a recording in the first month, and the logs cannot tell him which recordings were fetched in the rest of the window, so he does not know which pupils or which school owners are affected.

## What should happen

Marius Tangen owns the fact page and signs it off within 24 hours, and Amina Farah does the work in the systems. The page says whether anyone actually accessed the data, which means what the Nordsky Cloud logs show, and where the logs cannot tell, it says that access cannot be ruled out, and the case is then treated as accessed. What cannot be established is written as not established and never left blank, so the pupils and the school owners that cannot be identified from the logs are written as not established rather than omitted. The page is not finished until Kari Nordmann says she has what she needs, and she may ask for anything more her advice requires. Because the case is treated as accessed, every affected school owner is told within 24 hours of the company becoming aware, before Datatilsynet is notified, and the message says which data and how many pupils are affected. The specification does not say which school owners are told when the logs cannot say whose recordings were fetched; it requires only that the page name which pupils and how many and which school owners they belong to, or say that this is not established. The gap is not filled here.

## Pass

The fact page exists within 24 hours of 10:00 on the Wednesday, is signed off by Marius Tangen, and states the four-month window, that access cannot be ruled out and that the case is treated as accessed. Every fact that could not be established, including the pupils and school owners that the logs cannot identify, is written on the page as not established, and no field on the page is blank. Kari Nordmann has said that she has what she needs before the page is treated as final. The secured copy of the remaining Nordsky Cloud logs is stored where Kari Nordmann can find it again, and the record says what the logs showed and what could not be established.
