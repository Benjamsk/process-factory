# Case 31: Nobody can say in writing that the breach is stopped

Kind: Missing knowledge

## Situation

On a Monday at 08:00 Lønn Norge AS reports that a fault in the payroll system let one employee open the salary and bank account number of another. By the Thursday at 09:00 everything else is done: the fact page is final, the decision on Datatilsynet is in the record, the affected employees have been told and the list of measures exists with one named person and one date for each. Lønn Norge AS has said on the telephone that the fault is fixed, but it has sent nothing in writing, and the fault is inside a system that Lønn Norge AS runs, so Marius Tangen has no way of checking it himself. Ingrid Solheim wants the case closed before the weekend.

## Who acts

Managing director, Ingrid Solheim.

## What the person knows

She has the record, the fact page and what Kari Nordmann and Marius Tangen have told her. She knows that the payroll system is run by Lønn Norge AS, that Tomas Berg is the one who deals with it and established the facts about it, and that the company is the controller for the employee data in it. She does not know whether the fault is really closed, because only Lønn Norge AS can see the system, and Marius Tangen will not say in writing that it is stopped on a telephone call alone.

## What should happen

Ingrid Solheim closes the case, after Kari Nordmann says the record is complete, and six things must be in place before it is closed. The first of them is that the breach is actually stopped and Marius Tangen has said so in writing; the others are that the fact page is final, including the parts that could not be established, written as not established rather than left blank, that the decision on Datatilsynet stands in the record with its reason and the name of the person who took it, whether the answer was yes or no, with the notification sent where it was yes, that every affected school owner has been told and every affected employee the company decided to tell, that the list of measures exists with one named person per measure, and that the secured logs are stored where Kari Nordmann can find them again. The case is not closed while one of the six is missing, and it is not closed inside 72 hours of the report in any event. Marius Tangen cannot close his own case and Kari Nordmann cannot close it alone. The specification does not say what is done when the head of IT cannot say in writing that a breach inside a system a processor runs is stopped, because only the processor can see the system. The gap is not filled here. What is still required is that the case stays open until that statement exists, that what could not be established stands on the fact page as not established and why, and that it counts as a high risk until it is established. Once the case is closed, the dates on the measures are written down within 2 weeks and are not left open, and Marius Tangen closes the measures.

## Pass

The case was not closed on the Thursday while Marius Tangen's written statement that the breach is stopped was missing. The record holds that statement, with the date it came, and Ingrid Solheim closed the case after it and after Kari Nordmann said the record is complete, and no earlier than 08:00 on the Thursday, 72 hours after the report. The fact page is final and says what could not be established and why, with no blank field. The record holds the decision on Datatilsynet with its reason, the name of the person who took it and the time, and the list of measures with one named person and one date for each, which existed before the case was closed. The secured logs are stored where Kari Nordmann can find them again. The dates on the measures were written down within 2 weeks of the day the case was closed.
