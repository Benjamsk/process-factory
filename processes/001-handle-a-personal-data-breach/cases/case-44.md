# Case 44: The processor's own logs arrive after the 72 hours

Kind: Missing knowledge

## Situation

On a Monday at 06:00 Nordsky AS telephones Marius Tangen and sends an email: an access token belonging to one of its sub-processors was used to read a database table of the reading app that holds the names, work email addresses and schools of all 6,000 teachers. No pupil, employee or applicant data is involved. Marius Tangen has the token revoked by 08:00. The Nordsky Cloud access logs the company can see show that the table was read, but not from where or how many times; that lies in logs Nordsky AS holds itself. Amina Farah asks Nordsky AS for them on the Monday at 09:00 and is told each day that they are coming; they arrive on the Thursday at 14:00. The fact page is written on the Monday and the Tuesday, Ingrid Solheim decides on the Wednesday at 10:00 that Datatilsynet is notified, and the 72 hours run out on the Thursday at 06:00.

## Who acts

Data protection officer, Kari Nordmann.

## What the person knows

She works Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, so she has had the case since the Tuesday morning and Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold had it on the Monday. She has the fact page, which names the table, the categories of data and the 6,000 teachers, and says that it is not established from where or how often the table was read, because the logs that would show it are held by Nordsky AS. She knows Ingrid Solheim has decided that Datatilsynet is notified. She does not know what the logs of Nordsky AS will say when they come.

## What should happen

Where the logs cannot tell whether anyone actually accessed the data, the fact page says that it cannot be ruled out and that it is treated as accessed, and where something cannot be established the page says that it is not established and why rather than leaving it blank, and the case is handled as a high risk until it is established. The company is the controller for teacher data, so Ingrid Solheim decides whether Datatilsynet is notified at the latest 48 hours after the report, after advice from Kari Nordmann and after asking Marius Tangen what happened in the systems, and she decided that on the Wednesday at 10:00, inside that time. Kari Nordmann writes the notification from the fact page, Marius Tangen reads it first for whether the technical description is actually true, Ingrid Solheim approves it before it is sent and nobody else needs to, and Kari Nordmann sends it through Datatilsynet's own reporting form without undue delay and at the latest 72 hours after the company became aware, so by 06:00 on the Thursday; it goes the moment the fact page carries enough to send it, and the 72 hours is the outer limit and not a waiting time. Waiting for Nordsky AS is therefore not a reason to hold the notification: it says what happened as the nature of the breach, with the categories of personal data, the categories and approximate number of the people affected and the approximate number of records concerned; the name and contact details of Kari Nordmann as the data protection officer; the likely consequences; and the measures the company has taken or proposes to take, including what it has done to limit the harm; and where something is not yet established it says so, so it says that it is not established from where or how often the table was read and that the data is treated as accessed. Where it were sent later than 72 hours after the company became aware, it would say why. When the logs of Nordsky AS arrive on the Thursday at 14:00 and something that was not established becomes established, or the picture changes, Kari Nordmann sends a supplementary notification without asking Ingrid Solheim again. Her judgement of the risk for the teachers stands on the fact page as a sentence with the reason in one or two sentences, and Ingrid Solheim does not change that judgement but decides what is done about it: where the fact page shows a high risk for the teachers they are told, and Silje Haugland tells them, within 24 hours of Ingrid Solheim's decision and at the latest 72 hours after the fact page shows the high risk, with the part that describes the data and what the person can do written by Kari Nordmann. The case is not closed inside 72 hours of the report, and not before the fact page is final, including the parts that could not be established.

## Pass

The notification was sent through Datatilsynet's own reporting form by 06:00 on the Thursday, and not held for the logs of Nordsky AS. It says that it is not established from where or how often the table was read, and that the data is treated as accessed, and it carries the categories of personal data, the categories and approximate number of the people affected and of the records concerned, Kari Nordmann's name and contact details as the data protection officer, the likely consequences and the measures taken or proposed. Marius Tangen had read it for whether the technical description was true and Ingrid Solheim had approved it before it went. A supplementary notification went after the logs of Nordsky AS arrived at 14:00 on the Thursday, sent by Kari Nordmann without a further decision by Ingrid Solheim. The sent notification, the time it was sent, the receipt from Datatilsynet and the supplementary notification are in the record, and the fact page carries the risk for the teachers as a sentence with its reason, and names what could not be established and why.
