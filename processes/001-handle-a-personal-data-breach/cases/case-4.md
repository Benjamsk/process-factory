# Case 4: Friday night report and the head of IT does not answer

Kind: Outside working hours

## Situation

On Friday 27 November 2026 at 23:40 Nordsky AS telephones the on-call number and sends an email: an access token belonging to the reading app has been used from an address outside the company for the last two hours, and the token can read the pupil database. Marius Tangen's mobile rings out three times.

## Who acts

On-call developer, Erik Moen.

## What the person knows

He has the report from Nordsky AS on the on-call telephone. He knows that he can revoke an access token and lock an account in Nordsky Cloud and can restart and roll back the reading app, and that Marius Tangen has not answered. He does not know whether the token was used to read anything, how many pupils that would be or which school owners they belong to, and he knows that Friday is not one of Kari Nordmann's working days.

## What should happen

All the times of the process run around the clock, including evenings and weekends. Erik Moen takes the report and wakes Marius Tangen, which is what the process says when Marius Tangen cannot be reached. He revokes the access token and locks the account in Nordsky Cloud, and the logs are secured and the breach stopped within 4 hours of 23:40. He starts the fact page with what is known in the first hours. Kari Nordmann and Ingrid Solheim have the case within 4 hours; because Friday is not one of Kari Nordmann's three working days the case goes to Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold on the firm's emergency number and does not wait for Tuesday, and Ingrid Solheim is reached on her mobile. When support is closed to the subject, Erik Moen switches the AI support assistant into the standing hand-over configuration Petter Dahl prepared in advance and puts the standing holding message, approved in advance by Ingrid Solheim and Kari Nordmann, into the support desk, and Silje Haugland and Petter Dahl are telephoned at home at once; if neither of them answers at home it is escalated to Ingrid Solheim. The decision whether this is a real breach is Marius Tangen's, or Amina Farah's when he is away, so it is not taken by Erik Moen.

## Pass

The token was revoked and the account locked by 03:40 on the Saturday, and the secured copy of the Nordsky Cloud access logs exists. Marius Tangen was telephoned and woken, and the record names who called him and when. A fact page exists, started by Erik Moen with what was known in the first hours. Henrik Aas and Ingrid Solheim had the case by 03:40. The record says when the AI support assistant was switched into hand-over and the standing holding message put into the support desk, and that Erik Moen did it. Silje Haugland and Petter Dahl were telephoned at home that night, and if neither answered, Ingrid Solheim was told.
