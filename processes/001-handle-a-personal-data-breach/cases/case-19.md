# Case 19: The secured logs fall due for deletion while Datatilsynet is still asking

Kind: Conflicting rules

## Situation

A case about a breach of pupil and teacher data in the reading app was closed on 10 December 2025. The secured copies of the Nordsky Cloud access logs from that case fall due for deletion on Thursday 10 December 2026, 12 months after the day the case was closed. In October 2026 Datatilsynet wrote to the company with questions about the same breach, through Kari Nordmann, and that matter is still open. One of the affected school owners has also written and has not been answered yet.

## Who acts

Head of IT, Marius Tangen.

## What the person knows

He knows the day the case was closed, where the secured copies are stored and that they hold the personal data of pupils and teachers, and he knows from Kari Nordmann that Datatilsynet is still asking about the breach and that a school owner has an open question. He does not know when Datatilsynet will close its own case.

## What should happen

Two rules of the specification pull in different directions. Marius Tangen deletes the secured copies of the Nordsky Cloud access logs 12 months after the day the case is closed, which is 10 December 2026. At the same time they are kept longer while a case about the breach with Datatilsynet, with a school owner or before a court is still open, and then until that case ends. The specification settles it: because the matter with Datatilsynet and the question from the school owner are still open, the copies are not deleted on 10 December 2026 but kept until those end, and the record of the case says why they were kept longer. The record also says where the secured copies are kept and that they hold personal data, and Kari Nordmann keeps the record of the case, with the record of processing activities in the folder of the data protection officer in the office suite, for 5 years. When the matter with Datatilsynet and the question from the school owner have ended, Marius Tangen deletes the copies.

## Pass

The secured copies of the Nordsky Cloud access logs still existed after 10 December 2026 and were not deleted on that day. The record of the case says that they were kept longer and why, naming the open matter with Datatilsynet and the open question from the school owner, and it says where the copies are kept and that they hold personal data. The copies were deleted after both of those matters ended, and the record says when. The record of the case is in the folder of the data protection officer in the office suite and is kept for 5 years.
