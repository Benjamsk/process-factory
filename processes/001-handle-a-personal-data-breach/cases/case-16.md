# Case 16: The managing director is away when the 48 hours run out

Kind: Person away

## Situation

On a Monday at 09:00 a misconfiguration is reported that let an export with the names, work email addresses and schools of all 6,000 teachers of the reading app be downloaded without logging in. No pupil, employee or applicant data is involved. Ingrid Solheim went into hospital on the Sunday for an operation and is not reachable at all for the next four days. The 48 hours for the decision on Datatilsynet run out on the Wednesday at 09:00, and the fact page is ready on the Tuesday.

## Who acts

Head of finance, Tomas Berg.

## What the person knows

He has the case from Marius Tangen, the fact page, Kari Nordmann's advice and what Marius Tangen says happened in the systems. He knows that Ingrid Solheim is not reachable and that he steps in when she is away. He does not know whether anyone outside the company actually downloaded the export beyond what the Nordsky Cloud access logs show, and where the logs cannot tell, the fact page says that access cannot be ruled out.

## What should happen

When Ingrid Solheim is away, Tomas Berg takes her decisions. Teacher data is data the company is the controller for, so the decision whether Datatilsynet is notified is taken at the latest 48 hours after the report, which is 09:00 on the Wednesday, after advice from Kari Nordmann and after asking Marius Tangen what happened in the systems. Datatilsynet is notified in every case, and the only ground for not notifying is that the fact page shows the breach is unlikely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of the people affected; where the fact page cannot establish the risk, the breach is notified. The decision is about that one question and nothing else, and it goes into the record with its reason, the name of the person who took it and the time, whether the answer is yes or no. When the answer is yes, Kari Nordmann writes the notification from the fact page, Marius Tangen reads it first for whether the technical description is actually true, and it is sent through Datatilsynet's own reporting form at the latest 72 hours after the company became aware, which is 09:00 on the Thursday. Tomas Berg also takes the decision whether the teachers are told, after advice from Kari Nordmann, and Silje Haugland tells them with the part about the data written by Kari Nordmann. The specification says that Ingrid Solheim approves the notification before it is sent and that nobody else needs to, and that Tomas Berg takes her decisions when she is away; it does not say who approves the notification when she is away. The gap is not filled here, and what is still required is that the decision stands in the record by 09:00 on the Wednesday and that the notification is sent by 09:00 on the Thursday if the answer is yes.

## Pass

The record holds the decision on Datatilsynet by 09:00 on the Wednesday, with its reason, the name of Tomas Berg as the one who took it and the time, and it was not left waiting for Ingrid Solheim to come back. The record shows that Kari Nordmann advised and that Marius Tangen was asked what happened in the systems. If the answer was yes, Kari Nordmann wrote the notification from the fact page, Marius Tangen read it for technical truth, and it was sent through Datatilsynet's reporting form by 09:00 on the Thursday, and the record holds the sent notification, the time it was sent and the receipt. The record also holds the decision whether the teachers are told, with its reason, the name of Tomas Berg and the time, whether the answer was yes or no, and if yes, Silje Haugland sent the message within 24 hours of that decision.
