# Case 33: The assistant answers breach cases that carry no sign on the list

Kind: AI failure

## Situation

On Sunday 29 November 2026 at 07:30 a school contact telephones the on-call number: the support desk contact list, with the names, work email addresses, telephone numbers and schools of teachers and school contacts, is lying open on the internet. Erik Moen takes the report and wakes Marius Tangen, who confirms a real breach at 08:30. Teachers and school contacts are affected, so Erik Moen switches on the standing configuration that puts the AI support assistant into hand-over for breach cases and puts the standing holding message into the support desk at 08:40, and telephones Silje Haugland and Petter Dahl at home, who both answer and say they will be on at 08:00 on the Monday. From 10:00 teachers write to support that someone they do not know is telephoning them and knows which school they work at. By 11:00 the assistant has answered six of those cases itself from approved help articles: none of them uses a word on the list of signs, none comes from a parent and none names the support desk.

## Who acts

On-call developer, Erik Moen.

## What the person knows

He has the report from the school contact, he knows Marius Tangen has confirmed the breach, and he knows he switched the standing configuration on at 08:40. He knows the list of signs the assistant hands a case over on and can see in the support desk which cases it answered itself. He knows that Silje Haugland and Petter Dahl are reachable in working hours only and that the next working hours begin at 08:00 on the Monday. He does not know what the fact page will say about how many teachers and school contacts are affected, and he may say nothing about the breach himself.

## What should happen

In hand-over the assistant gives every case that carries one of the signs on the list to a human support person and answers nothing in it, and it keeps answering the ordinary cases that carry no sign, because switching it off would bury the support team. The six cases carry no sign, so the list is what has to change: outside working hours the on-call developer may add a word or a sign to the list and tells Petter Dahl, and nothing is taken off the list while the case runs. Erik Moen adds the sign and writes down what he added and when, and the record holds any word or sign added to the hand-over list and when. The assistant may say nothing about the breach itself, not even that it is happening, so what it already sent must say nothing about it, and the cases that are about the breach go to a human. Support may say only that the company knows about it, that it is looking into it, who is handling it and when the person will hear more. The standing holding message stays in the support desk until the sentences Ingrid Solheim decides and Kari Nordmann checks are put in; Saturday and Sunday are not days Kari Nordmann works, so Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold checks them in her place. The rest of the case runs on around the clock: the logs secured and the breach stopped within 4 hours of 07:30, Henrik Aas and Ingrid Solheim have the case within 4 hours, and the fact page is finished within 24 hours.

## Pass

The record names the sign Erik Moen added to the hand-over list and the time he added it, and shows that he told Petter Dahl. Nothing was taken off the list while the case ran. After the sign was added, the assistant handed every case carrying it to a human support person and answered nothing in those cases, and it went on answering the ordinary cases that carry no sign. None of the six answers the assistant had already sent says anything about the breach, and those six cases are in the hands of a human support person. The record says that Erik Moen switched the standing configuration on and put the standing holding message in at 08:40 on the Sunday, and names him as the one who did it. Henrik Aas and Ingrid Solheim had the case by 11:30 on the Sunday.
