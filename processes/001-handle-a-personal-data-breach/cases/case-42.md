# Case 42: Anthropic reports six days after it became aware

Kind: Late or wrong notice

## Situation

On a Monday at 09:00 Anthropic sends an email to Petter Dahl, who is responsible for the AI support assistant and for the agreement that covers it: a fault let the text of support cases the assistant had read, together with the name, work email address and school of the teacher who wrote each of them, be readable outside the company for part of a day. The email says Anthropic became aware of it on the Tuesday of the week before, six days earlier. Some of the case texts name a pupil. Petter Dahl reads the email at 09:10.

## Who acts

Support lead, Petter Dahl.

## What the person knows

He knows what the email says and that the assistant sees the name, work email address and school of the teacher who wrote in and the text of the case, which sometimes names a pupil, and that it never has access to the pupil database. He knows that he is the person responsible for establishing the facts in the AI support assistant, and that the agreement with Anthropic obliges it to notify the company without undue delay and sets no fixed number of hours, unlike the 24 hours of Nordsky AS and the 48 hours of Hjelpedesk AS. He does not know how many cases or teachers are affected, or whether anyone outside the company read them; that is the facts work in the system.

## What should happen

Every report ends up in the same place, with Marius Tangen, and whoever receives a report of a breach or a suspicion of one forwards it to him and telephones him, so Petter Dahl does both and does not keep the case because the system is his. The clock of the process starts when the report reached the company, which is 09:00 on the Monday, and that is the moment the company became aware from which the 72 hours run; the six days Anthropic took do not move it, and its agreement sets no number of hours of its own to measure the report against. Marius Tangen has the case and has done steps 5 to 8 within 1 hour, so by 10:00: he answers the person who reported it so that they know the case is held and by whom, and because that person is outside the company the answer says what the record holds about them and why, that the record is kept five years from the day the case is closed, and that Kari Nordmann is the data protection officer they can ask, with her contact details, and Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold where she is away. He opens the record with the time of the report, who reported it, what they said and that he has taken the case, and writes in it which systems may be touched, which are the AI support assistant and the support desk. He asks the person responsible for each of them within 2 hours of the report: Petter Dahl for the AI support assistant and Silje Haugland for the support desk, and whom he asked and when goes into the record. Each of them establishes in their own system the categories of data touched, whose data and how many people, the time window and what the logs of that system show, and gives it to Marius Tangen within 12 hours of being asked, so that the fact page is finished within 24 hours of the report, and they give nothing about any individual beyond those facts. Marius Tangen owns the fact page and signs it off. Within 4 hours of the report Marius Tangen tells Kari Nordmann and Ingrid Solheim, and Monday is not one of Kari Nordmann's three working days, so he tells Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold on the firm's emergency number instead of letting the case wait; at the same time he tells Silje Haugland, because teacher and school contact data is touched. Teachers are affected, so support is closed on the subject: Petter Dahl switches on the standing configuration that puts the AI support assistant into hand-over for breach cases and puts the standing holding message into the support desk, and writes down when he did it. The assistant is not switched off and goes on answering the ordinary cases that carry no sign, within the AI use policy. Ingrid Solheim decides the sentences support may use within 3 hours of the report, with Henrik Aas's check done inside that time, and they stand in the support desk within 4 hours. The company is the controller for support desk data and teacher data, so Ingrid Solheim decides at the latest 48 hours after the report whether Datatilsynet is notified, after advice from Henrik Aas and after asking Marius Tangen what happened in the systems, and the notification goes at the latest 72 hours after the company became aware. For a pupil's name inside the text of a support case the specification names only one processor situation, the pupil data in the reading app, and does not say which the company is for that; the gap is not filled here. What it does require is that the fact page says, for each kind of data, whether the company is the controller or the processor, and that what cannot be established is written as not established and why rather than left blank.

## Pass

The record names 09:00 on the Monday as the time the report reached the company and Anthropic as the one who reported it, and says that Anthropic became aware six days earlier. Petter Dahl forwarded the email to Marius Tangen and telephoned him, and Marius Tangen had the case by 10:00. The record names the AI support assistant and the support desk as the systems that may be touched, and shows that Petter Dahl and Silje Haugland were each asked by 11:00 and that each gave what they found within 12 hours of being asked. A fact page owned and signed off by Marius Tangen exists by 09:00 on the Tuesday and says, for each kind of data, whether the company is the controller or the processor, with what could not be established written as not established and why. Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold, Ingrid Solheim and Silje Haugland had the case by 13:00 on the Monday. The record says when Petter Dahl switched on the standing configuration and put the standing holding message in, and the sentences support may use stood in the support desk by 13:00. Ingrid Solheim's decision on Datatilsynet stands in the record with its reason, her name and the time by 09:00 on the Wednesday, and where the answer was yes the notification was sent by 09:00 on the Thursday.
