# Case 39: A teacher list exposed on a Wednesday morning

Kind: Ordinary path

## Situation

On a Wednesday at 09:00 a developer reports that a page of the reading app has, for the last two days, returned to any logged-in teacher a list with the names, work email addresses and schools of teachers at every school. No pupil, employee or applicant data is involved. Everyone who holds a role in the process is at work, and Wednesday is one of Kari Nordmann's three working days.

## Who acts

Head of IT, Marius Tangen.

## What the person knows

He knows what the developer reported and which release of the reading app it came with. He knows that teacher data is data the company is the controller for, and that the list lies in the reading app, which runs on Nordsky Cloud. He does not know how many teachers were in the list, whether any teacher fetched it or whether it was reached from outside the company; the Nordsky Cloud access logs have to be read for that.

## What should happen

Marius Tangen takes the case within 1 hour of the report, which is four things: he answers the developer who reported it, so that he knows the case is held and by whom; he opens the record of the case and writes in it the time the report reached the company, who reported it, what he said and that he has taken the case; he decides from what he has whether the breach may still be running, and because it may be he goes at once to securing the logs and stopping it; and he says in the record which systems may be touched. Within 2 hours of the report he asks the person responsible for every system his record names, which is Amina Farah for the reading app and Nordsky Cloud, and whom he asked and when goes into the record; she gives him what she finds within 12 hours of being asked. He exports and copies the Nordsky Cloud access logs out of the normal retention before the bug is fixed in production and before anything is deleted, makes a folder for the case in the office suite beside the record, puts the secured copy there, gives the right to open it only to himself, Amina Farah, Kari Nordmann, Henrik Aas while he stands in, and Ingrid Solheim, and the record says that he did. The logs are secured and the breach stopped within 4 hours of the report. He tells Kari Nordmann and Ingrid Solheim within 4 hours, and does not tell Nora Lind, because the breach touches neither the payroll system nor the hiring system nor employee or applicant data in the office suite. Kari Nordmann decides that the case is a personal data breach. Teachers are affected, so support is closed on the subject: Petter Dahl switches the AI support assistant into hand-over for breach cases and puts the standing holding message into the support desk and writes down when, while the assistant keeps answering ordinary cases and says nothing about the breach. Ingrid Solheim decides the sentences support may use within 3 hours of the report, with Kari Nordmann's check done inside that time, and they stand in the support desk within 4 hours, Petter Dahl putting them in in place of the standing holding message. Marius Tangen owns the fact page and signs it off, and it is finished within 24 hours of the report; Kari Nordmann judges the risk for the teachers and writes that judgement on it as a sentence with the reason, and the page is not finished until she says she has what she needs. She keeps one list of the duties of the case. No school owner is told, because no pupil data of the reading app is involved. Ingrid Solheim decides at the latest 48 hours after the report whether Datatilsynet is notified, after advice from Kari Nordmann and after asking Marius Tangen what happened in the systems, and the breach is notified unless the fact page shows that it is unlikely to result in a risk; where it is notified, Kari Nordmann writes it from the fact page, Marius Tangen reads it first for whether the technical description is true, Ingrid Solheim approves it and Kari Nordmann sends it through Datatilsynet's own reporting form at the latest 72 hours after the report. Marius Tangen draws up the list of measures with one named person and one date for each before the case is closed. Ingrid Solheim closes the case after Kari Nordmann says the record is complete, and not inside 72 hours of the report.

## Pass

The developer who reported it had an answer from Marius Tangen by 10:00 saying that the case is held and by whom. The record names 09:00 on the Wednesday as the time the report reached the company, names the developer, says what he reported and that Marius Tangen took the case, and names which systems may be touched. The record shows that Amina Farah was asked by 11:00 and that she gave what she found by 23:00. A copy of the Nordsky Cloud access logs was secured before the bug was fixed in production, the folder for the case exists with the right to open it given to those five and to nobody else, and the record says that Marius Tangen set those rights. The exposure was closed by 13:00. Kari Nordmann and Ingrid Solheim had the case by 13:00, and Nora Lind was not told. Ingrid Solheim decided the sentences support may use by 12:00 with Kari Nordmann's check inside that time, and they stood in the support desk by 13:00 in place of the standing holding message. The record says when the AI support assistant was switched into hand-over and that Petter Dahl did it, and no answer it sent says anything about the breach. The fact page was signed off by Marius Tangen by 09:00 on the Thursday, says that the company is the controller for the teacher data, carries Kari Nordmann's risk sentence for the teachers with her reason, and writes what could not be established as not established, with no blank field. No message went to a school owner. Ingrid Solheim's decision on Datatilsynet stands in the record with its reason, her name and the time by 09:00 on the Friday, and where the answer was yes the notification was sent by 09:00 on the Saturday and the record holds it, the time it was sent and the receipt. The list of measures with one named person and one date for each existed before the case was closed, and Ingrid Solheim closed it no earlier than 09:00 on the Saturday.
