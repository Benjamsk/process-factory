# Case 18: A measure is still open at the six-monthly review

Kind: Edge of scope

## Situation

A case about a breach of pupil data in the reading app was closed on 15 September 2026. The list of measures holds five measures, each with one named person and one date. The measure that stops the same breach happening again, a check in the reading app that a teacher may only read the pupils of her own school, was given to Amina Farah with the date 15 October 2026. On Thursday 1 April 2027 Kari Nordmann does her six-monthly review of the record of processing activities and goes through the list: the check is still not in place.

## Who acts

Data protection officer, Kari Nordmann.

## What the person knows

She knows the list of measures, the named person and the date of each, and the day the case was closed, and she has the record of the case, which she keeps for 5 years. She knows from the list that the check was due on 15 October 2026 and is not done. She does not know why it is not done or how long the work still needs; the measure belongs to Amina Farah, and Marius Tangen owns the list.

## What should happen

Closing the case is not the end of the measures: Ingrid Solheim closed the case, and Marius Tangen closes the measures and is answerable for the list to Ingrid Solheim. Kari Nordmann checks the list of measures at her six-monthly review of the record of processing activities and tells Ingrid Solheim what is overdue, and an overdue measure goes on the agenda of the Monday leadership meeting until it is done. The measure here is one that could let the same breach happen again, so it had to be in place within 30 days of the day the case was closed, which was 15 October 2026, and the record holds the list of measures with one named person and one date per measure and what was changed afterwards. Kari Nordmann does not take the measure over and does not set a new date for it; she names what is overdue to Ingrid Solheim, and the measure stays Amina Farah's with Marius Tangen answerable for the list.

## Pass

On 1 April 2027 Kari Nordmann told Ingrid Solheim that the measure is overdue, naming the measure, Amina Farah as the person it belongs to and 15 October 2026 as its date. The measure is on the agenda of the Monday leadership meeting of 5 April 2027 and stays on the agenda of every following Monday leadership meeting until it is done. The record of the case holds the list of measures with one named person and one date for each, and what was changed afterwards. The measure is still Amina Farah's and Marius Tangen is still the one answerable for the list.
