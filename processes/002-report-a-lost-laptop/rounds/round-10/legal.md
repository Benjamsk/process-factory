# legal review of draft-12.md

Verdict: CHANGES

- [high] Steps 19 and 23: step 19 counts a device whose holder has left the company as one with "no person behind the device", and step 23 then says that where there is no person behind the device there is nobody to tell and the device is wiped at once, so a person who has left is never told before the wipe and is never asked what exists only on the device, although the company holds an address that reaches them in the employment file, which steps 16 and 17 use; step 19 must keep the person who had the device as the person behind it where they have left the company, so that the attempt of step 23 and its hour still apply to them, and step 34 must not move their police report to Marius Tangen on that ground. Rule: `Lock or wipe a lost device remotely` of `organisation/authority.md`, which says the employee who lost the device is told before it is wiped where they can be reached, and the duty of `Duties to notify` of `spec.md` that Marius Tangen tells the person who had the device, before the wipe, that the device will be wiped and asks what exists only on it.
- [high] Steps 11 and 17: step 17 says that a company laptop, work phone or tablet in device management is in it also where it does not answer, while step 11 changes or revokes a password, key or token stored on the device only "where the device is not in device management", so for a company device that device management cannot reach because it does not answer, the passwords, keys and tokens stored on it are never changed or revoked anywhere; step 11 must cover every device that device management cannot reach, including one that is in device management but does not answer, within 2 hours of the report. Rule: the deadline "A password, key or token stored on a device that device management cannot reach is changed or revoked in every system it could reach — Within 2 hours" of the section `Deadlines` of `spec.md`.
- [high] Steps 16 and 17, and the fifth bullet of the telling in step 13: Nora Lind reads the employment file of a person who has left the company, which is their private email address or postal address, when they left, what they handed back and what they said about it then, and sends the telling to them at that private address, and no rule covers that processing; the section `Legal basis` of `spec.md` names GDPR Article 6(1)(f) for the personal data of the person who had the device only as the device's own entries in device management and the entries of that one account in the sign-in logs of the office suite and the access logs of Nordsky Cloud in the window from when the device went missing until its access was blocked, and names GDPR Article 6(1)(c) with Articles 33 and 34 only from the moment the loss is decided to be a personal data breach, so the draft must name the basis for the employment-file data of the person who had the device; `spec.md` does not name one either. Rule: the section `Legal basis` of `spec.md`, which names no rule for reading the employment file of the person who had the device or for writing to them at a private address.
