# readability review of draft-12.md

- [medium] Step 13: the step asks for three actions at once: establish the facts from the three sources; keep the reading inside the window from when the device went missing until its access was blocked; and ask the person who had the device for the facts while telling them the seven things about what is read about them. Each has to be its own step, as `spec.md` keeps them apart in its steps 10, 12 and 13.
- [medium] Step 41: the step asks for four actions: write into the record that the device was found, when, where and whether anyone else could have used it; undo nothing that was already done; do not wipe a device recovered inside the 72 hours; and check the device and say it is safe before the person uses it again.
- [medium] Step 37: `Give the person their access back within 2 working days of the report` names a result and no action the reader can do. Step 5 says what blocking the accounts and access consists of, and says there is no other measure behind that name, but no step says what giving the access back consists of in the office suite, in Nordsky Cloud and in the support desk. `spec.md` does not say either: its step 32 says only `and their access back`.
