# Case 21: The laptop of an employee on three weeks' leave is gone from his desk

Kind: Person away

## Situation

Thursday 26 November 2026. Support lead Petter Dahl comes to the desk of support adviser Jostein Vold at 10:30 to fetch a cable and sees that the company laptop Jostein Vold left at the office before his leave is not there. Nobody can say when it was last seen. Jostein Vold began three weeks of leave abroad on Monday 23 November and does not answer his telephone. Petter Dahl telephones head of IT Marius Tangen at 10:40, and the report reaches the company at 10:40. The laptop answers in device management. The equipment register shows it was bought for 15,400 kroner.

## Who acts

Head of IT, Marius Tangen.

## What the person knows

He knows the loss reached the company at 10:40 and that the laptop was at the office when Jostein Vold went on leave. Device management gives him the model and serial number, whether the disk was encrypted, whether the screen was locked and when and from where the laptop last contacted the company, and it answers, so he can locate and lock it. The sign-in logs of the office suite and the access logs of Nordsky Cloud tell him which systems it was signed in to, with which account, and whether anything was reached from it after it went missing. He knows the laptop was signed in to the office suite and the support desk, which hold personal data. He knows that where the person who had the device has not left the company but cannot be reached, the device is handled the same way as one with no person behind it, and that he asks them for the facts as soon as they can be reached and adds what they say to the record. He does not know where and when the laptop went missing, whether it was taken or mislaid, whether anyone else could have seen the screen or the password, or what was on it that exists nowhere else: those are the facts only Jostein Vold can give, and he cannot be reached.

## What should happen

Every time counts from 10:40.

- By 11:40 Marius Tangen locates and locks the laptop in device management, which needs no asking.
- By 12:40 he ends Jostein Vold's sessions, changes his password and revokes his access tokens in the office suite, in Nordsky Cloud and in the support desk, blocks the device in device management, and tells Nora Lind that the accounts and access are blocked.
- He tells Kari Nordmann about the lost device at the same time as he starts establishing the facts. She works on Thursdays.
- By 14:40 the six facts stand in the record. The four from device management and the ones from the sign-in logs of the office suite and the access logs of Nordsky Cloud he establishes himself. Jostein Vold cannot be reached, so the device is handled the same way as one with no person behind it: Nora Lind gives what the company knows in place of the facts only he could give, and everything that is still open is written as `not established` with its reason, among it whether the laptop was taken or mislaid, which nobody can say. Marius Tangen asks Jostein Vold for those facts as soon as he can be reached and adds what he says to the record. For what Nora Lind gives, the specification names who had the device, when they left, what they handed back and what they said about it then, which are facts about a person who has left the company, and it does not say what she gives for a person who is only away; that gap is not filled here.
- By 14:40 Marius Tangen tells Jostein Vold in plain language what he reads about him and why: that he looks at where and when the laptop last contacted the company and at the entries of his account in the sign-in logs of the office suite and the access logs of Nordsky Cloud, for the window from when the laptop went missing until its access was blocked; that this is to find out what could have been reached, and nothing else; that it is not used to judge how he works; that what is found goes into the record of the case, which is kept 3 years, or 5 years where the loss was also a personal data breach; and that Kari Nordmann is the data protection officer he can ask about it. He cannot be reached in time, so Marius Tangen sends him the same thing at his work email within those 4 hours so that it is waiting for him, and says it again when he first speaks to him.
- By 14:40 Marius Tangen decides whether the laptop is wiped, having asked Kari Nordmann first because it held personal data. It was signed in to the office suite and the support desk, which is a ground for a wipe, so it is wiped. Before the wipe he tries to reach Jostein Vold to tell him that the laptop will be wiped and to ask him what exists only on it; the wipe is not held back for longer than an hour because he cannot be reached, and the record says what was tried and when, and that what was lost is not known from him.
- By 14:40 Kari Nordmann decides from the established facts whether the loss is a personal data breach and writes the decision into the record with her name, the reason and the time, whether the answer is yes or no.
- Whether the laptop was taken or was mislaid is not established, so there is no report to the police; where the facts later show it was taken, Jostein Vold reports the theft with Marius Tangen's help within 24 hours of 10:40 and the case number goes into the record.
- The laptop was worth more than 10,000 kroner, so Tomas Berg tells the insurer within 3 working days.
- Within 2 working days of the loss Marius Tangen gives Jostein Vold a replacement laptop and his access back, and tells Nora Lind that he could not work and for how long. The specification sets that time whether or not the person is at work, and it does not say what the replacement and the access back mean for someone who is on leave for three weeks; that gap is not filled here. The replacement goes into the equipment register and the lost laptop is marked there as lost, with the date.

## Pass

The record holds an entry timed no later than 14:40 saying that what the company reads about Jostein Vold and why was sent to his work email, with all five things in it, and a later entry saying that it was said to him again when Marius Tangen first spoke to him. It shows the lock by 11:40, the sessions ended, the password changed, the tokens revoked and the device blocked by 12:40 with Nora Lind told, none of them waiting for Jostein Vold to answer his telephone. It shows the six facts by 14:40, with what Nora Lind gave in place of the facts only he could give, every fact still open written as `not established` with its reason and no blanks, and the facts he gives when he can be reached added to the record afterwards. It shows a wipe decision by 14:40 with Kari Nordmann asked beforehand, the attempt to reach him before the wipe with its time, the wipe held back no longer than an hour on his account, and a line saying what was lost is not known from him. It shows Kari Nordmann's breach decision with her name, her reason and the time by 14:40, no entry saying the police were told while it is not established that the laptop was taken, the insurer told within 3 working days, and a replacement laptop and the access given within 2 working days with Nora Lind told. The record says plainly that the specification does not say what Nora Lind gives for a person who is away rather than gone, and what the replacement and the access back mean for someone on leave.
