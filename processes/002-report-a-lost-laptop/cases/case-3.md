# Case 3: Work bag taken from a car on a Saturday night

Kind: Outside working hours

## Situation

Saturday 28 November 2026. Head of product Jonas Vikane comes back to his car outside a restaurant at 22:40 and sees that the window is broken and his work bag with his company laptop is gone. He telephones the on-call telephone number in the staff handbook at 22:50, and the report reaches the company at 22:50. The week beginning 23 November 2026 is held by Erik Moen.

## Who acts

On-call developer, Erik Moen.

## What the person knows

He knows the time of the call, that the laptop was taken and not mislaid, and where and when it went missing. He knows he may locate and lock the device in device management at once without asking anyone, and that he can revoke in Nordsky Cloud what the role can revoke but cannot end sessions or revoke tokens in the office suite and the support desk. He knows Marius Tangen is reachable on his mobile around the clock. He does not know whether the screen was locked when the bag was taken or what was stored on the laptop that exists nowhere else, and he does not decide whether the device is wiped.

## What should happen

Every time counts from 22:50, and all times run around the clock, including the weekend.

- By 23:50 Erik Moen locates and locks the device in device management, at once and without asking anyone.
- He revokes in Nordsky Cloud what the role can revoke, and writes the time of the call and what was done into the record.
- He telephones Marius Tangen at once on his mobile and tells him what was done. Where Marius Tangen cannot be reached he reaches Amina Farah for anything the role cannot do or decide.
- Marius Tangen then does the rest himself or decides what waits until working hours, and says so in the record. By 00:50 the sessions are ended, the password changed, the tokens revoked in the office suite, Nordsky Cloud and the support desk, and the device blocked, and Nora Lind is told.
- Kari Nordmann is told when the facts are started; it is a Saturday and she does not work, so Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold is told on the firm's emergency number.
- By 02:50 the six facts are established and written into the record, with `not established` and the reason where one cannot be established.
- By 02:50 the decision whether the device is wiped is taken. The device was taken rather than mislaid, which is one of the grounds, so it is wiped, and Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold is asked first because the laptop held personal data. Before the wipe Jonas Vikane is told where he can be reached and asked what exists only on the device, and that goes into the record; the wipe is not held back more than an hour because he cannot be reached.
- By 02:50 Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold decides from the six facts whether the loss is a personal data breach, and his decision, his name, his reason and the time go into the record whether the answer is yes or no.
- Within 24 hours of 22:50 Jonas Vikane reports the theft to the police with Marius Tangen's help, and the police case number goes into the record.
- Where the laptop was worth more than 10,000 kroner, Tomas Berg tells the insurer within 3 working days and the record says it was told.
- Within 2 working days of the loss Marius Tangen gives Jonas Vikane a replacement laptop and his access back, and tells Nora Lind. The replacement goes into the equipment register and the lost laptop is marked there as lost, with the date.

## Pass

The record shows the time of the call, 22:50, what Erik Moen did and when, the lock in device management no later than 23:50 and the Nordsky Cloud revocations, and that he telephoned Marius Tangen at once. It shows what Marius Tangen did himself and, for anything he decided should wait until working hours, that he said so in the record. It shows the remaining measures and the block by 00:50 with Nora Lind told, the six facts by 02:50, a wipe decision by 02:50 with Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold asked first and Jonas Vikane told and asked beforehand, Henrik Aas's breach decision with his name, his reason and the time by 02:50, a police case number within 24 hours, the insurer told within 3 working days where the laptop was worth more than 10,000 kroner, and the replacement and access within 2 working days.
