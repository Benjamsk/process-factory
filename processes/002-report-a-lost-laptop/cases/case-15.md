# Case 15: The employee must be told at three in the morning what is read about them

Kind: Outside working hours

## Situation

Saturday 28 November 2026, 23:10. Support adviser Jostein Vold telephones the on-call telephone number from his hotel in Bergen: his company laptop is not in his room, and he last had it there at about 19:00. The report reaches the company at 23:10. The week beginning 23 November 2026 is held by Erik Moen, who takes the call, locks the laptop in device management, revokes the access token and locks the account in Nordsky Cloud, writes the time of the call and what was done into the record, and telephones Marius Tangen at once on his mobile, who answers. The four-hour deadlines of the process fall at 03:10 on Sunday 29 November 2026.

## Who acts

Head of IT, Marius Tangen.

## What the person knows

He knows every deadline counts from 23:10 and runs around the clock, through the evening and the weekend. He knows what Erik Moen did and that the office suite and the support desk are left to him. He knows that within 4 hours he must tell Jostein Vold in plain language what the company reads about him and why, in the same answer in which he asks him for the facts, and that Jostein Vold is awake and reachable on his mobile. He knows Kari Nordmann works Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, so on a Saturday night Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold is reached on the firm's emergency number. He does not yet know whether the screen was locked when the laptop went missing, whether anyone else could have seen it or the password, or what was on it that exists nowhere else.

## What should happen

Every time counts from 23:10 and nothing waits for Monday.

- By 00:10 the laptop is located and locked in device management, which Erik Moen did at once at 23:10.
- By 01:10 Marius Tangen ends Jostein Vold's sessions, changes his password and revokes his access tokens in the office suite, in Nordsky Cloud and in the support desk, and blocks the device in device management, and tells Nora Lind that the accounts and access are blocked. Where he decides that something waits until working hours, he says so in the record.
- He tells the data protection officer about the lost device at the same time as he starts establishing the facts; it is a Saturday, so Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold is told on the firm's emergency number.
- By 03:10 the six facts stand in the record, each established or marked `not established` with its reason.
- By 03:10, and in the same answer in which he asks Jostein Vold for the facts, Marius Tangen tells him in plain language what the company reads about him and why: that he looks at where and when the laptop last contacted the company and at the entries of his account in the sign-in logs of the office suite and the access logs of Nordsky Cloud, for the window from 19:00 on the Saturday until the access was blocked; that this is to find out what could have been reached and nothing else; that it is not used to judge how he works; that what is found goes into the record of the case, which is kept 3 years, or 5 where the loss was also a personal data breach; and that Kari Nordmann is the data protection officer he can ask about it. The deadline does not move because it falls at three in the morning.
- By 03:10 the decision whether to wipe is taken, with the data protection officer asked first because the laptop held personal data, which on a Saturday night is Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold, and with Jostein Vold told beforehand that it will be wiped and asked what exists only on it. The record says who decided, why and when, and what was lost.
- By 03:10 Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold decides whether the loss is a personal data breach and writes the decision into the record with his name, the reason and the time, whether the answer is yes or no.
- Within 2 working days of the loss Jostein Vold has a replacement laptop and his access back, and Marius Tangen tells Nora Lind that he is working again and for how long he could not work. The replacement goes into the equipment register and the lost laptop is marked there as lost, with the date.

## Pass

The record shows 23:10 on Saturday 28 November 2026 as the time the report reached the company, written by Erik Moen with what he did, and the lock, the Nordsky Cloud revocation and the account lock at 23:10. It shows the remaining measures and the block by 01:10 with Nora Lind told, the six facts by 03:10, and above all an entry timed no later than 03:10 on the Sunday saying that Jostein Vold was told what the company reads about him and why, naming the device's last contact and the entries of his account in the sign-in logs of the office suite and the access logs of Nordsky Cloud, the window from 19:00 to the block, that it is only to find what could have been reached, that it is not used to judge how he works, that it goes into the record kept 3 years or 5, and that Kari Nordmann is the data protection officer he can ask. It shows the wipe decision by 03:10 with Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold asked first and Jostein Vold told beforehand, his breach decision with his name, his reason and the time by 03:10, and the replacement and access within 2 working days with Nora Lind told how long he could not work. No entry is timed after the deadline it belongs to on the ground that it was night or the weekend.
