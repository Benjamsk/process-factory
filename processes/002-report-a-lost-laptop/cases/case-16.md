# Case 16: Neither the data protection officer nor her stand-in answers within four hours

Kind: Person away

## Situation

Sunday 29 November 2026, 08:00. Support adviser Hanne Riise telephones the on-call telephone number: there was a break-in at the holiday cabin overnight and her company work phone is gone with her handbag. She noticed it at 07:50. The report reaches the company at 08:00. Erik Moen, who holds the week beginning 23 November 2026, locks the phone in device management, revokes the access token and locks the account in Nordsky Cloud, writes the time of the call and what was done into the record, and telephones Marius Tangen at once, who answers. The phone held her work email in the office suite and the support desk, so it held personal data. Kari Nordmann works Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday; the emergency number of Aas & Wold is not answered and Henrik Aas does not call back before 12:00. The register shows the phone was bought for 9,400 kroner.

## Who acts

Head of IT, Marius Tangen.

## What the person knows

He knows the phone was taken rather than mislaid, which on its own is a ground for a wipe, and that the decision whether to wipe is due within 4 hours of 08:00. He knows he must ask the data protection officer before a wipe where the device held personal data, that Kari Nordmann is not reachable on a Sunday and that Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold steps in on the firm's emergency number, which he calls at 09:10, 10:15 and 11:40 without an answer. He knows that where neither of them can be reached within 4 hours of the report and a ground for a wipe is met, he decides the wipe alone and does not wait. He knows Hanne Riise can be reached. He does not know when either of them will answer.

## What should happen

Every time counts from 08:00 and runs through the Sunday.

- By 09:00 the phone is located and locked in device management, which Erik Moen did at 08:00.
- By 10:00 Marius Tangen ends Hanne Riise's sessions, changes her password and revokes her access tokens in the office suite, in Nordsky Cloud and in the support desk, and blocks the device in device management, and tells Nora Lind that the accounts and access are blocked.
- He tries to reach the data protection officer when he starts establishing the facts, and writes each attempt and its time into the record.
- By 12:00 the six facts stand in the record, each established or marked `not established` with its reason, and by 12:00 Hanne Riise has been told in plain language what the company reads about her and why.
- Neither Kari Nordmann nor Henrik Aas can be reached by 12:00, which is 4 hours from the report, and a ground for a wipe is met, so Marius Tangen decides the wipe alone and does not wait. He writes into the record that he could not reach either of them, what he tried and when. Before the wipe he reaches Hanne Riise, tells her that the phone will be wiped and asks her what exists only on it, and writes her answer into the record, and the wipe is not held back for longer than an hour because someone cannot be reached. He wipes the phone and writes into the record who decided it, why and when.
- He reaches Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold at 17:00 on the Sunday, the first hour one of them is reachable, and tells him what was done to the device. Where Henrik Aas, or Kari Nordmann afterwards, writes into the record that he or she would have advised otherwise, that stands as a finding for the next version of this process and nothing that was done is undone.
- Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold then decides whether the loss is a personal data breach and writes the decision into the record with his name, the reason and the time, whether the answer is yes or no. The specification requires that decision within 4 hours of the report and does not say what becomes of that deadline where neither he nor Kari Nordmann can be reached in time; that gap is not filled here, and what it does require is that the decision stands in the record with a name, a reason and a time.
- The phone was taken, so within 24 hours of 08:00 Hanne Riise reports the theft to the police with Marius Tangen's help and the case number goes into the record. The phone was worth 9,400 kroner, which is not more than 10,000 kroner, so the insurer is not told. Within 2 working days of the loss she has a replacement device and her access back, and Nora Lind is told that she is working again and for how long she could not work.

## Pass

The record shows every attempt to reach Kari Nordmann and Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold with its time, and that none of them was answered by 12:00. It shows that Marius Tangen decided the wipe alone at or before 12:00, naming himself, the ground and the time, and that the phone was wiped without waiting for either of them; that Hanne Riise was told beforehand that it would be wiped and asked what existed only on it, with her answer and what was lost; and that he told Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold at 17:00, the first hour one of them was reachable, what had been done. Where an advice to the contrary is written into the record afterwards, it stands there as a finding and no measure is undone. The record also holds the lock at 08:00, the account measures and the block by 10:00 with Nora Lind told, the six facts and the message to the employee about what is read about her by 12:00, a breach decision with a name, a reason and a time, a police case number within 24 hours, no entry saying the insurer was told, and the replacement device and access within 2 working days.
