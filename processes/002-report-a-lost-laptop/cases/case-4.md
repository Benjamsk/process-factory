# Case 4: The 72 hours for a waiting wipe run out on a Sunday

Kind: Outside working hours

## Situation

Thursday 26 November 2026. Support adviser Sondre Lie telephones head of IT Marius Tangen at 09:20 to say his work tablet was left in a hotel room in Trondheim that morning. The report reaches the company at 09:20. Device management locates and locks the tablet in the hotel. The tablet is encrypted, its screen was locked when it went missing, the sign-in logs show it was signed in only to the office suite and never to the reading app, Nordsky Cloud or the support desk, and nothing was reached from it after it went missing. It was mislaid, not taken, and the hotel is looking for it. Marius Tangen decides the wipe can wait while the hotel searches. The 72 hours from the report run out at 09:20 on Sunday 29 November 2026, outside working hours.

## Who acts

Head of IT, Marius Tangen.

## What the person knows

He knows all six facts are established and in the record, that the tablet is located, locked and encrypted and is being looked for, and that none of the five grounds for a wipe is true. He knows he decided to wait and wrote in the record who decided and why. He knows every deadline runs around the clock, including every weekend, and that he is reachable on his mobile around the clock. On Sunday morning he knows whether the hotel has found the tablet.

## What should happen

- By 10:20 on Thursday the tablet is located and locked; by 11:20 the sessions are ended, the password changed, the tokens revoked and the device blocked, and Nora Lind is told; by 13:20 the six facts are in the record and the decision whether to wipe is taken; by 13:20 Kari Nordmann, who works on Thursdays, has decided whether the loss is a personal data breach, with her name, her reason and the time in the record whether the answer is yes or no.
- Because the tablet is located, locked, encrypted and being looked for and none of the five grounds is true, the wipe can wait, at the latest until 72 hours from the report, which is 09:20 on Sunday 29 November 2026. The record says who decided to wait and why.
- The deadline does not move because it falls on a Sunday. By 09:20 on Sunday the tablet is either wiped or no longer lost. Where it is wiped, the data protection officer is asked first because the tablet held personal data, and it is a Sunday, so Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold is asked on the firm's emergency number in Kari Nordmann's place. Before the wipe Sondre Lie is told where he can be reached and asked what exists only on the device, and that goes into the record, and the wipe is not held back more than an hour because he cannot be reached.
- Where the hotel finds the tablet, the case is closed with a line in the record saying it was found, when, where and whether anyone else could have used it in the meantime, and nothing already done is undone until Marius Tangen says the device is safe to use again.
- Within 2 working days of the loss Sondre Lie has a replacement device and his access back, and Nora Lind is told. Where the tablet was worth more than 10,000 kroner, Tomas Berg tells the insurer within 3 working days.

## Pass

The record shows the decision to wait, who took it and why, taken by 13:20 on Thursday, and it shows that by 09:20 on Sunday 29 November 2026 the tablet had either been wiped or been found. Where it was wiped, the record shows Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold asked beforehand, Sondre Lie told where he could be reached and asked what existed only on the device, and what was lost. Where it was found, the record holds the closing line with the time, the place and whether anyone else could have used it, and nothing done is undone before Marius Tangen has said the device is safe to use again. No step waits for Monday.
