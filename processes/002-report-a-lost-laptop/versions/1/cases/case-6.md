# Case 6: The loss is reported three days after it was noticed

Kind: Late or wrong notice

## Situation

Developer Lise Berntsen noticed at about 19:00 on Friday 20 November 2026 that her company laptop was not in her bag. She assumed she had left it at the office, told nobody and did not telephone. On Monday 23 November 2026 at 09:05 she finds it is not at the office either and telephones head of IT Marius Tangen. The report reaches the company at 09:05 on the Monday. She last saw the laptop at about 16:00 on the Friday.

## Who acts

Head of IT, Marius Tangen.

## What the person knows

He knows the employee noticed the loss at about 19:00 on Friday and should have telephoned him the same hour, and that the report only reached the company at 09:05 on Monday. He knows every deadline counts from the moment the loss reached the company. Device management shows the model and serial number, the encryption, the screen lock and that the device last contacted the company at 16:20 on the Friday. He knows a developer laptop is signed in to Nordsky Cloud, where the data of the reading app lies, and that the Nordsky Cloud access logs and the office suite sign-in logs will tell him whether anything was reached from the laptop after 16:00 on Friday. He does not yet know where it went missing, whether it was taken or mislaid, or whether anyone else could have seen the screen or the password.

## What should happen

The specification says nothing about what a report three days late changes. It says the clock starts when the report reaches the company, so every deadline counts from 09:05 on the Monday and none of them is already missed, and the record says who reported the loss, when she noticed it, which was about 19:00 on the Friday, and the time it reached the company, which was 09:05 on the Monday. Nothing in the specification says the delay itself is followed up in this process.

- By 10:05 the device is located and locked in device management.
- By 11:05 the sessions are ended, the password changed, the tokens revoked in the office suite, Nordsky Cloud and the support desk, and the device blocked, and Nora Lind is told.
- The data protection officer is told about the lost device when the facts are started. It is a Monday and Kari Nordmann works Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, so Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold is told.
- By 13:05 the six facts are established and written into the record, among them whether anything was reached from the laptop after 16:00 on the Friday, with `not established` and the reason where one cannot be established.
- By 13:05 Marius Tangen decides whether the device is wiped. The laptop was signed in to Nordsky Cloud, which is one of the grounds, so it is wiped, and because it held personal data he asks the data protection officer first, which on a Monday is Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold. Before the wipe Lise Berntsen is told where she can be reached and asked what exists only on the device, and that goes into the record.
- By 13:05 Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold decides whether the loss is a personal data breach, and his decision, his name, his reason and the time go into the record whether the answer is yes or no. Where it is a breach, the 72 hours of `Handle a personal data breach` count from 09:05 on the Monday, the moment the loss reached the company.
- Where the facts show the laptop was taken rather than mislaid, Lise Berntsen reports the theft to the police with Marius Tangen's help within 24 hours of 09:05 and the case number goes into the record.
- Where the laptop was worth more than 10,000 kroner, Tomas Berg tells the insurer within 3 working days. Within 2 working days of the loss Lise Berntsen has a replacement laptop and her access back, and Nora Lind is told.

## Pass

The record shows 09:05 on Monday 23 November 2026 as the time the loss reached the company and about 19:00 on the Friday as the time the employee noticed it, and every deadline measured from 09:05: the lock by 10:05, the block and the account measures by 11:05 with Nora Lind told, the six facts and the wipe decision by 13:05, and Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold's breach decision with his name, his reason and the time by 13:05. The record holds the fact of whether anything was reached from the laptop after 16:00 on the Friday, either established from the logs or marked `not established` with a reason. No deadline is counted from the Friday, and none is treated as already missed.
