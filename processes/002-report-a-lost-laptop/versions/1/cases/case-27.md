# Case 27: The on-call developer answers the report and must tell the reporter within the hour

Kind: Outside working hours

## Situation

Saturday 28 November 2026. Head of customer success Silje Haugland and support adviser Sondre Lie share a taxi home from a customer event. Sondre Lie gets out first, and as the taxi drives on Silje Haugland sees his work phone lying on the back seat. The taxi is gone before she can stop it, and she cannot telephone him because the work phone is the number she has for him. At 17:20 she telephones the on-call telephone number from the card in her wallet. Erik Moen, who holds the on-call week beginning 23 November 2026 with Lise Berntsen as his stand-in, answers and takes the report. The report reaches the company at 17:20. Kari Nordmann works Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday.

## Who acts

On-call developer, Erik Moen.

## What the person knows

He knows that he takes a report that arrives outside working hours, that he locates and locks the device, revokes an access token and locks the account in Nordsky Cloud, writes what was done and the time of the call into the record and reaches Marius Tangen on his mobile, who can be reached around the clock. He knows that where he answers the report he is the one who tells the reporter, within 1 hour of the report, that her name, how she can be reached and what she said are written in the record, why, how long the record is kept and whom she can ask, and that a reporter who is an employee is told the same. He knows the times given in hours run around the clock, including the weekend, and that nothing waits for Monday. He knows that Kari Nordmann is not reachable on a Saturday and that Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold stands in on the firm's emergency number. He knows what Silje Haugland said: where and when the phone went missing and that it was left behind rather than taken. He does not know whether anyone else could have seen the screen or the password, or what was on the phone that exists nowhere else: those are facts only Sondre Lie can give, and the lost phone is the number the company has for him. He cannot himself end the sessions, change the password or revoke the tokens in the office suite and the support desk, or block the device: the role locks and locates the device and works in Nordsky Cloud, and Marius Tangen does the rest or decides what waits until working hours.

## What should happen

Every time counts from 17:20 and runs around the clock, through the Saturday evening.

- By 18:20 Erik Moen answers Silje Haugland and tells her, in plain language, that her name, how she can be reached and what she said are written in the record of the case; that this is so that the company can show how it handled the loss and from what moment; that the record is kept 3 years from the day the case is closed, or 5 years where the loss was also a personal data breach, and is then deleted; and that Kari Nordmann is the data protection officer she can ask, with Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold when she is away. She is an employee and is told the same as a reporter from outside the company would be.
- At 17:20 the record is opened and holds who reported the loss, how she can be reached, what she said, when she noticed it and the time the report reached the company, together with the time of the call to the on-call number and what Erik Moen did.
- By 18:20 Erik Moen locates and locks the work phone in device management, at once and without asking anyone.
- He revokes the access token and locks the account in Nordsky Cloud, and telephones Marius Tangen at once on his mobile and tells him what was done and what is left.
- By 19:20 the sessions of Sondre Lie's account are ended, its password changed and its access tokens revoked in the office suite, in Nordsky Cloud and in the support desk, the device is blocked in device management, and Nora Lind is told that the accounts and access are blocked. Marius Tangen does what the on-call role cannot do, or decides what waits until working hours and says so in the record.
- The data protection officer is told about the lost device at the same time as the facts are started. It is a Saturday, so Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold is told on the firm's emergency number.
- By 21:20 the six facts stand in the record, each established or written as `not established` with its reason, among them whether anything was reached from the phone after it was left in the taxi.
- By 21:20 Sondre Lie is told what is read about him and why, in the same answer in which he is asked for the facts only he can give. The lost phone is the number the company has for him, so he cannot be reached within the 4 hours; he is still at the company, so Marius Tangen sends him the same thing at his work email within those 4 hours so that it is waiting for him, and says it again when he first speaks to him.
- By 21:20 the decision whether the phone is wiped is taken, and Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold is asked first because the phone held personal data in the office suite and the support desk. The phone was signed in to the support desk, which is a ground, so it is wiped. Marius Tangen reaches Sondre Lie, or tries to, as soon as the wipe is decided, to tell him the phone will be wiped and ask him what exists only on it; he writes the time of that first attempt into the record and wipes the phone one hour after that first attempt whether or not Sondre Lie has answered, and sooner where he answers sooner.
- By 21:20 Henrik Aas of Aas & Wold decides from the established facts whether the loss is a personal data breach, and his decision, his name, his reason and the time go into the record whether the answer is yes or no.
- The phone was left behind rather than taken, so there is no report to the police. Where the facts show it was taken, Sondre Lie reports the theft to the police with Marius Tangen's help within 24 hours of 17:20 and the case number goes into the record.
- The equipment register shows the work phone was bought for 9,600 kroner, which is not more than 10,000 kroner, so the insurer is not told.
- Within 2 working days of 17:20 Marius Tangen gives Sondre Lie a replacement device and his access back, and tells Nora Lind that he could not work and for how long. The replacement goes into the equipment register and the lost phone is marked there as lost, with the date.
