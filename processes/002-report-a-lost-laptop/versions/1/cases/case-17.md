# Case 17: The report ends up with the head of finance on a Friday night

Kind: Person away

## Situation

Friday 27 November 2026. Support adviser Thea Nygård gets off the tram at 21:30 and sees that her company laptop is no longer in her bag. She telephones the on-call telephone number from the staff handbook at 21:40 and again at 21:45, and nobody answers; Erik Moen holds the week beginning 23 November 2026. At 21:50 she telephones Marius Tangen on his mobile and gets no answer. At 21:55 she telephones Ingrid Solheim, who is abroad and cannot be reached. At 22:00 she telephones Tomas Berg, who answers and takes the report. The equipment register shows the laptop was bought for 16,500 kroner.

## Who acts

Head of finance, Tomas Berg.

## What the person knows

He knows the report comes to him because neither the on-call developer nor Marius Tangen could be reached and Ingrid Solheim, who takes it in that case, is away, and that he steps in when she is away. He knows what Thea Nygård tells him: that the laptop was in her bag on the tram at 21:30, that she does not know whether it was taken or mislaid, and the times of every call she made. He knows the laptop is worth more than 10,000 kroner, which is the loss he reports to the insurer. He knows he cannot locate, lock, block or wipe the laptop or touch her accounts himself: device management is administered by Marius Tangen, and by Amina Farah when he is away. He does not know when Marius Tangen, Amina Farah or Erik Moen will answer.

## What should happen

The specification sends the report to Tomas Berg where neither the on-call developer nor Marius Tangen can be reached and Ingrid Solheim is away, and it says what he does in this process: he reports a loss of equipment worth more than 10,000 kroner to the insurer. It does not say what the person who takes the report does next, whom he passes it to or who carries out the measures while the head of IT, the lead developer and the on-call developer are all unreached, and it does not say whether the rota's stand-in for that week, Lise Berntsen, takes the call the on-call developer did not answer. Those gaps are not filled here. Nor does it say whether an unanswered call to the on-call number is the moment the loss reached the company; what it does fix is that the process and every deadline run from the moment the loss reached the company, which here is 22:00, when Tomas Berg answered.

- Tomas Berg takes the report at 22:00 and keeps what Thea Nygård told him: that she noticed the loss at 21:30, the times of her four calls, and where and when the laptop went missing.
- Every deadline runs from 22:00 and around the clock, so the laptop is to be located and locked by 23:00, and the sessions ended, the password changed, the tokens revoked and the device blocked by 00:00, by whoever of Marius Tangen, Amina Farah or the on-call developer is reached; the deadlines keep running whether or not one of them has been reached, and nothing waits for Monday.
- Marius Tangen writes into the record who reported the loss, when she noticed it and the time the report reached the company, and what was done and when.
- Tomas Berg reports the loss to the insurer within 3 working days, because the laptop was worth more than 10,000 kroner.
- Within 2 working days of the loss Thea Nygård has a replacement laptop and her access back, and Nora Lind is told that she is working again and for how long she could not work.

## Pass

The record shows 22:00 on Friday 27 November 2026 as the time the report reached the company and Tomas Berg as the one who took it, and it shows that she noticed the loss at 21:30 and the times of the unanswered calls to the on-call number at 21:40 and 21:45, to Marius Tangen at 21:50 and to Ingrid Solheim at 21:55. It shows every deadline counted from 22:00, with the lock due by 23:00 and the account measures and the block due by 00:00, and none of them counted from a later hour on the ground that nobody with access to device management had yet been reached. The insurer is told within 3 working days and the record says so, and the replacement laptop and the access are given within 2 working days with Nora Lind told. The record says plainly that the specification does not say what the person who takes the report does next, nor whether an unanswered call to the on-call number is itself the moment the loss reached the company.
