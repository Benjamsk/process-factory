# Policies

The policies and routines the organisation already has. Write one section per policy, with the name of the policy as its heading. Say in prose what the policy covers, who owns it, when it was last approved and where it is kept.

## Information security policy

The information security policy says how employees handle passwords, devices, access rights and personal data, and that every employee must report a suspected security incident to the head of IT the same day. The policy stops there: it says that the head of IT then "assesses the incident and takes the necessary measures", and describes no steps, no times and no handover to the data protection officer. Marius Tangen owns it. Ingrid Solheim approved it on 15 January 2025, and it is kept in the staff handbook.

## Privacy policy for the reading app

The privacy policy for the reading app says what personal data the app collects about pupils and teachers, why, how long it is kept and who it is shared with. It is written for school owners and parents. Kari Nordmann owns it. Ingrid Solheim approved it on 4 September 2025, and it is published on the company website and kept in the staff handbook.

## Deletion routine for the reading app

The deletion routine says that a pupil's read-aloud recordings are deleted 12 months after they are made, that a pupil's account and reading history are deleted 90 days after the school owner ends the subscription or the pupil leaves the school, and that Amina Farah checks the deletion job every month. Amina Farah owns it. Kari Nordmann approved it on 4 September 2025, and it is kept in the staff handbook.

## AI use policy

The AI use policy says which AI systems employees may use for work, that an AI system may not be given pupil data unless a data processor agreement covers it, that every answer the AI support assistant sends on its own must come from an approved help article, and that a human must decide any case that concerns a complaint, a contract or personal data. Petter Dahl owns it. Ingrid Solheim approved it on 19 August 2025, and it is kept in the staff handbook.

## Support service levels

The support service levels say that a support case from a teacher gets a first answer within four working hours, that a case from a school owner gets one within two working hours, and that a case marked as blocking teaching is escalated to the on-call developer at once. Silje Haugland owns it. Ingrid Solheim approved it on 20 June 2025, and it is kept in the staff handbook and in the subscription contract with each school owner.

## Record of processing activities

The record of processing activities lists every processing activity the company carries out, its purpose, its legal basis, the categories of data and people, the processors and the retention time. Kari Nordmann owns it and reviews it every six months. She last reviewed it on 1 October 2026, and it is kept in the office suite in the folder of the data protection officer.

## Lost or stolen equipment

The routine for lost or stolen equipment says that every company laptop and work phone is encrypted and screen-locked through device management, that an employee who loses one tells the head of IT the same hour and not only the same day, that the device is locked as soon as the loss is known and wiped where it cannot be recovered, that a theft is reported to the police by the employee with Marius Tangen's help and the case number written into the record, and that a loss of equipment worth more than 10,000 kroner is reported to the insurer by Tomas Berg. Marius Tangen owns it. Ingrid Solheim approved it on 3 March 2026, and it is kept in the staff handbook.
