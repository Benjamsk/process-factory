# Data processor agreements

The agreements the organisation has with those who process personal data on its behalf. Write one section per agreement, with the name of the processor as its heading. Say in prose which system or service the agreement covers, what personal data the processor handles, when the agreement was signed, where it is kept and who in the organisation is responsible for it.

## The school owners

Fjordlys Læring AS is the data processor for the pupil data in the reading app. Each of the 310 school owners is the controller for its own pupils, and the company processes that data only on the school owner's instructions, set out in the data processing annex to the subscription contract. The annex says that Fjordlys Læring AS must tell the school owner about a personal data breach that affects its pupils without undue delay and at the latest 24 hours after the company became aware of it, that the message must say what happened, which data and how many pupils are affected, what the company has done and what the school owner should do, and that the school owner decides whether Datatilsynet and the parents are told about its own pupils. The company is the controller for teacher data, support desk data, employee data and applicant data. The annex is part of every subscription contract, is kept in the contract archive, and Ingrid Solheim is responsible for it with advice from Kari Nordmann.

## Nordsky AS

The company has a data processor agreement with Nordsky AS for the cloud platform the reading app runs on. Nordsky AS handles all the data of the reading app: the names, classes, schools, reading levels, reading history and read-aloud recordings of about 90,000 pupils, and the names, work email addresses and schools of about 6,000 teachers. The agreement was signed on 12 February 2024, is kept in the contract archive and has a sub-processor list that Nordsky AS must give 30 days' notice to change. The agreement obliges Nordsky AS to report a personal data breach to the head of IT of Fjordlys Læring AS without undue delay and at the latest 24 hours after Nordsky AS became aware of it, and to help with the investigation. Marius Tangen is responsible for it.

## Hjelpedesk AS

The company has a data processor agreement with Hjelpedesk AS for the support desk. Hjelpedesk AS handles the names, work email addresses, telephone numbers and schools of teachers and school contacts, and the text of every support case. The agreement obliges Hjelpedesk AS to report a personal data breach to Fjordlys Læring AS without undue delay and at the latest 48 hours after it became aware of it. The agreement was signed on 3 May 2023 and is kept in the contract archive. Silje Haugland is responsible for it.

## Anthropic

The company has a data processor agreement with Anthropic for the AI support assistant, entered through the commercial terms for the Claude API. Anthropic handles the text of the support case and the name, work email address and school of the teacher who wrote in, and does not train on that data under the agreement. The agreement obliges Anthropic to notify Fjordlys Læring AS of a personal data breach without undue delay after becoming aware of it; it sets no fixed number of hours. The agreement was signed on 19 August 2025 and is kept in the contract archive. Petter Dahl is responsible for it, with advice from Kari Nordmann.

## Lønn Norge AS

The company has a data processor agreement with Lønn Norge AS for the payroll system. Lønn Norge AS handles the names, national identity numbers, bank account numbers, salaries and sick leave of all 48 employees. The agreement obliges Lønn Norge AS to report a personal data breach to the head of finance of Fjordlys Læring AS without undue delay and at the latest 24 hours after it became aware of it. The agreement was signed on 1 March 2024 and is kept in the contract archive. Tomas Berg is responsible for it.

## Rekrutt AS

The company has a data processor agreement with Rekrutt AS for the hiring system. Rekrutt AS handles the names, email addresses, telephone numbers, CVs, references and interview notes of job applicants. The agreement obliges Rekrutt AS to report a personal data breach to Fjordlys Læring AS without undue delay; it sets no fixed number of hours. The agreement was signed on 7 November 2024 and is kept in the contract archive. Nora Lind is responsible for it.

## Microsoft

The company has a data processor agreement with Microsoft for the office suite, through the Microsoft Products and Services Data Protection Addendum. Microsoft handles the email and documents of all 48 employees, which include documents about pupils, teachers and job applicants. The addendum covers device management in the same subscription, where Microsoft handles the model and serial number of each employee's laptop and work phone, the employee it is given to, when and from where the device last contacted the company, and whether its disk is encrypted and its screen locked. The addendum obliges Microsoft to notify Fjordlys Læring AS of a personal data breach without undue delay after becoming aware of it, through the tenant administrator, who is Marius Tangen; it sets no fixed number of hours. The addendum applies from the start of the subscription on 1 August 2022 and is kept in the contract archive. Marius Tangen is responsible for it.

## Aas & Wold

The company has a data processor agreement with the law firm Aas & Wold for the data protection officer stand-in service: lawyer Henrik Aas steps in for Kari Nordmann when she is away, on the days she does not work and at weekends. Under the agreement Henrik Aas handles whatever personal data the case he is standing in on holds, which can be the data of pupils, teachers, school contacts, employees and job applicants, and he may receive the record of a personal data breach and its fact page, advise on it, decide whether a case is a breach, and write and send the notification to Datatilsynet on behalf of Fjordlys Læring AS. The agreement obliges Aas & Wold to keep the data confidential, to use no sub-processor without the written consent of Fjordlys Læring AS, to report a personal data breach of its own to the company without undue delay and at the latest 24 hours after it became aware of it, and to delete or return the case data when the stand-in on that case ends. The agreement was signed on 1 October 2024, is kept in the contract archive, and Ingrid Solheim is responsible for it with advice from Kari Nordmann.
